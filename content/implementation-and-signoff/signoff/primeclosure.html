Get Started

Synopsys PrimeClosure™, enabled with Synopsys Multiphysics Fusion™ capabilities, delivers unified, signoff-accurate optimization that integrates timing, power integrity, thermal, and process effects directly into the design closure loop. By combining Synopsys PrimeTime®, RedHawk-SC™, and PrimeShield™ technologies, PrimeClosure enables engineering teams to identify and resolve the physical effects that increasingly limit timing, performance, and reliability at advanced nodes. Its high-capacity, AI-driven, single-box architecture scales efficiently to advanced-node, billion-instance designs, providing a unified path to multiphysics-aware design closure with optimized convergence, faster time-to-results, and predictable signoff success.

Leveraging signoff-accurate voltage- and thermal-aware insights, PrimeClosure applies surgical, timing-safe fixes that proactively address IR drop and thermal challenges earlier in the flow. This eliminates the inefficiencies of traditional analysis-ECO-place-and-route iterations, enabling faster convergence, higher ECO success rates, and improved power, performance, and area (PPA).

Built on a golden PrimeTime optimization engine, Synopsys PrimeClosure removes uncertainty from last-mile closure by delivering:

  • Signoff fidelity by construction
  • Deterministic convergence
  • Higher PPA recovery with fewer design iterations
  • Faster closure through multiphysics-aware optimization
  • Reduced late-stage ECO risk and improved predictability
