A Complete Power Device Solution

Synopsys Power Device WorkBench is a powerful solution designed for optimizing power transistors and electronic devices. Synopsys Power Device WorkBench improves the efficiency and reliability of these devices by meticulously analyzing and simulating the resistance and current flow within complex metal interconnects. With its high-throughput simulation engine, engineers can efficiently optimize design parameters, such as metal layouts and bond wire configurations, leading to better designs that reach the market faster. It provides insights into sensitivity, current density, electromigration, and hot spots, empowering process development and device design groups to enhance power arrays without the need for costly manufacturing iterations.
