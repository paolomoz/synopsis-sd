Award Recognition

Synopsys Receives Top Accolade from Frost & Sullivan

  • Awarded the prestigious 2025 Global Technology Innovation Leadership award for advancing Analog In-Memory Computing.
  • Honored for its cloud-native EDA platform, offering on-demand licensing, burst compute, and scalable tool access with minute-based billing—empowering companies to tape out rapidly and cost-effectively.
  • Positioned as a visionary, customer-centric leader in AI-enabled analog chip design and verification.

Synopsys AMS Leadership at a Glance

  • Custom Design Family: Unified schematic, layout, simulation & reliability across analog, RF, photonics, and multi-die
  • PrimeSim & PrimeWave: Industry-leading SPICE, FastSPICE, AMS simulation tools with AI-tuned flows across advanced nodes (N4P, N3E, N2) in partnership with major foundries
  • Mixed Signal Verification: High-performance mixed-signal simulation with transistor-level accuracy and FastSPICE speed; tight VCS digital integration
  • AI-Driven Migration Flow: Synopsys ASO.ai enables rapid analog migration across FinFET nodes with TSMC reference flows—cutting time-to-market

How Customers Benefit

  • Seamless Integration for End-to-End Design: The combination of PrimeSim simulation tools and Custom Design solutions provides a unified platform for analog and mixed-signal chip design, enabling efficient collaboration between design, simulation, and verification teams.
  • Unmatched Accuracy and Scalability: PrimeSim delivers industry-leading simulation accuracy for complex analog, RF, and mixed-signal designs, while Custom Design tools offer scalable and intuitive design environments, ensuring high performance and reliability for even the most demanding applications.
  • Productivity Boost with Advanced Automation: Synopsys AMS solutions leverage AI-driven automation, advanced layout tools, and comprehensive design rule checks, significantly reducing manual effort and accelerating the design cycle from concept to tape-out.
  • Foundry-Validated Flows: Certified by TSMC’s AMS reference flows and integrated with major foundry PDKs.
  • End-to-End Ecosystem: From schematic to silicon, covering design, simulation, IP, migration, and verification.
