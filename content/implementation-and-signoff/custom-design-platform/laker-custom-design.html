Laker Custom Design Tools

Synopsys Laker® custom design tools have a strong heritage of providing new innovations in custom layout productivity. Laker continues to be updated and supported.

Our latest tool for custom design, Custom Compiler™ design environment, goes well beyond the capabilities of Laker. Custom Compiler provides a complete visually-assisted automation flow. Laker users wishing to learn more about how they can gain access to Custom Compiler should contact Synopsys.
