overview

Synopsys PrimeSim™ circuit simulation solution provides a unified workflow of next-generation simulation technologies to accelerate the design and signoff of hyper-converged designs. The Synopsys PrimeWave™ design enviroment, a newly architected design verification environment, is integrated with the PrimeSim solution to deliver a seamless simulation experience around all PrimeSim simulation engines.
