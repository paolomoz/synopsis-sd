Digital Instance Technical Paper

The Synopsys Cloud Digital Instance offers pre-built and expertly tuned flows for two of the most popular digital design roles:

1. A synthesis flow that can enable RTL validation, exploration, and design handoff for RTL-focused architecture designers

2. A complete RTL-to-signoff flow is also available and targeted for full-flow digital designers.

The pre-built flows remain fully customizable and users can configure the Digital Instance to execute any subset of the flow for targeted design iteration or exploration; for example, signoff-driven engineering change order (ECO) and closure.

Download the technical paper to learn more about:

  • Pre-configured ready-to-use flows
  • Simplified and automated execution
  • Managed and flow-optimized compute
