Transforming Chip Design on Cloud

At SNUG Silicon Valley 2022, Aart de Geus, Synopsys Chairman and co-Chief Executive Officer, sat down with Satya Nadella, Executive Chairman and CEO of Microsoft, to discuss how Synopsys and Microsoft are partnering to transform the chip-design landscape with a software-as-a-service (SaaS) solution built on Microsoft Azure to offer customers ultimate flexibility on cloud.
