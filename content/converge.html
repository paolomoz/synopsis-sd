What is Synopsys Converge?

Synopsys Converge brings together leaders, engineers, and simulation experts to explore the future of system and silicon design. For the first time, the Executive Forum, Synopsys User Group (SNUG), and Simulation World are combined in one event, offering leadership insights, technical sessions, and the latest in simulation technology. It’s a unique opportunity to connect with peers, learn new strategies, and help shape the next era of innovation.

How do I register for Synopsys Converge and who is eligible?

To register for Synopsys Converge, visit the event’s official registration page and follow the instructions for your program of interest. Eligibility varies by program: SNUG (Synopsys User Group) is open to all current Synopsys users with active licenses; Simulation World is open to customers, prospects, and members of academia, although access to some sessions may be limited; and the Executive Forum is by invitation only. All registrations are subject to review and approval. For further details or assistance with registration, please refer to the event website or contact the event support team.

How will the content be delivered?

Synopsys Converge is happening in person at the Santa Clara Convention Center. All sessions will be hosted inside the convention center conference rooms. The location for each session will be posted in the Agenda details before the event

Do I need to live in the USA to be eligible to register?

No, however, please note that Synopsys will not cover any travel costs.

Can I invite my colleagues and friends to the event?

Absolutely. Anyone eligible is welcome to attend. Help us spread the word to other Synopsys users. You can simply share the link to the registration page, and they can register at their convenience.

Is there a registration fee?

No, Synopsys Converge is a free event for Synopsys users.

Do I need to pre-register to attend?

Yes, pre-registration is highly recommended to secure your space.

Will content be available on-demand?

SNUG presentations will be available in the post-event proceedings page here. Some presentations may have limited distribution rights and may not be published.

How do I check in onsite the day of the event?

For a smooth check-in experience, you can either scan the QR code sent to you via email or enter your name at one of the available check-in kiosks. Once you’ve checked in, you will receive your name badge at the registration desk. Please remember to bring your ID card for identity verification.

Is there a hotel room block for Synopsys Converge?

Yes, there is a hotel room block available at the Hyatt for Synopsys Converge attendees. Rooms must be booked by February 17. To reserve your room, please visit this booking link.

Is parking free at Synopsys Converge?

Yes, parking in the Santa Clara Convention Center parking garage is free for attendees
