True Random Number Generators for Truly Secure Systems

This white paper explains the role of True Random Number Generators (TRNGs) in cryptographic security for applications including consumer devices, IoT, and mission-critical systems. It covers methods for generating random numbers, issues with traditional approaches, and current security standards.

Why Read This Whitepaper?

  • Importance of Randomness: Why are random numbers vital for encryption?
  • Identify Vulnerabilities: What are typical flaws in random number generation?
  • Standards Compliance: TRNGs must meet NIST and FIPS requirements.
  • Risk Mitigation: Learn about attack methods and defenses for TRNGs.
  • Solutions: Synopsys offers TRNG IP that meets international standards.
