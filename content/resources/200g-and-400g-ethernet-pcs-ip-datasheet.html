Synopsys 200G and 400G Ethernet PCS IP Datasheet

The Synopsys 400G/200G PCS IP datasheet details a multi-port, multi-rate PCS with advanced FEC, MLD, and BER monitoring—optimized for 10G–400G, flexible SerDes, and seamless MAC/PHY system design.

What You Will Learn:

  • Multi-channel configuration: single/quad/octal PCS, up to 8x50G/16x26.56G SerDes, and 512-bit GMII/CDMII interface
  • Encoding/decoding: 64b/66b, 257b transcoding, scrambling, deskew, and AM lock
  • FEC: RS-FEC, firecode (Clause 74), low-latency options, and error indication for 10G–400G operation
