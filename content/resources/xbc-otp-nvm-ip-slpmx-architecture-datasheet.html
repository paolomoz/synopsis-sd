Synopsys XBC OTP NVM IP SLPMX Architecture Datasheet

Access the Synopsys XBC OTP NVM IP Datasheet and discover how SLPMX anti‑fuse technology delivers secure, low‑power, one‑time‑programmable memory—with no extra masks or process steps—for applications such as trim and calibration, secure boot, encryption‑key storage, firmware, chip ID, and eMTP.

What You Will Learn:

  • How the SLPMX anti‑fuse design provides intrinsic security and replaces mask ROM, eFuses, and Flash.
  • How the XBC OTP IP integrates easily as a hard macro with optional power supply and factory/field programming—requiring no added masks or process steps.
  • How flexible densities and read modes optimize area, speed, and power on standard 130nm BCD technology.
