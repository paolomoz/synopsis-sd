Synopsys IP for HDMI 2.0 and 1.4 RX IP Solutions Datasheet

Access the Synopsys HDMI RX IP datasheet and learn how a certified, configurable IP suite enables 4K/60, multi-port, deep color, and HDCP 2.3—optimized for rapid integration in DTVs, set-tops, projectors, and receivers.

What You Will Learn:

  • Integration: HDMI 2.0/1.4 controller + PHY, up to 18 Gbps, flexible port count, and full video/audio format support

  • Power/area: timing-hardened, clock-gated, and optimized for small footprint and long cable support
