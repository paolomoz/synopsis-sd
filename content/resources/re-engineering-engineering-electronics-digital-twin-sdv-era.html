Re-engineering Engineering with Electronics Digital Twins for the SDV Era

Unlock faster, smarter development with electronics digital twins


As vehicles become software-defined, traditional development approaches can’t keep pace with growing complexity, shorter innovation cycles, and rising validation demands.

By leveraging the Synopsys eDT Platform for Automotive, companies can accelerate software bring-up, enhance collaboration, reduce prototyping costs, and ensure higher safety and reliability across the vehicle lifecycle.


What you will learn:

  • How eDTs accelerate SDV development by virtualizing complete electronic architectures including chips, ECUs, sensors, and embedded software.
  • How the Synopsys eDT Platform for Automotive enables OEMs, Tier 1 suppliers, and semiconductor partners to collaborate in cloud-based environments, reduce reliance on physical prototypes, and shorten development cycles while improving quality and safety.
  • How eDT Platform can benefit scenarios such as ADAS feature co-validation and early SoC evaluation, enabling development to start up to 12 months before silicon availability.
