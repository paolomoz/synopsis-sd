Synopsys 25G Ethernet MAC IP Datasheet

Download the Synopsys 25G Ethernet MAC IP datasheet and see how a configurable, silicon-proven MAC delivers 1/2.5/5/10/25G support, TSN/AVB, Hyper DMA (up to 256 channels), and seamless MACsec integration—optimized for area, power, and ASIL D certification.

What You Will Learn:

  • How to configure AXI, MTL, or MAC-only interfaces, and select up to 256 virtualized DMA channels
  • TSN/AVB support: IEEE 802.1AS-2021, 802.1Qbv/bu/ci/cr, 802.3br, frame preemption, and traffic shaping
  • Automotive safety: ISO 26262 ASIL D/B compliant, FMEDA/DFMEA documentation, and advanced power management
  • Advanced packet filtering/classification, double VLAN, multicast duplication, and full-duplex 1–25G operation
