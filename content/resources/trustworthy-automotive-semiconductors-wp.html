Requirements and Best Practices for Trustworthy Automotive Semiconductors

The automotive industry is rapidly evolving with Autonomous Driving, Connectivity, Electrification, and Shared Mobility (ACES). This transformation brings increased complexity in electronic systems, demanding sophisticated System-on-Chip (SoC) designs to meet the growing needs for continuous updates, improved user experience, and automated driving capabilities. As market growth accelerates and semiconductor costs rise, staying informed on the latest requirements and best practices for trustworthy automotive semiconductors is essential for maintaining a competitive edge and ensuring product success.

Key Takeaways:

  • Complexity in Electronic Systems: Growing demands for continuous updates and improved user experience are driving the need for advanced SoC designs in automotive semiconductors.
  • Market Growth: The ACES transformation is significantly increasing the automotive semiconductor market revenue and the cost of semiconductors in vehicles.
  • Advanced Process Nodes: High-performance computing and connectivity in vehicles are pushing the adoption of more advanced semiconductor process nodes.
  • Trustworthiness and Dependability: Ensuring quality, functional safety, reliability, and security in automotive semiconductors is crucial to meet industry standards.
  • Role of Software and EDA Solutions: Advanced software and EDA solutions are essential for optimizing design, verification, and ensuring the dependability of automotive semiconductors.
