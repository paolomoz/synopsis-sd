Synopsys XAUI PHY IP Datasheet

The Synopsys XAUI PHY IP datasheet details a low-power, mixed-signal solution—supporting flexible clocking, per-channel diagnostics, and seamless integration with Synopsys XGXS and XGMII for networking, backplane, and optical designs.

What You Will Learn:

  • High-speed features: 3.125Gbps Tx/Rx, 8b/10b frame alignment, and power-down mode for energy savings
  • Flexible integration: 10/20-bit parallel ASIC interfaces, asynchronous operation, and XGXS compatibility
  • ATE/production: JTAG/IEEE 1149.6, pass/fail vectors, and analog test support on low-cost testers
