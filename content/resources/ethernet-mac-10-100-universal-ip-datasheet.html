Synopsys Ethernet MAC 10/100 Universal IP Datasheet

Access the Synopsys Ethernet MAC 10/100 Universal datasheet and learn how to deploy a highly configurable, standards-compliant MAC—optimized for low gate count, low power, and rapid system integration.

  • How to configure for MAC-only, MAC+MTL, MAC+DMA, or MAC+AHB architectures to fit any SoC topology
