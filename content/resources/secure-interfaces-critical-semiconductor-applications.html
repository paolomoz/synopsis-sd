Secure Interfaces for Critical Semiconductor Applications

This white paper explains the need for secure interface IP blocks in semiconductor applications in today's connected world. It covers security issues in interface design, relevant standards, and available solutions.

What is Discussed:

  • Why security is increasingly important in semiconductor design.
  • What are the industry standards and compliance requirements for secure interfaces, like PCIe, CXL, and Ethernet?
  • What are the security challenges for automotive, HPC, AI, and IoT applications?
  • How to address and mitigate these challenges.
  • How Synopsys secure interface IP solutions meet industry requirements and simplify design process.
