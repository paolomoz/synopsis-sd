Calibrate and Configure your Power Management IC with NVM IP White Paper

Choosing the right NVM technology is critical for PMIC success. The “Calibrate and Configure Your Power Management IC with NVM IP” white paper examines key considerations for selecting embedded NVM, including availability, cost, security, endurance, and automotive reliability. It highlights why modern PMICs increasingly rely on antifuse OTP and MTP NVM rather than traditional electrical fuses.

What You Will Learn:

  • How antifuse OTP provides strong security against reverse engineering
  • How MTP NVM supports firmware updates when required
  • How NVM reliability is validated for automotive temperatures
