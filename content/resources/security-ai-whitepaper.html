Securing AI at the Silicon Level: Synopsys Solutions for a Smarter, Safer Future

This white paper explains how Synopsys Security IP embeds hardware‑rooted protection into AI SoCs and chiplets to secure their data and models. It highlights growing AI attack vectors across edge and data‑center environments and shows how technologies like PUF, tRoot HSM, interface security, and PQC create long‑term, silicon‑level trust.

Why read this whitepaper:

  • Learn how silicon‑level security protects AI data, models, and accelerators across edge and cloud
  • Understand emerging AI security regulations and how hardware‑rooted designs support compliance
  • See how PUF, tRoot HSM, and interface security establish end‑to‑end platform trust
  • Discover how PQC and other quantum‑safe solutions futureproof long‑lifecycle AI systems
