Synopsys USB LPM Digital Controller and PHY IP Datasheet

Download the Synopsys USB LPM & HSIC IP datasheet and see how a silicon-proven solution delivers three orders of magnitude faster suspend/resume, up to 50% power/area savings, and seamless USB connectivity over just two wires.

What You Will Learn:

  • How LPM enables microsecond suspend/resume and maximizes battery life with 95% leakage reduction
  • HSIC features: cable-free, two-wire USB 2.0 at 480Mbps, integrated PHY (0.18mm²), and 1.2V LVCMOS
  • Power/performance: TX ~27mW, sleep ~4uW, low pin count, and GDSII delivery for easy SoC integration
  • Controller: AHB interface, configurable endpoints/buffering, transfer/transaction-based processing, firmware flexibility
