Synopsys 800G Ethernet PCS IP Datasheet

The 800G Ethernet Physical Coding Sublayer (PCS) is a critical component for enabling ultra-high-speed networking in modern data centers and high-performance computing systems. By aggregating multiple 400G channels and incorporating advanced error correction and interoperability features, the PCS enables reliable, scalable data transmission at 800G speeds. This datasheet will discuss the architecture, features, and capabilities of Synopsys 800G Ethernet PCS IP and how it supports next-generation high-bandwidth networking applications.

What You Will Learn:

  • The role of the PCS in enabling 800G Ethernet systems through channel bonding and high-speed data interfaces
  • How features such as RS-FEC and multi-lane SerDes improve data integrity and reliability at high speeds
  • The scalability and flexibility of the PCS architecture for multi-rate, multi-channel networking applications
