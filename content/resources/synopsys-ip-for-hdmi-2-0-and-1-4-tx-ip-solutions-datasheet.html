Synopsys IP for HDMI 2.0 and 1.4 TX IP Solutions Datasheet

Download the Synopsys HDMI TX IP datasheet and see how a silicon-proven, configurable controller and PHY solution delivers HDMI 2.0/1.4 and HDCP 2.3 certification, deep-color, and 18 Gbps bandwidth—optimized for low power, small area, and long cable runs.

What You Will Learn:

  • How to implement 4Kx2K at 60Hz, YCbCr 4:2:0, and deep-color modes (30/36/48-bit)
  • PHY features: up to 18 Gbps, four levels of pre-emphasis, 5V I/O, and aggressive ESD protection
  • Controller features: TMDS scrambling, HDR, CEC 2.0, 3D formats, and full CEA-861F video/audio format support
