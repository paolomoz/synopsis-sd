Synopsys CCIX IP Complete Solution Datasheet

The Synopsys CCIX IP datasheet details a configurable controller, low-power 32G PHY, and comprehensive VIP—supporting 32GT/s, full RAS/debug, AMBA/PCIe/PIPE, and robust error monitoring for first-pass silicon success.

What You Will Learn:

  • Controller: CCIX 1.1/PCIe 5.0/4.0/3.1 support, up to 16 lanes, 6 BARs, in/out-of-band config space, and multi-client support
  • PHY: full-duplex transceivers, aggregation/bifurcation, PRBS/BERT, internal/external clocks, and on-chip margin analysis
  • RAS features: error injection, statistics monitoring, retry buffer, and flow-control credit management
