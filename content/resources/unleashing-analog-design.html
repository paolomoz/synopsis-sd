Unleashing Analog Design Innovation with Synopsys AI Powered AMS Solution

As AI, automotive, and connectivity technologies drive rapid growth in analog and mixed-signal chip design, engineering teams face mounting challenges with complexity, slow simulation times, and manual workflows. Learn how Synopsys is transforming analog design with its AI-powered, cloud-enabled AMS platform—helping customers overcome traditional bottlenecks and accelerate innovation.​

Key Takeaways​:

  • Faster Project Completion and Node Migration: Synopsys automates complex analog design tasks with AI, enabling teams to finish projects up to 10X faster and with less manual effort.​
  • Rapid SPICE Verification: Multi-Core Scalability, Optimized Solvers and GPU-accelerated simulation tools drastically reduce verification times, allowing more design iterations and faster signoff.​
  • Superior Accuracy: Advanced technologies such as Dynamic Parameter Control allows improved performance with greater accuracy for sensitive phases of simulation.​
  • Flexible Cloud Deployment: Synopsys Cloud provides scalable, pay-per-use access to design tools, supporting distributed teams and eliminating licensing delays.​
  • Learn about Synopsys Exclusive Technologies:
    • Real-Time View Swapping (RTVS)​
    • PrimeSim Hybrid Timing​
    • Synopsys NanoTime - Transistor level static timing​
    • Synopsys ESP - Equivalence Checking
