Synopsys Medium Density NVM IP Datasheet

Access the Synopsys Medium Density NVM IP Datasheet and discover how reprogrammable, on‑chip non‑volatile memory—built in standard CMOS/BCD with no extra masks—replaces external EEPROM/Flash for smart‑meter, PMIC, touchscreen, and mixed‑signal applications.

What You Will Learn:

  • How the Medium Density NVM IP integrates as a hard macro with built‑in high‑voltage generation and test modes—eliminating external EEPROM/Flash.
  • How flexible densities, ECC, and fast 32‑bit access improve reliability and performance in mixed‑signal designs.
  • How automotive‑grade qualification and mature BCD support enable long‑life use with 10‑year retention and 1,000‑cycle endurance.
