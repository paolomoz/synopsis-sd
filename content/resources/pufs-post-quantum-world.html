Synopsys PUFs in a Post-Quantum World

With the looming threat of quantum computing on the horizon, the security landscape is changing. This white paper provides an in-depth analysis of quantum computing's impact on security and explains how Synopsys PUF products can help you maintain robust security in the quantum era.

Key Takeaways:

  • Overview of how quantum computers work and their impact on security
  • Quantum computing will break existing cryptographic algorithms
  • Guidance on transitioning to post-quantum cryptography
  • Information to help plan future security measures
  • Synopsys PUF products remain secure against quantum attacks
