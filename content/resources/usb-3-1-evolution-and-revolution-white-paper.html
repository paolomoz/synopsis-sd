USB 3.1: Evolution and Revolution White Paper

USB 3.1 extends the USB architecture with higher signaling rates and improved encoding efficiency. These enhancements enable increased data throughput across system components and applications. This white paper defines the technologies that enable next-generation USB performance.

What You Will Learn:

  • Implement 10 Gbps signaling with enhanced SuperSpeedPlus architecture
  • Increase efficiency using 128b/132b encoding with ~3% overhead
  • Design systems targeting ~1 GB/s optimized throughput performance
