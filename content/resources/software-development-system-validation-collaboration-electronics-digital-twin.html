Software Development, System Validation, and Collaboration with Electronics Digital Twin

This white paper explores how the Synopsys Electronics Digital Twin (eDT) Platform for Automotive enables a virtual-first development approach, giving OEMs, Tier 1 suppliers, and semiconductor partners the ability to design, integrate, and validate software long before hardware is available.

By leveraging virtual ECUs (vECUs), scalable cloud-based workflows, and interoperable digital twins, organizations can dramatically shorten development cycles, improve software quality, and shift validation left, while fostering a collaborative ecosystem across the entire supply chain.

​

What you will learn:

  • How vECUs and eDTs enable early, hardware-independent software development, shifting validation left and accelerating delivery of complex automotive systems.
  • How the Synopsys eDT Platform provides an open, cloud-native environment that integrates third-party tools, supports multivendor collaboration, and automates largescale CI/CD/CT workflows.
  • How OEMs and suppliers can scale software quality and system integration by using multi-abstraction models, synchronized simulations, and automated test campaigns across the full E/E architecture.
