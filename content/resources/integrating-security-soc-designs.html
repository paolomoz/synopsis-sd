Integrating Security into SoC Designs

As hardware security continues to play an increasingly pivotal role in safeguarding complex, interconnected systems, a multi-layered and methodical approach to validation and protection is essential. The evolving threat landscape underscores the necessity for foundational mechanisms such as Root of Trust (RoT), robust verification methodologies, and advanced emulation platforms.

This whitepaper examines the evolving landscape of hardware security, focusing on key methodologies and validation techniques for protecting modern System-on-Chip (SoC) designs, including hardware-assisted emulation. It explores the role of hardware RoT as a foundation for secure systems, identifies vulnerabilities at critical design interfaces, and underscores the importance of layered security strategies.

Synopsys tRoot™ Hardware Secure Modules (HSM) and ZeBu® together provide a holistic solution for hardware security challenges, enabling organizations to build resilient, scalable, and high-performance systems. By leveraging these advanced technologies, teams can proactively address vulnerabilities, ensure system integrity, and accelerate time-to-market with greater confidence in the security and reliability of their products.
