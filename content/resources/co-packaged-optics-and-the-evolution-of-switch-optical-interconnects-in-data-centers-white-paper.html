Co-Packaged Optics and the Evolution of Switch/Optical Interconnects in Data Centers White Paper

Co-packaged optics (CPO) is an emerging solution for improving bandwidth, power efficiency, and performance in data center networks by integrating optical and electrical components within the same package as the switch ASIC. This white paper will discuss the evolution of interconnect technologies, their limitations, and how CPO addresses these challenges.

What You Will Learn:

  • The evolution of data center interconnects and the limitations of copper and pluggable optical solutions
  • The principles and benefits of co-packaged optics, including reduced power consumption, latency, and improved bandwidth density
  • The design challenges and considerations associated with implementing CPO in next-generation data center systems
