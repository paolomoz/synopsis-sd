Synopsys RedHawk-SC

Synopsys RedHawk-SC™ is the industry’s trusted gold-standard solution for voltage drop and reliability signoff of digital designs, certified across all advanced foundry nodes. It delivers comprehensive, high-fidelity analytics to identify power integrity and reliability risks, enabling rapid resolution and first-time silicon success.

At the core of its innovation is SigmaAV™, a next-generation voltage analysis technology that provides high coverage and transforms traditional voltage drop analysis into a root-cause driven, application-aware solution. RedHawk-SC also incorporates advanced capabilities including reduced-order modeling for efficient full-chip and multi-die analysis, enabling faster turnaround and improved design convergence. Incremental what-if analysis and root cause analytics, tightly integrated with physical design, ECO and static timing analysis flows, enable early prevention of voltage drop issues, accelerate design closure, and maximize signoff productivity.
