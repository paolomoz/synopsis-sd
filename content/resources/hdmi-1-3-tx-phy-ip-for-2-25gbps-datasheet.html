Synopsys HDMI 1.3 TX PHY IP for 2.25Gbps Datasheet

The Synopsys HDMI 1.3a TX PHY IP datasheet describes a silicon‑proven transmitter physical‑layer solution that enables high‑performance HDMI and DVI output connectivity in consumer and multimedia SoC designs. Compliant with HDMI 1.3a and DVI 1.0 specifications, this integrated PHY supports high‑resolution video formats with True Color and Deep Color modes while delivering low power consumption, small area, and reliable high‑speed signal transmission for next‑generation video and audio applications.

What You Will Learn:

  • How an HDMI 1.3a–compliant transmitter PHY enables high‑resolution video output up to 1080p and QXGA display formats
  • How support for True Color and Deep Color modes allows higher color depth and improved visual fidelity
  • How integrated TMDS encoding and high‑speed serialization simplify HDMI transmitter implementation
