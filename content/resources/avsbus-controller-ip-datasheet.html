Synopsys AVSBus Controller IP Datasheet

Adaptive Voltage Scaling (AVS) is becoming increasingly important in modern high-performance and AI systems to optimize power efficiency and thermal management. The AVSBus protocol enables fast, fine-grained communication between SoCs and power management devices, allowing dynamic voltage adjustments based on system demands. This datasheet will discuss the architecture, features, and capabilities of Synopsys AVSBus Controller IP and how it enables efficient, real-time power management in advanced systems.

What You Will Learn:

  • The role of AVSBus in enabling adaptive voltage scaling for power and thermal optimization
  • How the AVSBus controller IP facilitates communication between SoCs and power management devices
  • The key architectural features, including APB interface support, error management, and efficient low-power operation
