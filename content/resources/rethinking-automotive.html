Rethinking Automotive Development: Virtualization for the Software-Defined Era

Automotive Executive Guide

The automotive industry is undergoing its most significant transformation in over a century. As software becomes the primary driver of vehicle value, traditional development models are no longer enough to stay competitive.

This executive guide shows how virtualization is helping automotive leaders adapt faster and stay ahead.

What you’ll learn

  • Accelerate development with continuous integration and early validation
  • Reduce costs and avoid delays by minimizing hardware dependency
  • Overcome legacy challenges and respond to CASE disruption
  • Improve quality and streamline R&D and integration processes

Why it matters

Rising software complexity, increasing R&D costs, and costly SOP delays are reshaping the industry. Competitive advantage now depends on development speed and efficiency, not just production cost.
