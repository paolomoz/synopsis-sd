Synopsys USB 2.0 picoPHY IP Datasheet

The Synopsys USB 2.0 picoPHY IP datasheet details a small-area, low-power PHY—supporting 480 Mbps Hi-Speed, advanced power management, OTG 2.0, Battery Charger v1.1, and USB Type-C, with minimal external components.

What You Will Learn:

  • Architecture: mixed-signal CMOS, UTMI+ Level 3, on-chip PLL, single ground, and low-area ESD/CUP pads
  • Power: supply gating/scaling, suspend/resume, ultra-low standby, and minimal power for bus/self-powered designs
  • Connectivity: Hi-Speed, Full-/Low-Speed, Type-C/traditional connectors, and off-chip charge pump regulator support
