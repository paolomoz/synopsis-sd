Flexible Key Provisioning with SRAM PUF

With billions of connected devices, robust IoT security is essential. This white paper explains how SRAM Physical Unclonable Functions (PUFs) improve IoT security. It covers security challenges with connected devices and offers a flexible key provisioning method that addresses the limits of traditional key injection.

Key Takeaways:

  • SRAM PUF technology provides key provisioning for IoT devices
  • Unique cryptographic keys are generated from SRAM properties
  • Security can be added at any stage in the supply chain
  • Supports a range of devices, from high-end chips to low-end sensors
  • Reduces costs and simplify supply chain processes
