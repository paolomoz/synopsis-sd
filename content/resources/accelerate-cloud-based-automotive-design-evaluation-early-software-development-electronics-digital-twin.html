Accelerate Cloud-based Automotive Design Evaluation and Early Software Development with Electronics Digital Twin Platform

As automotive electronics grow more complex and development cycles shrink, engineering teams need earlier access to accurate MCU/SoC prototypes.

This whitepaper highlights how the Synopsys Electronics Digital Twin (eDT) Platform and cloud-hosted virtual prototypes enable design evaluation, early software development, and seamless collaboration—long before physical hardware is available.


What You’ll Learn:

  • Evaluate designs before silicon: How eDT Labs provide preintegrated, browserbased virtual prototypes that eliminate local setup and enable immediate MCU/SoC evaluation.
  • Accelerate early development: How cloudbased virtual prototypes let teams compile, debug, and validate software months before silicon arrives.
  • Collaborate at scale: How OEMs, Tier 1s, and semiconductor vendors use the eDT Platform to streamline access, share workflows, and validate continuously.
