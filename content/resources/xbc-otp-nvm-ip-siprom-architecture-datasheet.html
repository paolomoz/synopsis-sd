Synopsys XBC OTP NVM IP SiPROM Architecture Datasheet

Access the Synopsys XBC OTP NVM IP datasheet and discover SiPROM’s patented antiFuse technology delivers secure, fast, low‑power one‑time‑programmable memory—manufactured without any extra masks or process steps—for a wide range of SoC applications.

What You Will Learn:

  • How the SiPROM anti‑fuse architecture provides intrinsic security while offering a fast, low‑power alternative to mask ROM, eFuses, and Flash.
  • How the XBC OTP NVM integrates easily as a single hard macro with embedded charge pump, dual‑supply support, and factory/field programming—requiring no additional masks or process steps.
  • How flexible memory sizes (16Kbit–512Kbit) and multiple read modes allow designers to optimize PPA on standard CMOS technologies from 130nm to 55nm.
