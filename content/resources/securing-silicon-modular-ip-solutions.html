Securing Silicon From the Start – Modular IP Solutions for Long-Term Resilience

Security isn’t a feature; it’s the foundation for any device that stores data, connects, or makes decisions. This eBook explores how Synopsys is helping teams build more secure, future-ready products from the ground up — with modular IP, expert guidance, and end-to-end solutions proven across billions of SoCs.

Key takeaways:

  • Design with security from the ground up. Don’t rely on patches — embed protection in silicon to prepare for today’s and tomorrow’s threats, including quantum risks.
  • Integrate certified IP across the stack. Use modular, pre-validated blocks that work together seamlessly — from secure boot to interface security.
  • Plan for compliance and longevity. Align early with ISO, NIST, Common Criteria, and other standards so your devices meet requirements and remain secure in the field for years.
