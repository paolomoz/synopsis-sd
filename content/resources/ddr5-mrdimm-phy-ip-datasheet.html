Synopsys DDR5 MRDIMM PHY IP Datasheet

DDR5 MRDIMM technology is designed to meet the growing demand for higher bandwidth and performance in data-intensive applications such as AI, data centers, and high-performance computing. By enabling faster data rates and supporting simultaneous access to multiple cache lines, MRDIMMs significantly improve memory throughput compared to traditional RDIMM solutions. This datasheet will discuss the architecture, features, and capabilities of Synopsys DDR5 MRDIMM PHY IP and how it enables high-performance, high-capacity memory interfaces.

What You Will Learn:

  • The key features and performance advantages of DDR5 MRDIMM PHY IP, including higher data rates and increased bandwidth
  • How the PHY architecture and training mechanisms enable reliable, high-speed memory operation
  • The benefits of supporting RDIMM and MRDIMM configurations for data center, AI, and high-performance computing applications
