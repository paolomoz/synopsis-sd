Device identities and cryptographic root keys are at the foundation of every security system. With the explosion of the IoT, new and scalable ways are needed to get these credentials into the billions of connected devices and keep them secure.

Synopsys PUF is the industry-leading and certified PUF IP solution that enables device manufacturers and designers to secure their products with internally generated, device-unique cryptographic keys. Synopsys PUF leverages standard SRAM behavior to generate a device-unique hardware root of trust without storing secret keys. Because it uses a standard IP component available upon initial release of any process technology (SRAM) as a PUF source, Synopsys PUF can be used with any foundry and process-node technology.

Certification is important for security-sensitive applications across markets from IoT, and consumer, to data centers, automotive, industrial, defense, aerospace, and healthcare. Synopsys PUF Premium is the world’s first IP solution to be awarded “PSA Certified Level 3 RoT Component and SESIP Level 3.” This certifies that the IP includes substantial protection against both software and hardware attacks. It allows chip designers to fast-track their products for full PSA Level 3 certification and further helps ensure supply chain integrity, chiplet security, and protection against reverse engineering.

Synopsys also offers an Automotive PUF Premium product variant—the industry’s first PUF IP certified for ASIL B/ISO 26262 functional safety and ISO/SAE 21434 cybersecurity standards compliance—engineered for the automotive market, where safety and security meet.

Synopsys PUF can be applied easily to almost any chip – from tiny microcontrollers (MCUs) to high-performance systems-on-chip (SoCs). The IP is FIPS 140-3 ready and has been proven in hundreds of millions of devices certified by EMVCo, Visa, CC EAL6+, PSA, ioXt, and governments across the globe.

Highlights

  • Proven and mature PUF solution and world’s first IP to be awarded “PSA Certified Level 3 RoT Component and SESIP Level 3”
  • Uses standard SRAM power-up values as a PUF and is quantum safe
  • Offers key provisioning, wrapping, and unwrapping to enable secure key storage across the supply chain and for the entire lifetime of the device
  • Keys are never stored, but re-created from the PUF each time they are needed, offering a higher level of security than traditional key storage in NVM
  • Keys are bound to the device and can only be recreated and accessed on the device they have been created on
  • ISO 26262 functional safety (ASIL B Random, ASIL D Systematic) and ISO/SAE 21434 cybersecurity standards compliant (Synopsys Automotive PUF Premium)
  • Integrates easily and scales with all foundries and technology nodes
