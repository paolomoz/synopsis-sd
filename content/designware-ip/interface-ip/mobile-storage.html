Get Started

Consumers demand high-capacity storage and fast access to their data including large video files, high-resolution image files, OS and edge AI LLM models on battery-operated devices. Designers must integrate quality interface IP in their SoCs to meet their mobile storage needs.

With Synopsys’ high-quality and silicon-proven mobile storage IP solutions, designers can lower the risk and cost of integrating IP in their SoCs. Synopsys’ power-, performance- and area-optimized solutions address the specific storage interface needs of a variety of consumer, mobile, Laptop PCs, Artificial Intelligence of Things (AIoT) and automotive applications.

A portfolio of configurable and synthesizable digital controllers as well as silicon-proven PHY IP support the latest SD, SDIO, MIPI UniPro and M-PHY, JEDEC UFS and eMMC specifications.
