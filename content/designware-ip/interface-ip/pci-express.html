overview

With over two decades of expertise in PCIe IP, Synopsys enables AI, HPC, networking, storage, mobile, and automotive SoCs with the low latency and high-performance connectivity needed for next-generation designs. The complete PCIe IP solution, including silicon-proven PHYs, digital controllers, Integrity and Data Encryption (IDE) Security IP, and Verification IP, combined with Synopsys’ expert engineering support provides designers a low-risk path to silicon success. Synopsys PCIe IP has undergone extensive third-party interoperability testing, offering seamless ecosystem integration and accelerating time-to-production. As active members of the PCI-SIG Consortium, Synopsys has deep insights into spec development and status, helping ensure compliance to evolving standards.
