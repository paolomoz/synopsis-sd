Get Started

Synopsys offers a comprehensive HBM IP solution, including controller, PHY, and verification IP, to meet the high-bandwidth and low-power memory requirements of multi-die and system-on-chip (SoC) designs targeting AI and high-performance computing applications. The Synopsys HBM4/4E IP is the sixth generation of HBM IP, featuring pin bandwidths of up to 12 Gbps, resulting in an overall interface bandwidth of over 3 TBps. Synopsys' HBM3/3E and HBM2/2E IP have been validated in numerous designs and shipped in millions of chips, enabling designers to minimize integration risk, accelerate time-to-market, and achieve first-pass silicon success.
