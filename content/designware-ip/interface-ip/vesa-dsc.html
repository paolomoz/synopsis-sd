Get Started

Synopsys VESA Display Stream Compression (DSC) IP, including encoder and decoder, delivers high-bandwidth, small area, and low-power image compression for mobile, AR/VR, and automotive SoCs. The IP delivers a full display solution by working seamlessly with Synopsys HDMI 2.1, DisplayPort, and MIPI DSI IP. For immersive viewing experiences, the IP enables visually lossless data transmission and minimizes latency. Compliant with the latest VESA DSC 1.1 and 1.2a standards, the Synopsys IP enables displays to deliver the required 120Hz refresh rate, high dynamic range, and wide color gamut for up to 10K content.
