The Synopsys USB 3.1 Device Controller is a set of synthesizable soft IP that ASIC/FPGA designers can use to implement a complete USB 3.1 Device for 10 Gbps speeds. Integrating this digital, synthesizable logic into an ASIC, FPGA, or ASSP peripheral design helps to ensure USB 3.1 compliance, device functionality, and backward compatibility with USB 3.0 and 2.0. Optimizing development time and minimizing engineering risk, the Synopsys USB 3.1 Device Controller helps designers bring their peripheral designs to market faster for wired and wireless applications such as smart phones, tablets, TVs, set-top boxes, cameras, modems, game consoles, PCs, and telecommunications equipment.

Optimized for low power, the Synopsys USB 3.1 Controller allows designers to maximize power efficiency for extended battery life. The Synopsys USB 3.1 Controller enables the fastest USB data transfer speeds while lowering overall power consumption.

The USB 3.1 Device Controller features industry-standard interfaces (PIPE, UTMI+ and ULPI) that connect to a USB 3.1 PHY.

Synopsys USB Controllers have shipped in over one billion units for electronics leading companies worldwide. Using Synopsys USB IP significantly reduces development time and engineering risk, bringing USB-based SoCs to market faster.

Synopsys USB IP is the most certified IP solution in the industry. With over 3,000 design wins, Synopsys' complete USB IP solution--consisting of controllers, PHYs, verification IP, drivers, and IP prototypes--enables designers to lower integration risk and speed time-to-market.

Highlights & Key Features

Highlights

  • Supports USB 3.1 at 10 Gbps, SuperSpeed USB 3.0 at 5 Gbps and High-Speed USB (USB 2.0)
  • Optimized power management with USB power savings modes, Uniform Power Format (UPF) and dual power rails
  • Configurable data buffering options to fine-tune performance/area trade-offs
  • Supports PIPE, UTMI+ and ULPI PHY interfaces
