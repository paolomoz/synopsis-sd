FAQ

What is Synopsys IP for Edge AI and how does it benefit my designs?

Synopsys IP for Edge AI is a comprehensive portfolio of silicon-proven, standards-compliant interface IP, processors, embedded memories, logic libraries, IOs, and security IP. It is purpose-built to accelerate Edge AI designs by providing reliable connectivity, robust security, efficient processing, and low-power operation, helping designers succeed in demanding market environments.

How does Synopsys IP ensure secure and reliable Edge AI solutions?

Synopsys Security IP offers hardware-based protection for sensitive AI workloads through individual security blocks (such as crypto, PUF, and TRNG) and complete embedded security subsystems. It also includes interface security modules for encrypted and authenticated communication, ensuring data protection and integrity throughout your design.

What performance features are available in Synopsys IP for Edge AI applications?

Synopsys Processor Solutions enables scalable AI performance from 1 to 400+ TOPS using ARC NPX NPUs, power-saving ARC-V CPUs with built-in DSPs for enhanced processing, and smart sensors for temperature and energy monitoring. Additionally, customizable embedded memories, logic libraries, and IOs are engineered for optimal power, performance, and area efficiency.

In what ways does Synopsys Foundation IP meet the unique demands of Edge AI design?

Choosing Synopsys Foundation IP for edge AI devices gives you access to a broad range of high-quality, silicon-proven IP, including Embedded Memories, Logic Libraries, IOs, and NVM. These solutions are designed for optimal performance, power efficiency, and area, and can be tailored to your specific requirements. With extensive characterization and strong technical support, Synopsys Foundation IP helps streamline integration and speed up your edge AI product development.

How does Synopsys Interface IP accelerate Edge AI applications?

Synopsys Interface IP delivers the high-bandwidth, low-latency connectivity edge AI SoCs require to move data quickly between sensors, processors, and memories. Standards-compliant IP for PCI Express®, MIPI, USB, LPDDR/DDR, and Ethernet enables seamless integration of AI accelerators, high-resolution image sensors, and external memory, while ensuring interoperability and reducing design risk. By providing silicon-proven, low-power interface IP optimized for advanced process nodes, Synopsys helps designers meet stringent power, performance, and area targets for edge devices.
