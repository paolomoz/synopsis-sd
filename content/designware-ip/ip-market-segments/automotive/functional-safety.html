IP for Automotive ISO 26262 Functional Safety

Automotive IP Brochure

Safety-critical systems rely on SoCs and IP to meet Automotive Safety and Integrity Levels (ASILs) specific to each application.

Synopsys’ ASIL B and D Compliant, automotive-grade IP portfolio is developed and assessed specifically for random hardware faults with ASIL D systematic. The ASIL B and D Ready, automotive-grade IP portfolio is developed and assessed specifically for random hardware faults. The Synopsys ARC processors are compliant up to ASIL D random and ASIL D systematic.

Using certified IP helps SoC designers reduce supply chain risk and accelerate the requirements specification, design, implementation, integration, verification, validation and configuration of their SoC-level functional safety.
