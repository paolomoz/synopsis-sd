overview

Synopsys provides the industry’s broadest portfolio of complete, silicon-proven IP solutions, with leading power, performance, area, and security, for the most widely used interfaces such as PCI Express®, CXL, UALink, USB, Ethernet, DDR, HBM, Die-to-Die, CCIX, MIPI, and HDMI.

With a strong investment in developing high-quality IP, designers can trust that the IP will be interoperable and successfully integrate into their SoCs, while minimizing risk and accelerating time to market. Thousands of customers trust Synopsys IP in their SoCs.
