overview

Synopsys comprehensive systems design solutions accelerate the development and verification of complex electronic systems, spanning IP through SoCs to systems and software. These solutions encompass virtual prototyping, emulation, FPGA-based prototyping, and system test generation, enabling early software development, thorough system validation, and rapid hardware-software integration. By providing high-performance, scalable, and integrated platforms, Synopsys helps customers accelerate their development cycles, reduce risks and costs, and bring high-quality, innovative products to market more quickly.
