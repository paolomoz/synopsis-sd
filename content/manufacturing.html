overview

Synopsys is a leading provider of software solutions for silicon manufacturing and silicon lifecycle management. Our manufacturing portfolio includes solutions for TCAD, Mask Synthesis, and Manufacturing Analytics. Using advanced techniques in AI/ML and big data, Synopsys enables its customers to reap the benefits of Industry 4.0. The smart manufacturing solutions from Synopsys are built upon Synopsys’ extensive expertise in IC design, mask synthesis, process modeling, on-chip test and monitoring techniques and cloud-based data analytics.
