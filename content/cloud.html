What is Synopsys Cloud?

Synopsys Cloud is a chip design automation platform that enables you to manage and access EDA licenses instantly from on-premises or from the cloud. Synopsys Cloud offers a unique FlexEDA business model with patented license management automation and metering service that allows designers to scale EDA workloads with extreme flexibility. It also features a Synopsys-managed SaaS infrastructure option that delivers a comprehensive EDA design environment.
