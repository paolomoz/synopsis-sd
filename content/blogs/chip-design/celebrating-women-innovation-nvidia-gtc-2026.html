Introduction

At NVIDIA GTC 2026, the world’s leading minds came together to explore the future of AI, silicon, and accelerated computing. This year, amid groundbreaking announcements and technical innovation, Synopsys showcased the progress and impact of its strategic partnership with NVIDIA to revolutionize design and engineering across industries. But we also set out to create a different kind of moment — one centered on connection.

March is Women’s History Month, which originated as a national celebration in 1981 when Congress authorized and requested the President to proclaim the week beginning March 7, 1982 as “Women’s History Week.” In 1987 a resolution was passed designating the entire month of March as “Women’s History Month.”

Helping Innovation Grow

In celebration of Women’s History Month, we brought a simple idea to life at GTC: Innovation blooms here.

Throughout the week, we shared flowers with attendees at our booth — a small gesture that quickly grew into something more meaningful. Engineers, industry leaders, and rising talent paused to connect, exchange stories, and celebrate the people behind the technology.

In an environment often defined by speed and scale, these moments stood out. They were a reminder that innovation doesn’t happen in isolation. Together with ecosystem partners, Synopsys is re-engineering how products are designed and developed. By enabling the co-design of electronics and multiphysics, accelerating compute-intensive workloads and applying the power of digital twin for virtual prototyping, we are helping customers engineer the future.

That future is driven by diverse perspectives, collaboration, and individuals connecting with one another to push boundaries every day. At GTC, we demonstrated how integrating the strengths of NVIDIA's AI and accelerated computing with Synopsys' market-leading engineering solutions is enabling R&D teams to design, simulate, and verify intelligent products at lower cost with greater precision and speed. We also demonstrated the power of connecting with each other.

Conclusion

At Synopsys, we’re proud to support the engineers and innovators shaping what’s possible. We’re also proud to celebrate the women who are helping lead that future.

Because behind every breakthrough, innovation blooms through people.
