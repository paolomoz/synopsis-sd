Companies are racing to harness the power of generative AI (GenAI) to boost productivity and drive operational efficiency. GenAI has the potential to increase annual U.S. productivity by 1.5 percent, according to Goldman Sachs, delivering $7 trillion in economic value over the next decade. But how is it best deployed and activated in an enterprise setting, and what are the results?

Getting enterprise GenAI applications off the ground securely and at scale has proven challenging. As a result, 48 percent of more than 2.8K global business leaders surveyed by Deloitte in late 2023 said they don’t anticipate a business transformation from their GenAI initiatives for up to three years. Synopsys has always been on the cutting edge of technology innovation and enablement. We were the first to introduce AI-driven EDA tools for our customers, and we’re at the forefront of enterprise GenAI. In fact, during the course of 2024, our GenAI Center of Excellence developed and deployed custom GenAI apps designed to improve operational efficiency while safeguarding the company’s proprietary information and best practices.

We are pleased to report the early success of these internal pilot projects. In addition to boosting cross-functional productivity and giving our corporate teams more time for high-value, customer-focused activities, more than 1.5 million lines of software code have been developed using AI assistance. And based on data analyses and projections, we expect our internal GenAI applications to yield 250,000 hours of employee capacity in 2025. Because we’re in the early stages of deployment and utilization, we’re just scratching the surface of what’s possible.

Leveraging generative AI to boost enterprise productivity

Teams acrossSynopsys — including sales, people, marketing, investor relations, and engineering — are now using custom GenAI applications that have been trained on our own proprietary data:

  • Synopsys’ global sales and customer success team members can use GenAI for immediate access to product information and answers, expediting and informing sales and support operations.
  • 100+ recruiters, staffing experts, and hiring managers are using GenAI apps to streamline and enrich the creation of job descriptions, facilitate hiring processes, and improve the candidate experience.
  • Our marketing teams have been early power-users, leveraging GenAI to create engaging brand collateral, improve message clarity, and boost social media engagement.
  • Our investor relations team is using GenAI to speed the analysis and reporting of market data.
  • Our engineering teams are using GenAI to boost productivity.

Accelerating engineering and software development workflows

Our software development activities have particularly benefited from the use of GenAI applications.

These teams are leveraging GenAI apps to accelerate engineering and development workflows by utilizing natural-language user interfaces, always-on assistants, and self-service platforms and microservices securely trained on our own proprietary data. Early results observed include:

  • Over the past three quarters, more than 1,500 Synopsys developers have embraced GenAI-powered apps, and we are onboarding hundreds of additional engineers each quarter to leverage the platform.
  • Synopsys has developed more than 1 million lines of code using GenAI assistance, boosting our team's productivity and supporting higher quality code thanks to more time available for implementation and testing.
  • The GenAI-powered Synopsys apps are becoming our engineering team’s "first line of defense" for internal support and an excellent way to accelerate the onboarding of new engineers.

“The ingenuity of our Synopsys teams is truly boundless. Just as we pioneered GenAI to address one of the most complex engineering challenges – the design process for semiconductors – we have invested in our own innovation engine to accelerate internal productivity, creativity, and operational efficiency.”

Raja Tabet, Senior Vice President | Synopsys Engineering Excellence Group
