Here's a snapshot of this year’s impact:

  • 3,500+ volunteer hours reported
  • 73 different projects
  • 20 participating countries
  • 43 office locations globally

In Porto, Portugal, employees planted and helped maintain native species of trees near Maia City Hall.

Under the Synopsys for Good program, employees participated in volunteer projects aligned with our three focus areas: preparing students for the future with STEM education, addressing essential needs and inequities in the community, and working to mitigate climate change.

A few examples: teams planted trees in Armenia; participated in a coastal cleanup in Shanghai, China; beautified a school in Chile; raised money and awareness of mental health in Northern Ireland; and assembled hygiene kits at Synopsys’ Sunnyvale U.S. headquarters. You can see a full list of volunteer projectshere, and check out photographs of some of the activities below.

Through volunteering, financial donations, matching funds, in-kind items, and grants, Synopsys has been contributing to causes in our communities since 1992. It’s part of our commitment toward creating a Smart Future, one that is sustainable, just, and secure.

Calgary, Canada employees baked 550 goods for BB4CK, Brown Bagging for Calgary's Kids.

Markham, Canada employees helped to clean and beautify Yellow Brick House.

Synopsys President and CEO Sassine Ghazi helped assemble hygeine kits for Sunnyvale Community Services at the company's Sunnyvale headquarters.

Gdansk, Poland employees pitched in to help clean and organize at the WAGA Association.

Shanghai, China employees helped remove litter along the Fengxian coastline.

Noida, India employees created planter boxes for new seedlings and cleaned up outdoor spaces at Gandhi Smarak Vidyalaya school.
