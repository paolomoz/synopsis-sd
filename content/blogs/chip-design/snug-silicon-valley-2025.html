What to expect

SNUG Silicon Valley 2025 will explore the Era of Pervasive Intelligence and the latest innovations in silicon-to-systems design. The conference will be packed with opportunities to get inspired, learn, and grow.

​

Featured keynotes and panel discussions

  • Re-Engineering Engineering: Sassine Ghazi, Synopsys President and CEO, will explain how traditional engineering practices are being re-engineered by – and because of – pervasive intelligence.
  • OpenAI Keynote: Richard Ho, Head of Hardware at OpenAI, will describe the impact AI is having on modern computing systems.
  • Sustainable Computing: Silicon-to-Systems: Rene Haas, CEO of Arm, and Sassine Ghazi will outline the critical needs and latest trends surrounding sustainable computing.
  • Is Pervasive Intelligence Accelerating the Multi-Die Revolution? Experts from several leading technology companies will discuss the confluence of AI, multi-die designs, and semiconductor innovation.

​

Technical sessions and demos

  • 100+ technical presentations: Explore a wide range of topics across 10 technology tracks.
  • Tutorials and peer-reviewed talks: Enhance your skills and knowledge through expert-led sessions.
  • Demo theater: See live demonstrations of the latest Synopsys and ecosystem solutions.

​

Networking opportunities

  • SNUG Pub: Relax and network with peers at our popular SNUG Pub.
  • After party and awards: Celebrate achievements and unwind with food, drinks, and entertainment.
