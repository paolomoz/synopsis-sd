Building and Scaling an AI Chip Startup Panel Highlights

This dynamic panel brought together founders and leaders from some of today's most innovative AI chip startups:

  • Mor Ben-Asher (CEO and Co-founder, AI-Fabrics)
  • Eric Norige (Co-founder and SW CTO, Baya Systems)
  • Steve Majors (Sr. Vice President of Engineering, DreamBig Semiconductor)
  • Pirooz Hojabri (CEO/CTO and Co-founder, IC Photonics LLC)
  • Wilfred Gomes (CEO and Co-founder, Mueon Corporation)

In the hour long conversation, moderated by Sanjay Bali (Sr. Vice President, Product Management, Synopsys), these panelists shared their candid perspectives on what makes building a silicon startup unique compared to software or services companies. They discussed their experiences with securing funding, setting up critical infrastructure, assembling talented teams, and finding the right market fit. The conversation also delved deeply into practical strategies for overcoming challenges in areas such as hiring, scaling operations, leveraging cloud resources, and launching products to market.
