Today’s workforce is varied and changing. Some workers are fully remote, geographically dispersed across time zones. Some workers are on site. Others are in a hybrid environment. While a shift in how work gets done was already happening, the pandemic accelerated the rate of the change. It taught us that relying solely on in-person classroom training is no longer an option. And in any case, it can be difficult to dedicate whole days away from your work for training. But here is a truth: to stay current in the fast-evolving world of technology, you must train…continually. In fact, your learning journey in tech will be a lifelong one, if you and your business want to be leaders in your field.

With this in mind, we’ve made Synopsys training easier and more accessible, with instant access and faster learning with our new Synopsys Learning Center. If you are a registered Synopsys user, you can decide how, when, and where to train on Synopsys products with ultimate flexibility—whatever is best for you. Read on to learn about Synopsys Learning Center’s broad spectrum of benefits to catapult your semiconductor design knowledge to the front of the class.

New ACE Subscription Model Delivers Learning Anytime, On-Demand

Our new Accelerated Customer Education (ACE) subscriptions now give you access to learning 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year. With an ACE subscription, you can launch online, modular coursework from anywhere via your web browser and take as many courses as you’d like. Out of the gate, Synopsys customers have access to a library of self-paced modules, webinars, and quick tips. Subscriptions include a mix of additional features, including premium content, gamification, badging, and hands-on labs. Soon, there will be unlimited certification exams and premium expert-led workshops, along with access to subject matter experts.

Personalized Learning Experience for All

The Synopsys Learning Center gives you exactly what you need with enhanced personalization experiences. There is content available for every kind of user:

  • New Users: You can start learning immediately. For premium plans, we have pre-defined learning journeys that will focus on key domains through progressive coursework. You can also gain practice with hands-on labs in the cloud, all at your own pace.
  • Expert Users: Search on topics as you need it. Refresh your knowledge and keep up with the latest advancements to stay current with the up and coming. The Synopsys Learning Center includes access to advanced topics and in-depth, expert-led webinars and downloadable labs.
anchor
2

Role-Based Learning Journeys

Our modular coursework has enabled us to bring you curated, progressive role-based learning journeys. We’ve developed curriculum on a variety of topics from A to Z (that’s Analog Design to ZeBu® Emulation System!) Our learning journeys are tailored to your function, structured, and self-paced.

anchor
3

Earn Digital Badges

Upskilling and reskilling are becoming increasingly important today both for individuals and organizations. To enable you or your workforce, Synopsys is now offering exams for select courses. Grow your skills by completing courses and passing the exam with set criteria. Get awarded with a verifiable Digital badge. Broadcast your achievements to friends and colleagues on social media – LinkedIn, FaceBook, or Twitter to get the recognition you deserve.

What’s Next in the Synopsys Learning Center?

You have a lot to look forward to with the Synopsys Learning Center. Our training library is growing all the time and enhancements are ongoing. It’s good to check back periodically to see what’s new. It is a central place that will help you sharpen your skill set and learn about the latest innovations in designing the future of Smart Everything.

Bookmark us and learn more at the Synopsys Learning Center.
