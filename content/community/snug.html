What is SNUG?

SNUG (Synopsys Users Group) is a global conference series hosted by Synopsys, bringing together semiconductor and electronic design engineers to share insights, technical innovations, and best practices in chip design, verification, and implementation.

Who attends SNUG?

SNUG is attended by engineers, designers, verification specialists, and technical experts from semiconductor companies, system integrators, and academia. It provides a platform for networking, knowledge sharing, and collaboration among industry leaders.

What topics are covered at SNUG?

SNUG covers a wide range of topics, including digital and analog design, verification methodologies, multi-die systems, AI-driven chip design, silicon lifecycle management, and advancements in EDA (Electronic Design Automation) tools and IP solutions.

Why should engineers attend SNUG?

Attending SNUG provides engineers with access to exclusive technical sessions, hands-on workshops, and keynote presentations from Synopsys experts and industry leaders. It is an opportunity to gain insights into the latest trends and technologies shaping semiconductor design.

How can someone participate in SNUG?

Participants can join SNUG by registering for the event in their region. Engineers can also contribute by submitting technical papers, presenting case studies, and sharing their experiences using Synopsys tools to solve design challenges.
