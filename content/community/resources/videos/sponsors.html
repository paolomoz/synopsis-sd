Samsung Foundry

Samsung Foundry’s advanced processes, design technologies, IP and high-volume manufacturing give customers access to deep design and process expertise. The business’s full suite of advanced technologies includes 14/11/10/8nm FinFET, 28/18 FD-SOI, 7/5/4nm EUV, 3nm GAA as well as other specialty technologies from RF, eNVM, high voltage, BCD, to CMOS image sensors and finger print sensors. In concert with its process technology leadership, Samsung Foundry provides integrated package and test solutions including 3D/2.5D. Samsung Foundry’s SAFE™ ecosystem also offers a full range of verified EDA/DM solutions, product-proven IP, flexible design environment through cloud, and comprehensive ASIC design service. Samsung Foundry prides itself on working closely with every customer from initial design concept to high-volume manufacturing, with transparency and flawless execution, to deliver the highest quality product. For more information, please visit Samsung Foundry.

TSMC

TSMC pioneered the pure-play foundry business model when it was founded in 1987, and has been the world’s leading dedicated semiconductor foundry. The Company supports a thriving ecosystem of global customers and partners with the industry’s leading process technologies and portfolio of design enablement solutions to unleash innovation for the global semiconductor industry. With global operations spanning Asia, Europe, and North America, TSMC serves as a committed corporate citizen around the world.

TSMC deployed 281 distinct process technologies, and manufactured 11,617 products for 510 customers in 2020 by providing broadest range of advanced, specialty and advanced packaging technology services. TSMC is the first foundry to provide 5-nanometer production capabilities, the most advanced semiconductor process technology available in the world. The Company is headquartered in Hsinchu, Taiwan. For more information please visit https://www.tsmc.com.

UMC

UMC (NYSE: UMC, TWSE: 2303)is a leading global semiconductor foundry. The company provides high quality IC production with a focus on both logic and specialty technologies. UMC’s comprehensive technology and manufacturing solutions include logic/RF, embedded high voltage, embedded flash, RFSOI/BCD on 8" & 12" wafers and IATF-16949 automotive manufacturing certification for all its manufacturing facilities. UMC operates 12 fabs that have a maximum capacity of more than 750,000 8-inch equivalent wafers per month. The company employs approximately 19,000 people worldwide, with offices in Taiwan, China, United States, Europe, Japan, Korea and Singapore. For more information, please visit www.umc.com.
