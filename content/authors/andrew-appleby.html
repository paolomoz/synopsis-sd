Andrew Appleby

Andrew Appleby is the Principal Product Manager for Synopsys Logic Library IP, having joined Synopsys in 2023. With over 30 years of experience in the semiconductor industry, he previously worked at Philips Semiconductors, STM, CSR, and Arm. Andrew holds a Master of Science in Telecommunications and a Bachelor of Science in Physics from Aston University and University College London, respectively.
