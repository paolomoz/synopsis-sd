Monica Olvera

Monica Olvera is a Senior Staff Technical Product Manager at Synopsys, leading PCIe technical product strategy and development. With ~10 years of experience in SerDes IP, and hands-off experience with validation and electrical testing, she has held roles in silicon validation, product management, and engineering. Monica has contributed to PCIe Gen 1 to 7 validation, automation, and customer support, and previously worked at Semtech, Maury Microwave, and United Launch Alliance. She holds a bachelor’s degree in engineering from California State Polytechnic University, Pomona.
