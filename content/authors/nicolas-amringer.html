Nicolas Amringer

Nicolas Amringer is an Automotive Architect for virtual prototyping in the Synopsys Technology and Product Development Group. His career spans over 18 years in the domain of automotive software development, with a focus on the development of X-in-the-Loop testing and simulation solutions, where he has also contributed to the field through publications and patents. Nicolas is steering the technical direction and development of market-leading automotive testing and simulation solutions in the context of electronics digital twins (eDT).
