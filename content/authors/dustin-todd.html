Dustin Todd

Dustin Todd serves as Vice President, Head of Worldwide Government Affairs, representing Synopsys before policymakers and supporting global government engagement. He previously led Government Affairs at Amazon and the Semiconductor Industry Association (SIA) and served in the U.S. House of Representatives as Chief of Staff to Congressman Paul Tonko. Dustin is a graduate of the University of Mississippi and Johns Hopkins University.
