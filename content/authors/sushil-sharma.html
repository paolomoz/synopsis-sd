Sushil Sharma

Sushil Sharma is senior director of product management for IP Accelerated Solutions at Synopsys, responsible for interface IP subsystems, hardening, and SIPI services. He has more than 30 years of experience working in the telecommunications and semiconductor industries, with roles spanning engineering, applications, and product management. Sushil holds a Bachelor of Engineering degree in Electrical Engineering (with electronics and telecom specialization) from the University of Rajasthan, India.
