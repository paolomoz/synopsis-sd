Buvanesh Balasubramanian

Buvanesh Balasubramanian is a Principal Engineer in Cloud development, focused on designing and scaling cloud-native platforms. He brings deep expertise in distributed systems, full-stack engineering, and modern cloud architecture, with an emphasis on building resilient, high-performance solutions. He holds a master’s degree in software engineering. He plays a key role in advancing the Synopsys Cloud initiative by driving key components and delivering robust, scalable cloud solutions that support next-generation SaaS capabilities for EDA workloads.
