Martin Niset

Martin Niset is the Executive Director for the Non-Volatile Memory (NVM) IP product line at Synopsys, responsible for the development of One Time Programmable (OTP) and Multi Time Programmable (MTP) NVM solutions. Prior to joining Synopsys, Martin managed all silicon testing and qualification activities for the NVM product lines at Virage Logic and Impinj. He has 25 years of experience working with embedded NVM, including automotive-grade embedded Flash technology while at Freescale. Martin holds a Bachelor of Science degree in Physics from the Université Libre de Bruxelles and a Master of Science degree in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Texas at Austin.
