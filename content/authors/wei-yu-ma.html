Wei-Yu Ma

Wei-Yu Ma is a Technical Marketing Manager at Synopsys with over 18 years of experience in the semiconductor industry. Throughout his career, he has worked at leading foundry and fabless semiconductor companies, with a strong focus on GPIO, specialty I/O, and 3D I/O technologies. Wei-Yu brings deep expertise in I/O and ESD design and is the holder of 16 patents in related technologies. He holds a Master of Science in Electronics Engineering from National Taiwan University and a Bachelor of Engineering in Mechanical Engineering from National Cheng Kung University.
