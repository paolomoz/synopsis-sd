Dr. Kevin Wei

Dr. Kevin Wei has more than two decades of experience in the semiconductor industry. As Principal of Strategic Management at Synopsys, he is responsible for strategic partnerships and initiatives in Taiwan. He also leads cross-departmental efforts to advance GenAI, high-performance computing (HPC), and automotive platforms. Dr. Wei is an active member of the AI-on-Chip Taiwan Alliance (AITA) and the Global Automotive Advisory Council (GAAC).
