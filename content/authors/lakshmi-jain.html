Lakshmi Jain

Lakshmi Jain is a Director of Product Marketing for Synopsys IO Library IP, bringing over 20 years of international experience in high-technology semiconductor companies. She is a seasoned marketing manager who has driven competitive advantage through innovation, collaboration, and the development of new products and solutions. Lakshmi has a unique combination of customer-facing experience, marketing expertise, and project and product management skills, which have contributed to the successful delivery of products that drive growth. She holds a bachelor’s degree in Electronics & Communication and is a PMP-certified professional.
