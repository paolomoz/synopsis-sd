Anand Thiruvengadam

Anand Thiruvengadam is Executive Director and head of AI product management at Synopsys. In this role, he is responsible for leading the integrated AI solutions/product strategy across UI, Apps, and Infrastructure. Prior to this, Anand held dual leadership roles as head of the circuit simulation line of business and head of the memory market segment at Synopsys. His experience spans high-tech product strategy, marketing, management, business development, and engineering. Before Synopsys, he was a management consultant at PwC, focusing on strategic and operational transformations. He also held engineering leadership roles at AMD and other leading semiconductor companies.
