Ravi Todi

Ravi Todi is Executive Director of Product Management at Synopsys, leading product strategy for the Foundation IP and Security IP portfolios. An IEEE Fellow with more than 20 years of experience in the semiconductor, foundry, and IP industries, he has held technical and leadership roles at Rivos, Western Digital, GLOBALFOUNDRIES, Qualcomm, and IBM, spanning advanced process technology, design-technology co-optimization, and product management. Ravi earned a Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Central Florida, along with master's degrees in electrical and mechanical engineering.
