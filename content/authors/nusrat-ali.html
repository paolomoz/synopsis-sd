Nusrat Ali

Nusrat Ali is Senior Director at Synopsys, leading System Verification solutions across diverse technologies like simulation, emulation, and chip bring-up. He also focuses on verification solution for MIPI, Storage, Display, and GenAI-enabled verification. With 25 years of experience in Architecture, FPGA/ASIC designs, IP/SoC verification, and board bring-up, he has expertise in Processor verification, ARMv8/v9, RISC-V Cache coherency, Memories and PCIe. Nusrat has submitted two patents and numerous technical papers.
