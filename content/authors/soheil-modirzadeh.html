Soheil Modirzadeh

Soheil Modirzadeh is a Senior Manager of Product & Solutions Marketing at Synopsys, where he drives thought leadership and go-to-market initiatives for Synopsys' multi-die design solution. He works closely with the product management team, leading semiconductor companies, foundries, and ecosystem partners to help accelerate AI, high-performance computing, and heterogeneous integration solutions. Prior to Synopsys, Soheil held product marketing roles at Xilinx (acquired by AMD), and Cadence. He has more than 25 years of experience in the semiconductor industry spanning design and IP technologies.
