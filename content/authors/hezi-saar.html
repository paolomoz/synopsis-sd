Hezi Saar

Hezi Saar is Executive Director of Product Marketing at Synopsys, where he leads the Mobile, Automotive, and Consumer IP product lines. He brings more than 20 years of experience in the semiconductor and embedded systems industries. Hezi holds a B.S. in Computer Science and Economics from Tel Aviv University, an MBA from Columbia Southern University, and executive certificates in strategy, innovation, management, and leadership from MIT Sloan. He also serves as Chair of the MIPI Alliance Board of Directors, which defines interface specifications for the mobile, automotive, IoT, Physical AI, and related industries.
