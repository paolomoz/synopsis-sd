Alex Wakefield

Alex Wakefield is a Synopsys scientist, with degrees in engineering and computer science from the University of Adelaide in Australia. Over the last few years, Alex has primarily been focused power estimation, leading the Synopsys global rollout of power estimation with the Synopsys ZeBu® emulation family. He is involved in all Synopsys power estimation engagements worldwide.
