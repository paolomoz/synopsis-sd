Barbara Donaldson

Barbara Donaldson is the vice president of workplace resources at Synopsys. She joined Synopsys in 1995 and has held various positions of progressive responsibility within the Real Estate and Facilities Group. Barbara holds a B.S. in organizational development from the University of San Francisco and an MBA from the University of California, Berkeley and Columbia University.
