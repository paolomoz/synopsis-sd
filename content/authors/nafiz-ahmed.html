Nafiz Ahmed

Nafiz Ahmed is a Staff Field Applications Engineer at Synopsys supporting the Foundation IP portfolio. He has more than a decade of experience in the semiconductor and software industries, focusing on standard cell library development, non‑volatile memory IP design, analog verification, and technical leadership. He has also worked extensively on advanced-node technologies, low‑power design, and IP enablement for Tier‑1 customers.
