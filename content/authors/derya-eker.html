Derya Eker

Derya Eker is a Senior Manager of Software Engineering at the Synopsys IP, overseeing ARC AI Processor software and tools product development. With over 24 years of experience in the semiconductor industry, Derya has a deep understanding of hardware/software co-design across diverse application domains. She excels in building diverse, global teams and driving product development with a focus on quality and customer satisfaction. Prior to joining Synopsys, Derya held various management and engineering positions at Intel, Ericsson, and NXP/Philips Semiconductors. She is a proud recipient of the 2019 Synopsys Excellence Award. Derya holds a bachelor’s degree in electrical engineering.
