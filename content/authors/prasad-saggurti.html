Prasad Saggurti

Prasad Saggurti is the Executive Director of Product Management for Foundation IP and NVM at Synopsys. Before joining Synopsys, Prasad held senior engineering and marketing roles at ARM, National Semiconductor, and Sun Microsystems. He holds a Master of Science in Electrical Engineering from the University of Wisconsin-Madison and an MBA from the University of California, Berkeley.
