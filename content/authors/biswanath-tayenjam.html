Biswanath Tayenjam

Biswanath Tayenjam is a Principal Engineer at Synopsys IP with 19 years of extensive experience in ASIC digital design and verification. His expertise primarily lies in the reliability, availability, and serviceability (RAS) features of Synopsys' DDR Controller IP. In addition to holding two patents, Biswanath earned a Bachelor of Engineering degree from Visvesvaraya Technological University.
