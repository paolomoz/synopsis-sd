Improving Quality of Service with Synopsys IP for AMBA Interconnect

By Sreenath Panganamala, Sriram Balasubramanian, Phani Emani, Suresh Venkatachalam

Tags: Silicon IP Technical Bulletin, SoC Infrastructure IP, Silicon IP

Suresh Venkatachalam

Suresh Venkatachalam is a Senior Architect for Synopsys IP, specializing in UFS and UniPro controllers. With over 28 years of experience in the semiconductor industry, including 22 years at Synopsys, Suresh has extensive expertise in developing various standard specification IPs. Some of his predominant architectural products include USB, 10/100 Ethernet MAC, OTG, eMMC/SD, and I3C controller IPs.
