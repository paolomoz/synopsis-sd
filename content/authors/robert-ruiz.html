Robert Ruiz

Robert Ruiz is a director of product line management for several verification products at Synopsys. Robert has held various marketing and technical positions for leading functional verification and test automation products at various companies. He has more than 30 years of experience in advanced EDA technologies and methodologies and spent several years designing application-specific integrated circuits (ASICs). Robert has a B.S. in electrical engineering from Stanford University.
