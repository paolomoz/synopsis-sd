By Bryan Kelly, Marc Serughetti

Tags: About Synopsys

Bryan Kelly

Bryan Kelly is a staff applications engineer in the Synopsys Systems Design Group. He has an M.S. in solid state physics and computer engineering from Iowa State University and was a visiting scholar at the Zhengzhou University physics department. His interests include modeling and simulation of power electronics, motor/generator drives and control systems, battery and renewable energy technologies, and hydraulics and HVAC.
