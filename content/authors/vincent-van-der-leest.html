Vincent van der Leest

Vincent van der Leest manages Product & Solutions Marketing for Security IP at Synopsys. Based in Eindhoven, the Netherlands, Vincent joined Synopsys following the acquisition of Intrinsic ID, where he spent over 14 years leading marketing, research, sales, and business development for their security IP portfolio. An authority in physical unclonable function (PUF) technology, Vincent has played a pivotal role in advancing security solutions for semiconductor and IoT applications. He holds a Master’s Degree in Electrical Engineering from Eindhoven University of Technology and he is the co-author of several granted patents in security and coding theory.
