Dana Neustadter

Dana Neustadter is Senior Director of Product Management for Security IP and Solutions at Synopsys, where she leads product strategy for advanced semiconductor security technologies. With over 25 years of experience across product leadership, engineering, architecture, and business development, Dana helps drive innovation in hardware security, trusted computing, and automotive cybersecurity. She holds M.Eng. and B.Sc. degrees in Electrical Engineering.
