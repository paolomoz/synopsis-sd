Datsen Davies Tharakan

Datsen Davies Tharakan is a senior applications engineer for the Synopsys Systems Design Group. He has a master's degree in power electronics and drives from the University of Nottingham, United Kingdom. With several years of experience as a Synopsys Saber™ applications engineer, he is actively involved in supporting automotive and aerospace customers.
