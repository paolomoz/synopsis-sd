Sutirtha Kabir

Sutirtha Kabir is Executive Director of R&D for Synopsys 3DIC Compiler. He supports the construction and analysis of multi-die systems, including stacked ICs and interposer configurations. Kabir has an MS in electrical engineering and more than two decades of product engineering experience in the EDA industry.
