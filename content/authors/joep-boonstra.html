Tags: Silicon IP Technical Bulletin, Energy-Efficient SoCs, Silicon IP

Joep Boonstra

Joep Boonstra is a Senior Architect for Synopsys IP, leading AI/ML processor hardware architecture and implementation. With over 30 years of experience in the semiconductor industry, Joep has an extensive background in AI/ML, DSP, and processor architecture, design, verification, modeling, as well as software. Throughout his career, Joep has worked for Philips, NXP, Virage Logic, and Synopsys. He holds a Master of Science degree in Electrical Engineering from the University of Twente.
