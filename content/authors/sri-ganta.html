Sri Ganta

Sri Ganta is a Principal Product Manager at Synopsys, managing the Test family of products. Sri has over 20 years of experience in semiconductor test, including EDA and ASIC/SoC product development. Prior to joining Synopsys, Sri was a director of business development for SLT products at Advantest. Sri has also held roles at LogicVision, Broadcom, Qualcomm, and Siemens-EDA, where he held different technical and leadership roles. Sri holds a MS degree in Engineering Management from San Jose State University, a BTech degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Sri Venkateswara University, and an Executive Education certification in Product Strategy from Northwestern Kellogg University.
