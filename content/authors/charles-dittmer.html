Charles Dittmer

Charles Dittmer is a senior staff product manager in the Solutions Group at Synopsys, where he manages low-power wireless IP products. A seasoned IoT professional, Charles offers a unique mix of engineering and business acumen in wireless semiconductors and end-user electronic solutions and services. He has a B.S. degree in electronics and telecommunications and an MBA from Texas A&M University.
