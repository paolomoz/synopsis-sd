Raja Tabet

Raja Tabet is the senior VP of the Engineering Excellence Group at Synopsys, where he and his team are responsible for leading corporate infrastructure transformation initiatives and executing on leadership IT operations, driving significant productivity, robustness, agility, and time-to-market advantage in the creation of Synopsys products and solutions, as well as researching, assessing, and driving Synopsys’ investment and engagement in the next wave of disruptive technologies. He has a master’s degree in computer engineering from Syracuse University and a B.S. in computer science from SUNY Plattsburg.
