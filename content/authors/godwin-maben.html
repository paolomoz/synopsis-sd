Godwin Maben

Godwin Maben is a low-power architect and scientist at Synopsys, where he focuses on low-power challenges associated with multi-voltage/multi-supply designs. A Bangalore University graduate, he has been instrumental in first-time silicon deployment of various low-power architectures and has defined many specifications for the automation of most low-power-sensitive designs.
