Luca Amaru

Dr. Luca Amaru is a principal R&D engineer for the Synopsys EDA Group. His areas of expertise include logic synthesis and verification, and he is a recipient of the 2022 Design Automation Conference (DAC) Under-40 Innovators Award. He has a Ph.D in computer science from the Swiss Federal Institute of Technology Lausanne (EPFL) in Switzerland.
