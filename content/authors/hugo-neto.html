Hugo Neto

Hugo Neto brings over 20 years of international experience in high-tech companies, semiconductor industries, and software corporations. As a Product Manager at Synopsys, his responsibilities include defining business models, pricing and packaging strategies, and overall product positioning to create the right value proposition. Prior to this role, he worked as a customer application engineer supporting MIPI and HDMI IP product lines. Before joining Synopsys, Hugo held roles in USB and DDR test engineering at ChipIdea and Qimonda, as well as application consulting for Verigy/Advantest. He holds a master’s degree in electrical and Telecommunications Engineering from Aveiro University, Portugal.
