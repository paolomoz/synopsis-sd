Anders Blom

Anders Blom is a Principal Solutions Engineer at Synopsys. He has a PhD in physics from Lund University in Sweden and has more than 25 years of experience in atomistic materials modeling and quantum transport simulations. Anders’ history with QuantumATK stretches all the way back to its origins in the company Atomistix, which he joined in 2004 and where he held the position as Director for Solution Services. He was then part of the founding team that morphed Atomistix into QuantumWise in 2008, and served there as Chief Customer Officer until the company was acquired by Synopsys in 2017. Anders has an H-index of 14 and has published more than 60 research papers with more than 2,500 citations in total.
