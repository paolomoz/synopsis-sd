Sylvain Bayon De Noyer

Sylvain Bayon de Noyer is Senior Director of Business Development at Synopsys’ Product Management & Markets Group (PMG), leading virtual prototyping solutions in Asia. With 25+ years of experience across R&D, system architecture, and engineering management, he has held key roles at VLSI Technology, IBM, CoWare, and Synopsys. His expertise spans automotive, AI, mobile, industrial, and networking sectors. He holds an MSEE from ESIEE Paris.
