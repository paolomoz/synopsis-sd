Rohan Bhatnagar

Rohan Bhatnagar is a Product Manager, Senior Staff at Synopsys in the Silicon Lifecycle Management business group and manages the PVT Monitor IP products. He is responsible for total product management including market research and strategy for new product roadmap definition, go-to-market planning, product collateral generation for customer engagements and sales trainings. He has more than a decade and a half of experience in the Electronic Design Automation (EDA) industry focusing on the analog and mixed-signal design space. Rohan holds a Master’s degree in Electrical Engineering from The University of Texas.
