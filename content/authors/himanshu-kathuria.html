Himanshu Kathuria

Himanshu Kathuria is a principal architect for static tools at Synopsys, where he is responsible for designing new technologies within the Synopsys SpyGlass and VC Spyglass product families. With over 16 years of experience in the static verification domain, Himanshu approaches problems with a systematic, creative and innovative mindset. He has a bachelor's degree in engineering from Netaji Subhas Institute of Technology (NSIT), New Delhi, India.
