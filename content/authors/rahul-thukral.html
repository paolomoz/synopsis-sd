Rahul Thukral

Rahul Thukral brings more than two decades of semiconductor experience to his role as product management director for embedded memories for the Synopsys Solutions Group. Rahul has held positions ranging from engineering to field applications at Mentor Graphics and Virage Logic prior to joining Synopsys. Rahul has an M.S. in electrical engineering from Rutgers University.
