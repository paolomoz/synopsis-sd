Sreenath Panganamala

Sreenath Panganamala is a Senior Manager for the Synopsys IP, focusing on AMBA Fabrics, Serial Protocols (SPI, I2C, I2S, UART), DMA Controllers, and Mobile Storage (SD/eMMC) controllers. With over 18 years of industry experience, Sreenath began his career at Synopsys as an application engineer for the IP/VIP product line and has contributed to VIP development. He has a background in IP Functional Verification and holds a master’s degree in VLSI from the National Institute of Technology, Tiruchirappalli.
