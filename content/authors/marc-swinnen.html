Marc Swinnen

Marc is a Product Marketing Manager at Synopsys. Before joining Synopsys, Marc worked at Ansys, Cadence, Azuro, and Sequence Design, where he gained experience with a wide array of digital and analog design tools. Marc holds a master’s in electrical engineering and a master’s in industrial management from KU Leuven in Belgium and an MBA from San Jose State University, California.
