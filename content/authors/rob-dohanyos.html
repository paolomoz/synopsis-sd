Analyze Semiconductor Power Devices More Accurately With Synopsys PowerX

By Rob Dohanyos, Emily Gerken

Rob Dohanyos

Rob Dohanyos serves as Principal Product Specialist at Synopsys, leading the charge on product development and customer innovation for the Diakopto product line and its flagship tool, ParagonX. With nearly 20 years of hands-on experience in the semiconductor industry—working across a vast spectrum of technology nodes, from 0.8um to 2nm—Rob offers a unique, real-user point of view. This practical background, honed through a career that included a successful transition from the field to a mature EDA startup (later acquired by Ansys), helps ensure that the Diakopto group of tools directly addresses the most critical needs of today's chip designers.
