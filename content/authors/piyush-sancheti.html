Piyush Sancheti

Piyush Sancheti leads the System Architects Group at Synopsys, focusing on end-to-end technology solutions for energy-efficient SoC design, design technology co-optimization (DTCO), and multi-die systems. He has previously held engineering, product marketing and management positions at Synopsys, Atrenta (acquired by Synopsys), Sequence Design (acquired by Ansys) and Cadence. He holds an M.S. in computer engineering from Iowa State University.
