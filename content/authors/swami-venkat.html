Swami Venkat

As senior director of strategic programs for the Synopsys EDA Group, Swami is responsible for managing interactions with strategic customers. He joined Synopsys in 1998 and has 20 years of experience in the VLSI design and EDA industries. Swami has a B.S. in electrical engineering from Mangalore University in India and an M.S. in engineering management from Stanford University.
