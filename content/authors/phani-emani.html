Phani Emani

Phani Emani is an R&D Manager for the Digital IP Subsystems team at Synopsys, managing subsystems for PCIe, DDR, UFS, USB, and other protocols. Previously, Phani was part of the R&D teams for Verification IP and Implementation IP at Synopsys. With over 20 years of experience in the semiconductor industry, Phani holds a master’s degree in Microelectronics and VLSI Design from the Indian Institute of Technology, Madras.
