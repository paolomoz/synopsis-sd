Using Build root to Build an Embedded Linux System for DesignWare ARC Processors

By Mischa Jonker

Tags: Silicon IP Technical Bulletin, Silicon IP

Mischa Jonker

Mischa is a Senior Software Engineering Manager in the IP group at Synopsys, with over two decades of experience in the field of embedded software, specializing in heterogeneous computing. His work spans applications in imaging, computer vision, and AI, with a strong focus on low-level control code. After spending most of his career as an embedded software engineer, Mischa recently transitioned into management, where he continues to leverage his technical expertise to guide his team in developing robust software solutions. Mischa holds a master’s degree in Technical Computer Science from the University of Twente, The Netherlands.
