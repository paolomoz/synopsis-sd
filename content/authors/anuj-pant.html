Anuj Pant

Anuj Pant is a senior product manager with the EDA Group at Synopsys, where he is responsible for building the Synopsys Cloud platform. He has over 14 years of experience in the semiconductor industry in various technical and business roles across EDA software and IP focus areas. He has a bachelor of technology degree in electronics and communication engineering from Amity University and a graduate degree in embedded systems and VLSI design from CDAC Noida.
