Gert Goossens

Gert Goossens is an Executive Director of Engineering at Synopsys, where he is currently leading the company’s tool development group for Application-Specific Instruction-set Processors (ASIPs). Previously, he was a co-founder and the CEO of Target Compiler Technologies, the company that pioneered the concept of ASIP tools. Gert Goossens holds MS and Ph.D. degrees from KU Leuven, Belgium.
