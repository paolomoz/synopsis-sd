Charlie Matar

Charlie Matar is the senior vice president of engineering for the Synopsys Solutions Group. His technical background spans from product definition to SoC and IP development on advanced process technologies. He has a B.S. in electrical engineering in semiconductor physics from The University of Texas at Austin and an M.S. in electrical engineering in microelectronics from Southern Methodist University.
