Magaly Sandoval-Pichardo

Magaly Sandoval-Pichardo is Mechatronics engineer with 10 years of experience in the semiconductor industry. She is currently a Products and Solutions Program Manager for the Synopsys Interface IP portfolio. Her main area of interest is HPC IP, including high speed Ethernet and PCIe. Magaly holds an MS in Engineering Management (MSEM) from Tufts University and a degree in Mechatronics engineering from Costa Rica Institute of Technology. Before Synopsys, Magaly was an SoC Design Engineer for Intel from 2015 to 2021, where she delivered multiple DDR tape-outs in 14nm, 10nm, and 7nm designs.
