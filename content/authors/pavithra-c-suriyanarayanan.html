Pavithra C Suriyanarayanan

Pav C. Suriyanarayanan is a certified automotive expert with a strong background and experience in functional safety (ISO 26262) and cyber security (SAE/ISO 21434) across OEMs and all-tier suppliers. Pav has been a catalyst in achieving the industry-first automotive compliance for ARC processors and the Security IP portfolio at Synopsys. With a master’s degree in electrical engineering from The University of Texas, Pav’s refreshing approach has made her a sought-after speaker. She has authored multiple white papers and technical bulletins showcasing industry methodologies and sustainable processes for delivering high-quality automotive IPs at Synopsys.
