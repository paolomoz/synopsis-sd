Urmimala Roy

Urmimala Roy is Principal Technical Product Manager for Foundation IP at Synopsys, where she focuses on standard cell library IP, advanced process-node evaluation, and foundry enablement. With more than 10 years of experience in the semiconductor industry, she has held engineering roles at Intel, GLOBALFOUNDRIES, and TDK-Headway Technologies, spanning CMOS and MRAM compact modeling, PDK development and design enablement. Urmimala earned a Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from The University of Texas at Austin, preceded by a master's and bachelor's in electrical/electronics engineering.
