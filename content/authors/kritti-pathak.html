Kritti Pathak

Kritti Pathak is an advanced technology-node solutions architect for Synopsys’ Ecosystem team where she drives advanced node collaborations for lead customers. She has extensive experience in chip design (including mobile, networking, and HPC processors) and leading design methodology development (low-power, high speed memory access, high capacity). She has held senior engineering positions with Qualcomm and Cisco Systems and has presented advanced design solutions at multiple conferences, including DAC, SNUG, and OIP. Kritti holds a Bachelor’s degree from Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Delhi and MSEE from University of Wisconsin, Madison.
