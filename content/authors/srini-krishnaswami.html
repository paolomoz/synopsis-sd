Srini Krishnaswami

Srini Krishnaswami is the Director of Functional Safety for the ARC Processor Group at Synopsys IP, focusing on functional safety and cybersecurity for ARC processors. Srini has over 30 years of experience in fault-tolerant computing, processor and system architecture, and has hands-on experience with multiple successful SoC tapeouts. He holds a master’s degree in electrical engineering from Virginia Tech.
