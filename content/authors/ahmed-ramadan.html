Ahmed Ramadan

Ahmed Ramadan is a senior product management and engineering leader with more than 28 years of experience in the Electronic Design Automation (EDA) industry. He has built a distinguished career leading global product strategy, engineering, and business development initiatives for top semiconductor design tools, with deep expertise in analog circuit simulation, compact modeling, parasitic extraction, and custom IC design flows. Ahmed has held leadership roles at Synopsys and Siemens EDA/Mentor Graphics, where he has driven innovation, built high-performing global teams, and strengthened strategic partnerships with major semiconductor foundries.
