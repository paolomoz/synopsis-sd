Narasimha Babu G V L

Narasimha Babu GVL is a Synopsys Scientist with 18+ years of experience providing protocol verification solutions for a variety of standard protocols including Ethernet, AMBA, MIPI, UFS, PCIe, CXL and UCIe. Babu is a primary Synopsys representative for the Compute Express Link (CXL) Compliance Work Group and part of the DVCon India steering committee. He is also actively involved in writing and editing technical blog publications on Protocol verification.
