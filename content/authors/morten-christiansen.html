Morten Christiansen

Morten Christiansen is a senior staff technical product manager for USB and DisplayPort IP for the Synopsys Solutions Group. Morten has contributed to more than 35 USB, MIPI, and VESA/DisplayPort standards, as well as communication standards including WMC, EEM, NCM, and MBIM. In addition to the non-patented USB standards contributions, Morten holds seven international patents for other USB inventions. Morten has an M.S. degree from The Norwegian Institute of Technology.
