Understanding Advanced Interconnect IP Peripherals for IoT and Automotive SoCs

By Sreenath Panganamala, Sriram Balasubramanian, Nishith Sharma, John Swanson, Srinivasa Yelavatti, Pratap Neelashetty

Tags: Silicon IP Technical Bulletin, Internet of Things, Automotive, SoC Infrastructure IP, Silicon IP

Pratap Neelashetty

Pratap Neelashetty is a Manager at Synopsys IP, specializing in I3C, UniPro, and UFS controllers. With over 13 years of experience in ASIC digital design, Pratap has extensive expertise in the architectural and design development of various products, including AMBA, I2C, UFS, UniPro, and I3C. He is a recognized industry expert in the I3C protocol and has been consistently acknowledged as a MIPI Specification Author for I3C by the MIPI Alliance for his contributions to the development of the I3C Bus Specification and several allied specifications such as 'MIPI I3C HCI,' 'MIPI Touch (ALI3C),' and 'MIPI I3C-TCRI.' Pratap has filed two patents with the USPTO, one of which has been granted. He holds a master’s degree in VLSI from Manipal University.
