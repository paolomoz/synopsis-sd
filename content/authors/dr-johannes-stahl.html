Dr. Johannes Stahl

Dr. Johannes Stahl is executive director in the Synopsys Systems Design Group, where he is responsible for marketing of our hardware-assisted verification solutions. He has more than 25 years of industry experience, including technical, business and product management responsibilities for a wide range of tools and services for SoC design and software development. Johannes holds an M.S. and Ph.D in electrical engineering from the Technical University of Aachen in Germany
