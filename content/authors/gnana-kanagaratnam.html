Gnana Kanagaratnam

Gnana Kanagaratnam is an executive director of product management at Synopsys EDA, where he is responsible for physical verification products and solutions. He brings over 25 years of experience spanning engineering and leadership roles across field operations, technical sales and go to market, and product engineering, with a focus on both emerging and established EDA solutions. Gnana holds a B.S. degree in electrical engineering from the University of Rochester, New York.
