Marc Serughetti

Marc Serughetti is vice president of product management at Synopsys. He drives the strategy, definition and go-to-market of Synopsys Electronics Digital Twin Platform, virtualization and system validation products and ecosystems collaborations. His experience spans simulation technologies, compilers, IDE, debugging and analysis tools, embedded runtime software (operating systems, middleware and applications) and vertical market deployment (including automotive, industrial, aerospace/defense, consumer, and networking).
