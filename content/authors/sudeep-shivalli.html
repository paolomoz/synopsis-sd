Sudeep Shivalli

Sudeep Shivalli is regional senior director at Synopsys India, where he leads go-to-market sales teams and drives business growth across high-performance computing, edge AI and IoT, automotive, and semiconductor manufacturing. With nearly three decades of experience spanning semiconductor engineering, product strategy, and business leadership, he has held roles ranging from EDA product specialist to startup co-founder. He holds a bachelor’s degree in Instrumentation and Electronics from Mysore University and an international certification in product management.
