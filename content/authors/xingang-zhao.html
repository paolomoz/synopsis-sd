Xingang Zhao

Xingang Zhao serves as Technical Product Manager for Synopsys Cloud, where he drives the evaluation and integration of cutting-edge cloud services and tools on leading public cloud platforms to optimize the performance and cost-efficiency of EDA workloads. Prior to his current role, Xingang gained more than 20 years of experience as a product and validation engineer, contributing to the advancement of Synopsys digital implementation solutions. He holds a Bachelor of Science degree in Electrical Engineering from Zhejiang University and is a certified product management professional from the Kellogg School of Management.
