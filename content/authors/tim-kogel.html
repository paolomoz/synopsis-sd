Dr. Tim Kogel

Dr. Tim Kogel is a principal engineer for virtual prototyping in the Synopsys Systems Design Group. He received his Ph.D from RWTH Aachen in 2005 and has authored numerous publications on system-level modeling. Tim leads a team of applications engineering specialists, responsible for the definition, realization, and deployment of Synopsys virtual prototyping solutions.
