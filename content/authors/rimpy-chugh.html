Rimpy Chugh

Rimpy Chugh is a staff product manager in the EDA Group at Synopsys, where she is responsible for conceptualizing and driving product management and business development initiatives for various product lines and solutions to achieve target year-over-year growth across the globe. She has a bachelor of technology degree in electronics and communication engineering from YMCA University of Science and Technology and an MBA from the Indian Institute of Management, Bangalore.
