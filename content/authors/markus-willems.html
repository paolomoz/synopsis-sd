Markus Willems

Markus Willems is the senior product manager for DSP solutions for the Synopsys Solutions Group. He has worked in the EDA and computer industries for almost 30 years in a variety of senior positions. Markus received his Ph.D and an M.S. in electrical engineering from the Technical University of Aachen in Germany. He also holds an MBA from Hagen University.
