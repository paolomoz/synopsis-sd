Hany Elhak

Hany Elhak manages the product management teams for the custom design, circuit simulation, TCAD, and transistor-level signoff at Synopsys. He has more than 25 years of EDA experience spanning both technical and business responsibilities. Hany holds B.S. and M.S. degrees in electrical engineering from Ain Shams University, Cairo, and an MBA from the UC Berkeley Haas School of Business.
