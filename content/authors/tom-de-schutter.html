Tom De Schutter

Tom De Schutter is Senior Vice President of Product Management at Synopsys, leading system verification solutions including software-defined hardware-assisted verification and digital twin technologies for automotive, mobile, data center, and edge AI. He has 25+ years of experience, holds a master's degree from the University of Leuven, Belgium, and was the editor of the book, “Better Software. Faster!"
