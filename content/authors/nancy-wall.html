Synopsys Season of Service: Making an Impact in our Communities

By Nancy Wall

Tags: About Synopsys

Nancy Wall

Nancy Wall manages employee community engagement and social impact through the Synopsys for Good program. She oversees Synopsys Season of Service, the annual, month-long volunteer program that spans 20+ countries. Nancy previously managed global programs for Levi Strauss & Co. and VISA. She holds a BA from Bowling Green State University.
