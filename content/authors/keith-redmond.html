Keith Redmond

Keith Redmond is a Principal Engineer on the ASIC verification team at Synopsys. Keith has over 15 years of experience spanning ASIC verification and software. Most of his experience is in ASIC verification where he both oversaw and contributed to the success of numerous projects across several companies – from architectural definition through implementation and verification to lab bring-up and customer case debug. Keith has a BEng in Computer Engineering from McMaster University, and an MASc from the University of Toronto.
