Hiromitsu Fujii

Hiromitsu Fujii has dedicated over 30 years to the EDA/IP business at Synopsys Japan, where he has successfully led the Field Marketing team for two decades. Before joining Synopsys, he built a strong foundation in the electronics and CAD industries, gaining valuable experience in both sales and company management. Hiromitsu earned his bachelor’s degree in law from Meiji University’s Faculty of Law. Known for his approachable leadership style and commitment to innovation, Hiromitsu enjoys collaborating with colleagues and clients to drive success. Outside of work, he values spending time with his family and exploring new interests.
