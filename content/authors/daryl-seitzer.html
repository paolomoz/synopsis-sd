Daryl Seitzer

Daryl Seitzer is a Principal Product Manager for the Embedded Memories product line at Synopsys. With over 25 years of experience in the semiconductor industry, Daryl has held a wide range of engineering and marketing positions. His expertise spans chip design, product development, platform architecture, and solutions engineering, leading to his current role managing Synopsys Embedded Memory IP. Daryl holds a bachelor’s degree in electrical and computer engineering from Clarkson University and an MBA from the Vermont University.
