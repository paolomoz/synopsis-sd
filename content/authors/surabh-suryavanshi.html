Saurabh Suryavanshi

Saurabh Suryavanshi is a Product Manager in the Manufacturing Solutions group. His primary focus is on development of Digital Twin, ML, and GenAI technology to help semiconductor engineers improve productive, reduce R&D cost, and bring new technology to market in faster time. In previous roles, he has led R&D team to develop a new memory technology, and collaborated with design and systems engineers to develop technology roadmaps for high value problems in the computing industry. He is the recipient of gold medal for overall excellence as undergraduate student at IIT Bombay and holds a PhD degree in Electrical Engineering from Stanford University. He has contributed to over 5 patents, and 30 research articles and conferences with 1800 citations.
