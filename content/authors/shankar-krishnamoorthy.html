Shankar Krishnamoorthy

Shankar Krishnamoorthy is the Chief Product Development Officer of Synopsys, an industry-leading company delivering engineering solutions from silicon to systems powering the era of pervasive intelligence. Shankar oversees technology and product development organizations across multiple lines of Electronic Design Automation (EDA) including design, verification, emulation, prototyping, virtualization, signoff, test, TCAD, and manufacturing domains. With the acquisition of Ansys, Shankar also leads technology and product development in the simulation and analysis areas of Structures & Mechanical Analysis, Fluids, Thermals, Electronics, Optics, Digital and Mission Engineering.
