Vaida Arcisauskaite

Vaida Arcisauskaite is a Staff Engineer at Synopsys, specializing in atomistic materials modeling with over 18 years of experience in the field. She holds a PhD in Chemistry from the University of Copenhagen, Denmark. Prior to joining Synopsys in 2017, she was a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the University of Oxford. At Synopsys, Vaida supports customers in leveraging the latest and most advanced features of the QuantumATK atomic-scale modeling platform, including GPU acceleration, Machine-Learned techniques, and other cutting-edge simulation technologies. Vaida has authored more than 20 peer-reviewed publications with more than 400 citations in total.
