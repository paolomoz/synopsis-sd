Samskrut Konduru

Samskrut J. Konduru is senior director of product Management for hardware-assisted verification at Synopsys. He has over two decades of experience in silicon and systems design spanning various architecture, engineering, applications, product development, and product management roles. Samskrut has a master’s degree in electrical engineering from Villanova University and holds two U.S. patents.
