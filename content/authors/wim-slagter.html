Wim Slagter

Wim Slagter is Senior Director of Partnerships at Synopsys, where he leads partner programs and high-performance computing initiatives in the Corporate Strategy Office. He has more than 32 years of experience in the engineering simulation software industry, with leadership roles spanning software development, consulting, sales, and product management. Wim holds a PhD in Aerospace Engineering from Delft University of Technology in the Netherlands.
