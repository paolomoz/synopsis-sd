Krishna Balachandran

Krishna Balachandran is Senior Director of Product Management for the NVM IP product line at Synopsys. He is responsible for product and business strategy, marketing operations, product management, promotion, and business development. He has extensive experience in the EDA and semiconductor IP markets and has previously held roles in Product Marketing and Business Development for EDA tools and Semiconductor IP at Synopsys, Cadence, and Virage Logic. He holds a master’s degree in computer engineering from the University of Louisiana, Lafayette.
