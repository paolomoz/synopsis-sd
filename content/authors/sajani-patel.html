Sajani Patel

Sajani Patel is a principal product manager for the die-to-die IP portfolio at Synopsys. She has more than 30 years of experience in the mobile, telecommunications, and high-performance computing industries, with roles spanning product management, design, testing, supply chain, and technical leadership. Sajani holds a Bachelor of Science degree in Electrical and Computer Engineering from University of California, Irvine. She also holds a Master of Science degree in Electrical Engineering from University of California, San Diego.
