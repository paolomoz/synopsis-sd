Walter Wottreng

Walter Wottreng is the Vice President of Automotive Strategy and Business Development at Synopsys. He leads the company’s automotive strategy and drives growth across all product lines, serving as the business architect for future expansion and strategic portfolio enhancements. Walter oversees key initiatives such as optimizing E/E vehicle architecture designs for SoCs, accelerating software development for software-defined vehicles through digital twinning and virtualization, and ensuring the delivery of secure, safe, and compliant software applications.
