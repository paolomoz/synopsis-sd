How to Augment SoC Development to Conquer Your Design Hurdles

By Rita Horner, Praveen Durga

Tags: Design, About Synopsys, HPC, Data Center

Praveen Durga

Praveen Durga is a senior director in the Synopsys SoC Solutions Group. He has 24+ years of experience in managing SoC and IP design R&D teams, championing processes, methodologies, and quality with a proven track record of delivering multiple SoCs, subsystems,and IPs. Praveen holds a PhD in electronic engineering from the University of Bath, England, UK, and an executive MBA from the Faculty of Management Studies, University of Delhi, India. He has been awarded patents in functional safety for automotive SoC design.
