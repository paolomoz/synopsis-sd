Brian Davenport

Brian Davenport is a principal applications engineer for the Synopsys EDA Group, with expertise in platform operations and validation. Brian leads the Synopsys functional safety verification solution for automotive. Prior to this role, Brian was a co-founder of WinterLogic, acquired by Synopsys in 2016. He earned his degree from Buena Vista University.
