Taruna Reddy

Taruna Reddy is a staff product manager for the Synopsys EDA Group. After deploying semiconductor software for enterprise customers as an applications engineer for 15 years, Taruna switched gears to product marketing. Her verification and applications engineering background provide her with the technical depth and business expertise to build the messaging and positioning for the software products she currently brings to the market. She has a B.S. in electronics and communications from Osmania University in India and an M.S. in electrical engineering from Clemson University.
