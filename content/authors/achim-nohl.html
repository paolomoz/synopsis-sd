Achim Nohl

Achim Nohl is a Distinguished Architect at Synopsys, where he co-architects and develops the Synopsys Cloud platform. With over 25 years of experience spanning EDA, embedded systems, and cloud-native architecture, his career has evolved from processor design and prototyping all the way to SaaS platform development. He holds multiple patents in Embedded Processor Design Automation, was part of the founding team of LISATek in 2000.
