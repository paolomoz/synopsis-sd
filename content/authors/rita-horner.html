Rita Horner

Rita Horner is a product marketing director for the Synopsys Solutions Group, with more than 25 years of experience in mixed-signal circuit design, ESD design, and test and packaging of high-speed ICs for consumer, computing, and high-end networking products in a broad range of semiconductor process technologies. She holds an M.S. in electrical engineering from the University of Tennessee, and has a patent in IC packaging and contributions to a number of technical publications.
