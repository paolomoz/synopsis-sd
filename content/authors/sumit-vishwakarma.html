Sumit Vishwakarma

Sumit Vishwakarma has over 20 years of EDA & semiconductor industry experience, specializing in Analog and Digital Verification. He currently drives the Strategic, Product, and Solutions Marketing at GMC, Synopsys. Previously, at Siemens EDA, Sumit was the head of product management for the mixed-signal product line and managed marketing functions for AMS verification, focusing on circuit simulation, mixed-signal, and library characterization. Sumit has published extensively in IEEE, DesignCon, DAC, TSMC OIP, DVCon, SNUG, U2U, Semi-Engineering, and various tech articles and blogs. He holds an MS in Electrical Engineering from Arizona State University and a MSE Management certification from Stanford University. Sumit is also a passionate digital artist and enjoys teaching art to kids.
