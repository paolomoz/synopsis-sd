John Moors

John Moors started working for Philips Semiconductors in 1997 on PC monitors (the old analog ones with the big tubes) in the Systems Laboratory Eindhoven. Around 2001 he moved to Philips Semiconductors Reuse Technology Group doing RTL design and synthesis on Epics7b DSP, and later also did maintenance place and route on ARM and MIPS processors. John started working on ARC Processors when the department was bought by Virage Logic and he joined Synopsys in 2011 as a result of the company’s acquisition of Virage Logic. John holds a Bachelor of Science degree from Fontys Hogeschool Eindhoven (Polytechnics).
