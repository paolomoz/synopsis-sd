Tri Nguyen

Tri Nguyen is a Senior Manager for the USB IP Group at Synopsys, responsible for hardware and software validation for USB digital IP products. With over 18 years of experience in USB IPs, Tri has an extensive background in IP design, FPGA system design, and interface protocols. He holds a Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering from San Jose State University.
