Varun Agrawal

Varun Agrawal is a product manager for verification IP, virtual system adaptors, and protocol solutions for hardware-assisted platforms at Synopsys. He has over 14 years of experience in functional verification with expertise in simulation, emulation, and virtualization domains. He holds a BTech in electronics and communications from NIT Hamirpur, India, and an MBA in marketing from India Institute of Foreign Trade (IIFT), New Delhi, India.
