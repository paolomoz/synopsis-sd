Thomas Andersen

Thomas heads the AI and ML solutions group at Synopsys, where he focuses on developing new technologies in the AI and ML space to automate the future of chip design. He has more than 20 years of experience in the semiconductor and EDA industry. Thomas holds a master’s degree from the University of Stuttgart and a Ph.D. in computer engineering from the University of Kaiserslautern in Germany.
