Rick Furtner

Rick Furtner is a principal Field Applications engineer in the Synopsys IP Group, with expertise across many areas of embedded software development, SOC design and verification, IP integration, and system-level SOC design methodologies. Currently, he is responsible for helping customers integrate Synopsys RISC-V processors, VPX vector processors, and NPX6 AI accelerators into their hardware and software platforms.
