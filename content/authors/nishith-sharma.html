Nishith Sharma

Nishith Sharma is a Senior Staff Engineer for Synopsys IP, specializing in AMBA controllers and peripherals. With over 13 years of experience in the semiconductor industry, Nishith has an extensive background in digital logic design and functional safety aspects of the digital design process. He holds a bachelor’s degree in information and communication technology (ICT) Engineering from DAIICT University, Gandhinagar.
