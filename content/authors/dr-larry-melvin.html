Dr. Larry Melvin

Larry Melvin is Sr. Director of Technical Product Management at Synopsys, with a career that spans X‑ray lithography research, fab‑level photolithography engineering, and advanced OPC and modeling. His current work focuses on mask modeling, high‑NA EUV, AR/VR, stochastics, and next‑generation digital‑twin data flows that improve performance and yield. Larry’s current passion is developing technology to help chip design and manufacturing interact to improve yield, performance, and area.
