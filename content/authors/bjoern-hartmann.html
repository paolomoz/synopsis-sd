Bjoern Hartmann

Bjoern Hartmann is a Staff Software Engineer at Synopsys with extensive experience in the semiconductor industry. He holds a master's degree in electrical and computer engineering from RWTH Aachen University. At Synopsys, Bjoern specializes in developing and applying advanced formal verification methods to ensure the reliability and correctness of Application-Specific Instruction-Set Processors (ASIPs) using ASIP Designer. His strong technical background and expertise in formal methods make him a key contributor to innovative processor verification solutions, supporting customers in building high-performance, reliable embedded systems.
