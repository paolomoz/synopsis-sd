Mohammad Tanveer

Mohammad Tanveer is a principal applications engineer for Embedded Memories IP at Synopsys, bringing nearly 30 years of semiconductor industry experience and 15 years with the company. He specializes in technical support, pre-sales, ASIC design, verification, and timing analysis, and has a strong track record in managing customer relationships and leading complex projects. Mohammad holds a bachelor’s degree in electrical engineering from California State University.
