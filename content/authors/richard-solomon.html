Richard Solomon

Richard Solomon, Senior Staff Technical Product Manager at Synopsys, has over three decades of experience in developing next-generation interfaces. Richard has been a key contributor to the development of the PCI Express standard, from the early days of PCI and PCI-X, to the current generations of PCI Express specifications and implementations. With over 20 years on the PCI-SIG Board of Directors, Richard is a frequent presenter at PCI-SIG Developers Conferences and other industry events. Additionally, Richard is an active participant in the CXL Consortium and previously served on the UALink Board of Directors. He holds a Bachelor of Science in Electrical Engineering (BSEE) from Rice University and has been awarded 27 US patents. Richard's passion for high-speed interfaces continues to drive his contributions to the field.
