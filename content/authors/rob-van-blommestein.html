Rob van Blommestein

Rob van Blommestein is a senior staff product and solutions marketing manager at Synopsys. He has managed the Synopsys Cloud Verification Instance as well as the Synopsys Verdi debug system. Prior to Synopsys, he was the head of marketing for OneSpin Solutions, a formal verification technology company. He also worked to promote formal solutions as the director of corporate marketing for Jasper Design Systems and vice president of marketing for Oski Technology.
