Kiran Vittal

Kiran Vittal is the executive director of product management for the Synopsys Design-for-Test line of business in the Product Management and Markets Group. He has 25+ years of experience in EDA and semiconductor design. Prior to joining Synopsys, Kiran held product marketing, field applications and engineering positions at Atrenta, ViewLogic, and Mentor Graphics (now Siemens EDA). He holds a B.S. in electronics engineering from India and an MBA from Santa Clara University.
