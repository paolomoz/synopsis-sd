Neeraj Paliwal

Neeraj Paliwal is Senior Vice President for Silicon IP Product Management at Synopsys. He is responsible for the Synopsys complete Silicon IP portfolio for Interface, Foundation, Processor, and Security technologies. With more than 25 years of experience in the semiconductor industry, Neeraj brings deep expertise in System-on-Chip (SoC) development and hardware/software integration. His focus at Synopsys is to help customers by delivering industry-leading Silicon IP solutions with rapid time-to-market and product differentiation.
