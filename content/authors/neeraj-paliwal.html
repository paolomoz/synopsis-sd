Neeraj Paliwal is Senior Vice President for Silicon IP Product Management at Synopsys. With more than 25 years of experience in the semiconductor industry, Neeraj brings deep expertise in System-on-Chip (SoC) development and hardware/software integration. His focus at Synopsys is to help customers by delivering industry-leading Silicon IP solutions with rapid time-to-market and product differentiation.
