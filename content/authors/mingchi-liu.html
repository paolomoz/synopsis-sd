Mingchi Liu

Mingchi Liu is a Senior Technical Marketing Manager for Synopsys IP, focusing on embedded memory, TCAM, eMRAM, and ReRAM. With over 25 years of experience in the semiconductor industry, Mingchi has an extensive background in embedded memory and business development for EDA, IP, and IC. He holds several U.S. patents in the field of memory characterization. Mingchi has a master’s degree in electrical engineering from Stony Brook University.
