Geert-Jan Schrijen

Geert-Jan Schrijen is a senior R&D engineering manager at Synopsys, focusing on the development of security IP. As the former CTO of Intrinsic ID, he has an extensive background in the field of Physical Unclonable Functions (PUFs) and their application in embedded security architectures. Previously he worked in the security group of Philips Research in Eindhoven on topics such as digital rights management, low-power authentication protocols and private biometrics. Geert-Jan holds a Master's degree in Electrical Engineering from the University of Twente in the Netherlands.
