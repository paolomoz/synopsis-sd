Jamil Kawa

Jamil Kawa is a Synopsys Fellow in the Solutions Group at Synopsys, where he works on cold and cryo CMOS design, emerging memories and computational applications for advanced technology nodes, and IP development and architectures. Jamil holds over 40 patents and has authored over 60 papers, articles, and presentations. He received his M.B.A. at Santa Clara University, with his master’s degree and post-graduate work in electrical engineering at The University of Michigan, Ann Arbor.
