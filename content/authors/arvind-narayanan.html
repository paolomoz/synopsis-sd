Arvind Narayanan

Arvind Narayanan is a senior director of Product Line Management at Synopsys driving the digital implementation product portfolio including Fusion Compiler, ICCII & DSO.ai. He has been actively driving the Synopsys.ai initiative – application of AI across the entire EDA stack. Arvind has more than 25 years of industry experience in various capacities including digital design, product engineering and product marketing. Prior to Synopsys he worked at various EDA and semiconductor companies, including Mentor Graphics, Magma Design, and Hal Computer Systems. Arvind holds a Bachelors in EEE from University of Madras, a Master’s in ECE from Mississippi State University, and a Master’s in Business Administration from Duke University.
