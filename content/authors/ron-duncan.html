Ron Duncan

Ron Duncan is a Principal Manager in the Technology Strategy & New Horizons organization at Synopsys, bringing more than 30 years of technical and leadership experience across the semiconductor industry. He has represented Synopsys at international conferences and government research symposiums, presenting and publishing papers on a range of semiconductor technologies. Most recently, he has served as the lead technologist for EDA University Research Partnerships and Workforce Development. Ron holds degrees in Electrical Engineering from MIT and Cornell University.
