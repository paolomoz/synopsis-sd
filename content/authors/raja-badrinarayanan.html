Dr. Raja Badrinarayanan

Raja Badrinarayanan is a Senior Staff Applications Engineer specializing in digital twins, system simulation, and model-based engineering. He received his PhD from Nanyang Technological University (Singapore), where his research focused on battery systems and system simulation. He works with global teams and customers to develop, deploy, and scale digital twin solutions across a wide range of engineering applications.
