Shekhar Kapoor

Shekhar Kapoor is a senior director of product line management for the Synopsys EDA Group, responsible for multi-die system design, digital platform, and analytics solutions. He has over 20 years of professional experience managing the design realization and signoff products at Synopsys. Prior to that, he had leading design and application engineering roles at LSI Logic (now Broadcom) and IBM. Shekhar holds an M.S. in electrical and computer engineering from Virginia Tech and an MBA from the University of California, Berkeley.
