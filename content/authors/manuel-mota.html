Manuel Mota

Manuel Mota is a Principal Product Manager responsible for the die-to-die interface IP product line at Synopsys. Previously, he held various product management roles focusing on SerDes interfaces and analog/digital converters. Manuel has contributed to the industry's evolution towards multi-die designs by authoring multiple technical papers on the subject. Prior to joining Synopsys, Manuel held research, technical, and project management positions at CERN, Chipidea, and MIPS. He holds a PhD in Electronic Engineering from Lisbon Technical University.
