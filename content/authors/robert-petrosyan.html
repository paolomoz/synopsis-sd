Robert Petrosyan

Robert Petrosyan is a talent attraction brand content specialist on the People Team at Synopsys. Prior his full-time job, he had the privilege of interning at Synopsys, where he contributed as a brand and social media marketing intern on the People Team. His academic achievements include a dual degree from the University of Rochester, encompassing a B.S. in Business Marketing and a B.A. in Digital Media Studies. He further honed his expertise by pursuing an M.S. degree in Marketing Analytics at Simon Business School.
