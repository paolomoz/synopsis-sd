Ron Lowman

Ron Lowman joined Synopsys in 2014 and is currently a staff product manager for the Synopsys Solutions Group. Ron is responsible for driving the company’s AI market IP initiatives -- including strategic business and market trend analysis. Ron holds a B.S. in electrical engineering from Colorado School of Mines and an MBA from The University of Texas at Austin.
