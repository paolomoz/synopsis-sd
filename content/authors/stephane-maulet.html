Stephane Maulet

Stephane Maulet is a Senior Staff Applications Engineer at Synopsys. He has almost 20 years of experience in Synopsys front-end tools spanning Synopsys Design Compiler, Synopsys Fusion Compiler, Synopsys Formality and Synopsys Formality ECO. Stephane holds a master’s degree in Microelectronics Engineering from Grenoble INP, France.
