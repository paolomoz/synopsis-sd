Lam Trinh

Lam Trinh is a staff sales account manager at Synopsys, where he is responsible for emerging markets such as Vietnam, Pakistan, and Bangladesh. He works closely with start-ups in the IC design field to promote Synopsys technologies that help customers shorten time to market. Lam has been in the semiconductor industry for more than 20 years.
