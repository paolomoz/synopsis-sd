Rich Collins

Rich Collins is a director of product management for ARC processors and subsystems for the Synopsys Solutions Group. Rich has over 25 years of experience in embedded semiconductor R&D, product marketing, and business development -- including 17 years at Motorola/Freescale before joining Synopsys. Rich holds an MBA and a B.S. in electrical engineering from Duke University.
