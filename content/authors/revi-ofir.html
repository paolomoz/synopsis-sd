Revi Ofir

Revi Ofir serves as a Product Manager for ARC-V Processor IP at Synopsys. Before joining Synopsys, she worked as a Product Marketing Manager for desktop and entry-level laptop processors at Intel. Revi holds a BSc in Mechanical Engineering from the Technion – Israel Institute of Technology, and an MBA from the University of Arizona.
