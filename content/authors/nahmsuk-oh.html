Nahmsuk Oh

Nahmsuk joined Synopsys in 2001 and currently focuses on optimization and timing/power closure in Fusion Compiler. He also spent 15 years as an architect behind PrimeTime ECO, PrimeECO, and Aspen that later evolved into PrimeClosure. He earned a Bachelor’s in Electronics Engineering from Yonsei University in Korea, a Master’s and Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering and Ph.D. minor in Computer Science from Standford University, and holds 15 patents in optimization and ECO.
