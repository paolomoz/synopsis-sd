Deepak Kumar Lnu

Deepak Kumar Lnu is a Principal Engineer at Synopsys with over 16 years of experience in Verification IP development, specializing in PCIe, CXL, SDIO, and USB protocols. He is a recognized contributor to PCI-SIG for PCIe 6.0 and 7.0 specifications development and holds a BTech in Electronics and Communications from Delhi Technological University, New Delhi, India.
