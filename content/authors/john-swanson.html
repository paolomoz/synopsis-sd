John Swanson

John Swanson is a senior staff product manager for the Synopsys Solutions Group and has been working in IP since 1990 when he joined Logic Automation/Modeling, which was later acquired by Synopsys. Currently, he is the product line manager for the Ethernet family of IP cores and portfolio of datapath IP. John also manages IP reuse engagements and the associated IP reuse tools. He holds an engineering degree from the DeVry Institute of Technology.
