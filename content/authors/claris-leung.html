Claris Leung

Claris Leung brings extensive experience in the semiconductor industry and currently serves as a Product Marketing Manager at Synopsys Inc. In this role, she leads strategic marketing initiatives, including defining go-to-market strategies and driving product launches. Previously, Claris was a Product Manager at Intel Corporation, where she managed the full product lifecycle for server and embedded system SoC platforms. Earlier in her career, she worked in semiconductor manufacturing and yield, successfully increasing product yield and production efficiency through data-driven initiatives. Claris holds a BS in Chemistry/Materials Science from the University of California, Los Angeles, and an MS in Materials Science & Engineering from Portland State University.
