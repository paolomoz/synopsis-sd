Sassine Ghazi

Sassine Ghazi is President and Chief Executive Officer of Synopsys and serves on the company’s board of directors. Known for his deep customer relationships and passion for innovation, Ghazi has steered Synopsys to record revenue growth and profitability by driving ambitious business strategies and fostering a culture of agility, courage, excellence, and trust. He joined Synopsys in 1998 and was named CEO in January 2024.
