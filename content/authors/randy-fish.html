Randy Fish

Randy Fish is the director of product line management for the Silicon Lifecycle Management (SLM) family at Synopsys. Randy has over 30 years of experience in the EDA, IP and semiconductor industries with B.E.E. and M.S.E.E. degrees in electrical engineering from the Georgia Institute of Technology.
