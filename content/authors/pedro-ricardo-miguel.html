Pedro Ricardo Miguel

Pedro Ricardo Miguel is a Senior II ASIC Digital Design Engineer at Synopsys, working on MIPI CSI-2 and DSI-2 controllers. With over 20 years of experience in the semiconductor industry, Pedro has an extensive background in electronics and IC design. He began his career at Chipidea, where he primarily worked on digital signal processing for analog-to-digital and digital-to-analog converters before joining the MIPI team. Pedro holds a degree in Electrical Engineering from Faculdade de Ciências e Tecnologia da Universidade de Coimbra.
