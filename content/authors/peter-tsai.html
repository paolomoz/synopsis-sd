Peter Tsai

Peter is a Product Manager at Synopsys. Peter focusing on ESD and reliability signoff for advanced designs. Prior to Synopsys, Peter held technical and customer-facing roles at Cadence, Siemens EDA, and AUO, developing expertise in analog/mixed-signal design, physical verification, and signoff flows. Peter holds a master’s degree in Photonics Engineering from National Chiao-Tung University, Taiwan.
