Lauren Wu

Lauren Wu is a Staff Applications Engineer at Synopsys, boasting over 10 years of experience in design verification. Leveraging her extensive expertise, she focuses on enriching customer experience and developing comprehensive training materials and collateral. With a passion for technology and a commitment to excellence, Lauren is dedicated to delivering innovative solutions that empower Synopsys' clients in their design endeavors. Lauren holds a M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from University of Southern California.
