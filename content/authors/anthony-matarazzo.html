Anthony Matarazzo

Anthony Matarazzo is the Go-To-Market Leader for AI Solutions at Synopsys, focused on helping organizations accelerate the adoption of next-generation digital engineering and intelligent systems. Working at the intersection of AI, simulation, and high-performance computing, he develops strategies that connect emerging technologies to measurable business outcomes. His work is centered on advancing Physical and Industrial AI, with a particular focus on enabling data centers, digital infrastructure, and intelligent systems through physics-based modeling and AI-driven workflows. Anthony is recognized for building strategic partnerships and turning emerging technologies into real-world impact.
