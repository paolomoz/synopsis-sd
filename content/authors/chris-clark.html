Chris Clark is a senior staff manager for the Automotive Group at Synopsys. He uses his experience in management, information systems, and cybersecurity to help organizations effectively integrate meaningful security practices into their environments. Chris holds an M.S. in cybersecurity from the University of Maryland University College and has held numerous certifications throughout his career.
