Catherine Le Lan

Catherine's experience focuses on worldwide customer success management, encompassing technical support, training, projects, and team management. She rejoined Synopsys in 2022 to combine her passions for academia and industry, by establishing education and research partnerships with European universities. Catherine has a master's degree in Microelectronics from ESIEE Paris, and held roles as a Technical Project Manager and Team Manager at LSI-Logic, Synopsys, and Texas Instruments in France and the United States.
