Shruti Masaguppi

Shruti Masaguppi is an Application Engineer in the VC Formal AE team at Synopsys. She has been focused on VC Formal product engineering, training and assistance to customers as well as the Synopsys field teams deploying formal technology. Prior to joining Synopsys, she has worked on simulation and static technologies across multiple organizations. She has a MS in Microelectronics Engineering from BITS, Pilani.
