Avinash Palepu

Avinash Palepu is a senior staff product manager for Synopsys Formality®, Synopsys Formality ECO, and Synopsys VC LP™ products. Starting with Intel as a design engineer, he has held various ASIC design, applications engineering management and product marketing roles in the semiconductor design and EDA industries. Avinash holds a master’s degree in electrical engineering from Arizona State University.
