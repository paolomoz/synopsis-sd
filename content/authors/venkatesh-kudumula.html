Venkatesh Kudumula

Venkatesh Kudumula is a senior R&D engineering manager in the Synopsys Systems Design Group, based out of Bengaluru, India. With 17+ years of experience in functional verification, Venkatesh’s focus is on providing industry leading verification solutions for Arm based systems in client/mobile, automotive, HPC and data centre segments. His expertise includes Cache Coherency, Arm C2C protocols along with other AMBA protocols such as CHI, AXI/ACE and PCI Express. He holds master’s degree in VLIS Design from PSG College Of Technology – Coimbatore, and bachelor’s degree in Electronics and Communications Engineering from JNTU – Hyderabad.
