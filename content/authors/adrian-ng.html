Adrian Ng

Adrian Ng is the executive director for Synopsys in Southeast Asia and country manager for Singapore, Malaysia, and Vietnam. He has more than two decades of experience in IC design and electronic design automation (EDA). Adrian holds a Master of Business Administration and a Bachelor of Engineering in Electrical and Electronic Engineering, both from the University of Bradford (UK).
