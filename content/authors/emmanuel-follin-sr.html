Emmanuel Follin, Sr.

Emmanuel Follin is a senior product manager for Ansys, part of Synopsys, leading the product strategy for the Autonomous Vehicle simulation solutions. With over 20 years of experience in system simulation, Emmanuel plays a pivotal role in leading the company’s efforts in the ADAS and Autonomous Vehicle simulation domain, managing the AVxcelerate product, and advancing scalable simulation solutions, including HiL, SiL, and DiL validation technologies.​ Prior to joining Ansys, Emmanuel built a strong foundation in Driver-in-the-Loop (DiL) simulation during his career at OKTAL, a leading simulation company. He then moved to Atos, where he transitioned into management, leading a large development project for Hardware-in-the-Loop (HiL) management solutions designed for Airbus Helicopters. With extensive multi-industry experience spanning Automotive and Aeronautics, Emmanuel combines technical expertise with strategic vision, delivering innovative simulation solutions that shape the future of autonomous systems.​
