Archana Varanasi

Archana Varanasi is a Senior Staff Program Manager at Synopsys, focusing on cloud enablement initiatives that expand customer access to EDA technologies. Archana drives cross-functional programs that accelerate EDA platform adoption, customer evaluations, and technical training. Her work includes advancing AI-enabled EDA, expanding access to semiconductor technologies, and developing future talent. Archana has more than 15 years of experience in EDA and physical design implementation. She holds a Master of Science in Computer Engineering from the Rochester Institute of Technology and a bachelor's degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University (JNTU), India.
