Khalid Khan

Khalid Khan is a Senior Staff Application Engineer with Synopsys System Design Group based out of Synopsys HQ in Sunnyvale, CA. He has 20+ years of experience in FPGA Synthesis, Verification and ASIC Prototyping fields. He is currently the overall lead for the Synplify FPGA synthesis product for field application engineering team in helping define the product road map and strategy. He also helps support many of the major FPGA Synthesis and Prototyping customers in the Silicon Valley region.
