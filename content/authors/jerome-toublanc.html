Jerome Toublanc

Jerome has over 20 years of semiconductor industry experience, working for various EDA companies such as Avanti, Synopsys, and Apache. He has contributed to the successes of worldwide customers in roles ranging from Application Engineer to Product Management and has covered the chip development flow from RTL to GDSII. Jerome is now a Business Development executive for Ansys. He aims to identify emerging multiphysics integrity challenges within the semiconductor industry and develop product solutions for the complete Chip-Package-PCB ecosystem.
