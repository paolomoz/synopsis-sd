Varun Shah

Varun Shah is on the product management team for Synopsys Cloud, responsible for the technology roadmap, cloud enablement of EDA software, and voice-of-customer analysis. Prior to joining the cloud team, Varun was an application engineer supporting ASIC verification software for more than a decade. Varun holds a master's degree in electrical engineering from the University of Missouri–Rolla and is a certified product management professional from the Kellogg School of Management.
