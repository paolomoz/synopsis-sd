Ann Keffer

Ann Keffer is a director in the Synopsys Systems Design Group, where she is responsible for marketing hardware-assisted verification solutions. She holds an undergraduate degree in computer science and mathematics and began her career as a software developer in Hewlett-Packard’s proprietary operating systems division. She later advanced through application engineering and support, product management, and executive leadership roles across multiple HP divisions. Since then, Ann has led global product management and marketing for companies in the automation and robotics industries, as well as for electronic design automation companies focused on functional safety solutions.
