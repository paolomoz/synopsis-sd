Patrick Verbist

Patrick Verbist is a senior staff product manager for ASIP designer tools for the Synopsys Solutions Group. Previously, he was business development manager and field application engineer for the ASIP designer tools and, prior to the acquisition by Synopsys in 2014, director of sales at Target Compiler Technologies. He holds a master’s degree in electrical engineering from KU Leuven in Belgium.
