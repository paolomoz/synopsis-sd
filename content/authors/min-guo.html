Min Guo

Min Guo is a Principal Application Engineer at Synopsys responsible for driving key technical evaluations and support for circuit analysis and simulations together with the Synopsys Go-To-Market (GTM) team. He has 20+ years of experience in spice. Before Synopsys, he worked for Motorola and Epson R&D as a mixed-signal designer.
