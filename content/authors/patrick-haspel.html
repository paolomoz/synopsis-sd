Patrick Haspel

Patrick Haspel, Ph.D., is global director of Synopsys Academic & Research Alliances. He has 20 years of experience designing and leading collaborative academia-corporate programs in complex, competitive, matrixed environments. Patrick earned his doctorate degree in computer architecture and microelectronic design systems from the University of Mannheim.
