faq

What does Synopsys' acquisition of Ansys mean for customers?

With Ansys now part of Synopsys, we are combining leaders in silicon design, IP and simulation and analysis enabling customers to rapidly innovate AI-powered products. Together, we’re best positioned to deliver new holistic system design solutions for customers in industries spanning semiconductors, high-tech, automotive, aerospace, industrial and more.

How quickly will you integrate the Ansys business and how does this intercept your technology roadmap?

Together, we can maximize the capabilities of product R&D teams broadly enabling them to rapidly innovate AI-powered products. As one company, we’re best positioned to deliver new, holistic system design solutions for customers in industries spanning semiconductors, high-tech, automotive, aerospace, industrial, and more.

We’re fast-tracking the development and delivery of holistic engineering solutions from silicon to systems to rapidly enable the next generation of AI-powered products. We expect to deliver our first set of integrated capabilities in the first half of 2026 that fuse multiphysics across the full EDA stack, including for multi-die chip packaging. Our combined roadmap also includes integrated solutions to advance testing and virtualization of complex, intelligent systems for automotive and other industries.

I am an Ansys customer. Who is my sales contact for the combined company?

The majority of sales contacts will remain the same at close. Any changes will be communicated directly and proactively. Our goal is to ensure continuity, minimize disruption, and continue delivering the high level of support you expect.

I am a Synopsys and Ansys semiconductor customer. Where should I go for support?

No change. Technical support requests should be submitted through your current support portal. Each has links to the direct support site for your current products.

ACSS is still available to support any questions on Ansys products. SolvNetPlus is still available to support any questions on Synopsys products. All existing documentation remains available.
