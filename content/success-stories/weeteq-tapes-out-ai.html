weeteq Tapes Out AI Enabled Ultra Edge Chip in 6 Months on Synopsys Cloud SaaS Platform

Discover how weeteq taped out an AI enabled embedded chip within 6 months. Learn more about the challenges and results they were able to achieve using Synopsys solutions.

What You'll Discover:

  • How weeteq achieved substantial area optimization compared to original design specification
  • How weeteq was able to shorten development time by 50%
  • How weeteq achieved concept to working silicon in just 6 months with turnkey SaaS environment that removed all the barriers to engineering productivity

In addition, we used Synopsys foundry sponsored IP at 22nm providing frontend to backend views, enabling seamless and fast integration of IP blocks in our design thereby shortening the overall development time by 50%. Martin MacDonald, COO ,weeteq
