Fujitsu Develops Next-Generation Processor for FugakuNEXT Supercomputer

Fujitsu leveraged Synopsys ZeBu® emulation technology and the Arm Architecture Compliance Suite (ACS) to perform comprehensive pre-silicon Arm SystemReady compliance verification for its next-generation FUJITSU-MONAKA® processor, the foundation of the future FugakuNEXT supercomputer.

By shifting compliance testing and software validation ahead of silicon availability, Fujitsu executed approximately 120 billion emulation cycles, identified critical system-level issues before tape-out, and improved confidence in software readiness, interoperability, and overall platform compliance.

​

What You Will Learn

  • How Fujitsu used Synopsys ZeBu Server 5 and Arm ACS to perform large-scale pre-silicon Arm SystemReady compliance verification for a next-generation HPC processor.
  • Best practices for identifying and resolving hardware, firmware, operating system, and PCIe interoperability issues before silicon fabrication.
  • How hardware-assisted verification accelerates debug, expands compliance coverage, and reduces development risk while improving software ecosystem readiness.
