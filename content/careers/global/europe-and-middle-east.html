Europe & Middle East Careers

Apply Now

Synopsys Europe and Middle East established its presence in Munich and Reading in 1990. Today, we have more than 40 offices in 17 countries in Europe and the Middle East focused on selling and supporting Synopsys' extensive range of solutions to all the major European Electronic companies. With the recent acquisition of Black Duck and Coverity, we have increased our focus on Software Integrity and Security by bringing to market the industry’s most advanced automated tools.

Our Europe and Middle East employees are an integral part of our global market, technology and community leadership. Several hundred engineers are working on the design and development of key technologies and products that accelerate innovation in the global electronics markets. Our people and values – Integrity, Customer Success through Execution Excellence, Leadership and Passion – make Synopsys Europe and Middle East a great place to accelerate your career!
