Mumbai Careers

Synopsys Mumbai is more like a family than an office.

This site is known for its employee commitment, great time management, and the fittest employees in health and wellness competitions. We consistently have CSR activities run by employees that promote education for needy children with Teach for India. Our Mumbai team raised 45,000 INR for 65 students from Municipal School to visit Kidzania to explore, challenge themselves, and open their minds to new learning experiences.

Whether it's new product launches or holidays like Independence Day, Republic Day, Holi (the festival of colors), Women's Day, Kids' Day, Navratri Utsav (a traditional festival), or Daan Utsav (joy of giving week), Synopsys Mumbai brings joy to the community by celebrating employees.
