NCR Careers

Did you know that Synopsys NCR houses over 700 of our employees and is our second largest site in South Asia? Read on to learn more about this region and the great work being done there!

In 2003, Synopsys opened its first office in the National Capital Region (NCR). Synopsys started with just a team of Field Engineers in NCR, and since then has continued to grow. This has occurred organically, as well as with strategic acquisitions like nSys in 2011, Magma in 2012, and the Asset Purchase from ST Microelectronics and Atrenta in 2015. Today, we have five offices in Noida and one in Delhi.

Our presence in NCR offers us an excellent opportunity to attract talent from some of India’s best engineering schools. Over the last few years, NCR has become the center of excellence (CoE) for Virtual Prototyping, VIP, Emulation, Simulation, Embedded Memories, and evolving High Speed Serial interface PHY IPs. Amazing work is being done on a variety of innovative technologies, and our customer base has also expanded in the region to include Qualcomm, NXP, and ARM.

Our NCR operations in South Asia are significant because of the region's engineering development, customer proximity, diversity in merged entities, and stellar talent pool from various local universities. As a result, we are more competitive, agile, and responsive to the needs of our customers.

Dr. Pradip Dutta | Group Vice President & Managing Director – South Asia
