Bangalore Careers

Often called the "Silicon Valley of India," Bangalore is the perfect place to work.

The city got its name thanks to the large concentration of software and high tech companies that set up their R&D centers in the area. The establishment and success of high tech firms in Bangalore has led to the growth of IT in India. IT firms in Bangalore employ about 35% of India's pool of 10 lakh +(1 million) IT professionals and account for the highest IT-related exports in the country. Bangalore also has a significant number of good engineering schools that feed the IT Industry. This cosmopolitan city is transforming into an e-city with numerous software and IT companies flooding it with their respective offices. 35 to 45% of India's IT talent works in Bangalore and generates a massive chunk of India's overall GDP.

Ease of talent acquisition, excellent weather, cosmopolitan outlook, easy-going attitudes, and the hospitality of the people of Bangalore makes it a great place to live and work.
