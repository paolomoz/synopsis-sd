Israel Careers

This office was established in 1997 in Herzeliya, Israel at the heart of the high tech industry. Israel is a hub for innovation and a great place for skilled and experienced engineers to build their careers.

Our technical teams are exposed to vast and different design projects from various leading markets such as CPUs, network processors, AI processors, smart sensors and sophisticated solutions for autonomous cars. The high pace of innovation, combined with growing complexity, ensures the work of our employees is always at the cutting edge of technology. Our commitment to the success of our customers and the growing cybersecurity market ensures a bright future for this office.
