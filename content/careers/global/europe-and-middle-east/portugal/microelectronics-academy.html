Course Benefits

Course Benefits

​

You will have an opportunity to get hands on training from company and world-class experts on topics ranging from Scripting Languages to Analog and Digital IC Design.

Scholarship for training

Privileged path to become Synopsys Engineer with a permanent contract.

Requirements

Requirements

​

Minimum bachelor’s degree in engineering or Sciences (or in final year) with fundamental knowledge of electronics.

Course Outline

Course Outline

​

- IC Design Introduction;

- Operating Systems;

- Scripting Languages;

- Analog and Mixed Signal IC Design;

- Custom IC Design Flow and EDA Tools;

- Digital IC Design;

- Digital IC Design Flow and EDA Tools;

- Final Project;

​

Duration and Location

Duration and Location

​

Total time of course: 320h (theorical & practical training) – 8 weeks

Location: Lisbon or Porto (on-site)
