Who qualifies as an intern?

Undergraduate or graduate students (Masters or PhD) who are enrolled at least part time at an accredited college or university are qualified for internships at Synopsys. Typically, interns have reached at least their third year or achieved junior status in an undergraduate program or equivalent before being considered.

Where are internships located?

Internship opportunities are offered in many Synopsys offices worldwide. We have various offices in North America, Europe, Asia-Pac, Japan, and South Asia.

What positions are available?

Engineering students may find opportunities in the areas of Design Verification, Compiler Development, IC Design, FPGA/AMSG, Static Analysis, Machine Learning, Digital Design, and Full Stack Development. Our Infrastructure groups may offer opportunities in areas such as Information Technology, Enterprise Information Systems, Finance, Legal, Marketing, and People and Places.

How long is an internship?

The duration of the internship will vary based on the location of the team and business need. Those details should be included in the internship description.

What benefits do interns receive?

Interns can expect to receive the following for their work at Synopsys:

  • Competitive salary
  • Holiday pay
  • Real world work experience and research opportunities
  • Exposure to EDA and semiconductor IP.

Opportunity to work with engineering and business experts, Synopsys executives, and community leaders
