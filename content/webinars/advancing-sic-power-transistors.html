Advancing SiC Power Transistors: Design and Market Trends

Silicon Carbide (SiC) technology is revolutionizing power electronics, offering superior efficiency, higher voltage operation, and improved thermal performance. This webinar brings together industry experts to explore key advancements in SiC power transistor design and market trends.

  • Topic 1: SiC Power Transistor Design and Optimization: Unit Cell, Edge Termination and Outlook for Chip-Level Electro-thermal Simulation. Speaker: Vinay Kumar Dasarapu, Synopsys
  • Topic 2: SiC Power Transistor Market, Applications and Technology Roadmap. Speaker: Shela Aboud, Synopsys

Principal Product Manager, Synopsys

Vinay Kumar Dasarapu

Principal Applications Engineer, Synopsys

Vinay Dasarapu received the M. Tech. and the Ph. D. degrees from the Indian Institute of Technology, Bombay, in 2001 and 2005, respectively. His main interests are in the areas of semiconductor device modelling and simulation of advanced logic and high-power semiconductor devices. Experienced in developing optimized process, device, and circuit simulation flows for RF and High-Power Energy efficient systems using wide bandgap semiconductors. Skilled in building IoT/DeepLearning based smart products and worked on several projects developing large-scale machine learning models for complex image detection and NLP problems for embedded-system platforms (ARM-v7/v8) / Edge Computing.
