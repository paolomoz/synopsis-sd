In-Field RAS Challenges with Chiplets and AI-based Systems

Date: May 05, 2025 | 10:00 AM PDT

Featured Speakers:

  • Jyotika Athavale, Director, Engineering Architecture, Synopsys
  • Yervant Zorian, Chief Architect & Fellow, Synopsys

Abstract:

Advancements in data center and automotive System-on-Chips (SoCs) to meet AI workload demands have resulted in the increased adoption of emerging technology nodes and chiplet packages. This presentation will explore the resiliency challenges faced by these critical systems. Additionally, it will cover the optimization of safety-critical system health using prognostics and silicon lifecycle management (SLM) solutions, which enhance quality and yield while addressing aging and degradation challenges for improved predictive maintenance and overall dependability.

AI-based components introduce new risks concerning data center availability and automotive functional safety. To address these growing challenges, there has been a significant effort in developing new standards for dependable computing. This Synopsys webinar will also discuss the latest topics being addressed in recent international standardization efforts related to silicon health across key application domains.

Jyotika Athavale

Director, Engineering Architecture, Synopsys

Jyotika is the Director of Engineering Architecture at Synopsys, specializing in dependable technologies. With prior roles at Intel and NVIDIA, she has received multiple awards for her contributions to women in tech and leadership in functional safety standards. Additionally, she has authored patents and technical publications, and chairs the IEEE P2851 standards on functional safety interoperability.

Yervant Zorian

Chief Architect & Fellow, Synopsys

Dr. Zorian is the Chief Architect & Fellow at Synopsys, with a distinguished background including roles at AT&T Bell Laboratories and Virage Logic. He holds multiple degrees, including a PhD in Electrical Engineering and an MBA, and currently serves as President of the IEEE Test Technology Technical Council. A prolific inventor with 45 US patents and over 400 published papers, he has received numerous awards, including the IEEE Lifetime Contribution Medal in 2022.
