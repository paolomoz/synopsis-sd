NoC Design and Platform Integration for Early Benchmarking of Automotive Processor SoCs

Automotive SoC teams face a critical challenge: validating performance and design parameters before silicon—without accurate benchmarking, costly respins and delayed time-to-market become inevitable.

ST demonstrates their proven architectural exploration flow for the StellarApp SoC, leveraging Platform Architect and LPDDR5 models to enable fast, multi-abstraction simulation with third-party IP integration and comprehensive HW/SW analysis.

What You'll Learn:

  • Assemble and integrate multi-abstraction models from ARM, Arteris, and other IP vendors
  • Accelerate design exploration through rapid parameter sweeping and simulation cycles
  • Analyze SoC performance from both hardware and software perspectives simultaneously
  • Validate automotive benchmarks using accurate LPDDR5 memory controller models
  • Eliminate late-stage design risks through early architectural validation
