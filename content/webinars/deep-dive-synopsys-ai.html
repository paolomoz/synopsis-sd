Deep Dive into Synopsys.ai

Synopsys Webinar

Join us for this deep dive into Synopsys.ai, our AI-driven Full-Stack EDA suite, that delivers significant quality of results (QoR) and productivity improvements across the entire stack. The Suite’s sustained differentiation to the chip design process includes comprehensive AI-driven design optimization, data analytics and generative AI capabilities. As the pioneer in AI-driven design, Synopsys.ai accelerates the chip design workflow by enabling companies to build more chips faster in the face of systemic complexity, shrinking talent shortages, and increased industry demands.
