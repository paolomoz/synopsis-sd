MediaTek Leverages Synopsys VDK to Shift SoC Development Left into Pre silicon Stage

As automotive platforms become increasingly software-defined, development teams need to begin software creation, validation, and optimization long before silicon is available. MediaTek presents how its Synopsys Virtualizer Development Kit (VDK) and virtual SoC enable a software-first methodology that accelerates development timelines, improves software quality, and helps bring next-generation ADAS and autonomous driving platforms to market faster.


What You Will Learn

  • How MediaTek enables software-first automotive SoC development by moving software development, validation, safety analysis, and performance optimization into the pre-silicon phase.
  • How a unified virtual platform improves efficiency across ADAS, cockpit, safety, and security domains, enabling software teams, customers, and ecosystem partners to begin development up to 12 months earlier.
  • How VDK accelerates software-defined vehicle innovation through software reuse, early safety and security verification, accurate pre-silicon performance analysis, and reduced hardware/software integration cycles.
