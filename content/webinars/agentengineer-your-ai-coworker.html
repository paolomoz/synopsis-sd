Meet Synopsys AgentEngineer: Your AI Coworker

Featured Speakers:

  • Anand Thiruvengadam, Senior Director, Product Management, AI, Synopsys

Why You Should Attend:

  • Explore Next-Generation AI Workflows: Discover Synopsys’ industry-first Level 4 (L4) agentic AI workflow for chip design and verification, powered by AgentEngineer™ technology.
  • Experience End-to-End Automation: Learn how this adaptive, multi-agent system manages the entire front-end design process—from natural language specifications to RTL generation, lint checking, and verification—without requiring engineers to write code.
  • Benefit from Collaborative Intelligence: See how AgentEngineer leverages collaborative, learning agents to automate and optimize complex chip design tasks, while integrating human-in-the-loop checkpoints for accuracy and oversight.
  • Achieve Breakthrough Productivity: Review early customer results showing up to a 2X productivity boost, with select cases reaching 5X improvements and significant reductions in verification time.
  • Accelerate Innovation in Chip Design: Understand how AgentEngineer, developed with industry leaders and built for interoperability, is transforming workflows and empowering engineering teams to drive innovation and redefine productivity in semiconductor design.
