Benefits of Early Architecture Design for Multi-Die Systems

Having an early view of your multi-die system architecture offers a myriad of benefits from die partitioning to system optimization. As such you can enable HW/SW co-development and co-optimization, e.g. how to best map functions and data into processing and memory resources across multiple dies.

Find out how to build a virtual prototype of your multi-die architecture to analyze and optimize your system for performance and power at the pre-RTL architecture specification phase, when design decisions have the highest impact.
