Accelerating SDV Development with Virtualization Tools and Technology

To accelerate the automotive industry’s shift toward Software-Defined Vehicles (SDVs), developers use Virtual Electronic Control Units (vECUs) to decouple software development from hardware development.

This presentation demonstrates how Synopsys virtualization and co-simulation techniques integrate multiple vECUs into an SDV domain, acting as a steppingstone to a complete Electronics Digital Twin (eDT).

By shifting validation left, OEMs achieve early software bring-up and bug discovery, ultimately saving 5–8 months of development time and significant physical hardware costs
