Keynote: Re-Engineering the Future

Watch Synopsys President and CEO Sassine Ghazi outline his vision for the future of engineering in an era defined by AI and the convergence of silicon, software, systems, and physics. Featuring an engaging discussion with NVIDIA founder and CEO Jensen Huang, along with insights from other industry leaders on AI-powered innovation, co-design, digital twins, and the technologies shaping the next decade.

Why You Should Watch This Session:

  • Why the convergence of AI, silicon, software, systems, and physics is transforming engineering.
  • How AI-powered copilots and emerging agentic AI technologies are accelerating innovation and engineer productivity.
  • Why silicon-to-systems co-design is critical for developing next-generation intelligent systems.
  • How digital twins and multiphysics solutions are helping organizations manage complexity and bring products to market faster.
  • Perspectives from industry leaders, including NVIDIA, on the future of AI-driven engineering.
