Meet Synopsys.ai Copilot - Your AI-Powered Design Assistant

Featured Speakers:

  • Koshi Wang, Principal Engineer Machine Learning, Synopsys
  • Vishnu Varre, Director, Intel

Synopsys.ai Copilot brings agentic AI into digital implementation to help engineers analyze, debug, and close designs faster—especially during late-stage timing closure and ECO. In this session, you’ll see how an AI-powered design assistant can interpret design context and results, prioritize violations, and guide next-best actions to accelerate convergence. Copilot also helps reduce repetitive effort by assisting with common closure tasks such as report triage, root-cause isolation, ECO strategy selection, and documenting changes for review and reuse. The outcome is fewer iterations, faster turnaround, and more consistent closure quality—while keeping engineers in control of decisions.

Highlights

  • Agent-assisted analysis and debug to quickly pinpoint root causes and prioritize what to fix first
  • ECO acceleration through guided fix strategies, iterative validation, and reduced manual triage
  • Faster, more predictable closure with reusable best practices and improved workflow consistency
