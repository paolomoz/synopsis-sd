Opportunities for Innovation in AI-Powered Chip Design

AI is transforming the landscape of chip design, unlocking new possibilities for efficiency, performance, and creativity. This panel brings together thought leaders and innovators from across the semiconductor industry to explore the opportunities for innovation in AI-powered chip design. Panelists discuss how artificial intelligence is driving breakthroughs in design automation, verification, optimization, and system-level integration. The session highlights practical strategies, emerging technologies, and collaborative approaches that are enabling teams to overcome traditional barriers and accelerate time-to-market with AI.

Highlights:

  • Where AI is delivering the biggest impact today across design, verification, and optimization
  • Practical strategies and emerging technologies to adopt AI in real chip design workflows
  • Collaboration approaches to overcome adoption barriers and accelerate time-to-market
