Designing for RISC-V Success: Addressing the Triple Threat in ASSP Development

SoC complexity is outpacing traditional development workflows, forcing teams to choose between meeting performance targets and staying on schedule. Without pre-silicon workload analysis, hardware/software co-design decisions become guesswork—risking costly respins and missed market windows.

MIPS Atlas Explorer eliminates this risk by delivering instruction-accurate simulation and microarchitectural modeling before RTL. Evaluate RISC-V processor options, validate application performance early, and optimize full-stack architectures with secure, containerized environments that protect proprietary IP while enabling distributed collaboration.

What You'll Learn:

  • Accelerate hardware/software co-design with pre-RTL workload performance analysis
  • Compare RISC-V processor architectures using detailed execution data and real benchmarks
  • Validate performance targets early through microarchitectural simulation and modeling
  • Optimize system resources without exposing proprietary software or design details
  • De-risk ASSP development with specification-driven modeling and reproducible workflows
