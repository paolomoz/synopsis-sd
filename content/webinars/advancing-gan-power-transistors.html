Advancing GaN Power Transistors: Market Trends & Design Challenges

GaN power transistors are transforming the power electronics industry with their superior efficiency and performance. Join us for an insightful webinar where industry experts will explore the latest market trends, key applications, and future roadmap of GaN technology. We will then dive into critical design challenges, including trapping effects, dynamic Rds(on), characterization, and compact modeling.

  • Topic 1: GaN Power Transistor Market, Applications, and Technology Roadmap. Speaker: Shela Aboud, Principal, Product Management, Synopsys
  • Topic 2: GaN Power Transistor Design & Optimization: Trapping Effects, Dynamic Rds(on), and Compact Modeling. Speaker: Vinay Kumar Dasarapu, Principal Engineer, Applications Engineering, Synopsys

Shela Aboud

Principal Product Manager, Synopsys

Shela Aboud received an M.S. and Ph. D. degree in Electrical Engineering from Arizona State University. She has extensive experience in solid state physics, semiconductor device simulations and atomistic materials modeling. She has spent more than 10 years at Synopsys. First as an application engineer supporting advanced logic, RF, and Power devices more recently as a Product Manager in TCAD.

Vinay Kumar Dasarapu

Principal Applications Engineer, Synopsys

Vinay Dasarapu received the M. Tech. and the Ph. D. degrees from the Indian Institute of Technology, Bombay, in 2001 and 2005, respectively. His main interests are in the areas of semiconductor device modelling and simulation of advanced logic and high-power semiconductor devices. Experienced in developing optimized process, device, and circuit simulation flows for RF and High-Power Energy efficient systems using wide bandgap semiconductors. Skilled in building IoT/DeepLearning based smart products and worked on several projects developing large-scale machine learning models for complex image detection and NLP problems for embedded-system platforms (ARM-v7/v8) / Edge Computing.
