Featured Speakers:

  • Ian Cutress, Chief Analyst, More than Moore
  • Manoj Selva, Vice President and General Manager, Intel
  • John Martinis, 2025 Nobel Prize Winner for Physics in Quantum, Qolab
  • Vamsi Boppana, Senior Vice President of AI Group, AMD
  • Prith Banerjee, Senior Vice President of Innovation Group, Synopsys

Why You Should Watch This Session:

As AI workloads continue to push the limits of traditional computing, innovation now depends on more than faster processors. From advanced chip architectures and high-performance software to AI-driven engineering and emerging quantum technologies, industry leaders are rethinking how compute is designed, scaled, and optimized. Join experts from Synopsys, AMD, Intel, and quantum computing pioneer John Martinis as they discuss the technologies, challenges, and opportunities shaping the next generation of AI infrastructure and accelerated innovation.
