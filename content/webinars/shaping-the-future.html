Shaping the Future of Software-Driven Mobility Platforms

Date: Jul 10, 2025 | 9:00 AM PDT

Cars are becoming smarter and more personalized thanks to software. To deliver the features people want in the next decade, companies need large-scale innovation, driven by research and industry demand. Having the right support systems is crucial to turn new technology into actual car features.

​

Key Takeaways:

  • Cars are moving from being mainly mechanical to software-driven machines.
  • Future features will require large-scale innovation.
  • Innovation is influenced by both research and industry needs.
  • A strong support ecosystem is needed to bring new technologies into real vehicles.
