Scaling SDV Development with Virtualization

Date: Jun 08, 2026 | 9:00 AM PST

As the automotive industry shifts toward software-defined vehicles (SDVs), integrating silicon, electronics, software, and vehicle systems is becoming increasingly complex.

This webinar explores how virtualization and end-to-end engineering solutions help automakers accelerate development, improve validation, and enable continuous innovation. Learn how to bridge system design with software development, streamline testing across hardware variants, and deliver high-performance, scalable SDV platforms faster.

Why You Should Attend:

  • Understand SDV Development Challenges: Learn about the growing complexity of integrating hardware, software, and systems in modern vehicle development.
  • Explore End-to-End Engineering Solutions: Discover how a complete development approach supports SDVs, from architecture and design to validation and deployment.
  • Leverage Virtualization for Faster Development: See how virtualization enables scalable testing, continuous validation, and faster software iteration across hardware environments.
  • Accelerate Innovation with Continuous Integration: Find out how collaborative platforms and digital models enable ongoing updates and improved performance throughout the vehicle lifecycle.
