New and Faster Standards Driving Data Center’s Paradigm Shift

Date: Apr 10, 2025 | 2:00 PM EDT

Explore the transformative impact of generative AI on data centers in this on-demand webinar. As the semiconductor industry adapts, data centers are shifting from aggregating heterogeneous resources to disaggregating homogeneous ones. This evolution demands efficient interfaces that are fast, power-efficient, and ensure ecosystem interoperability.

Why You Should Watch On-Demand:

  • Learn About Industry Challenges: Understand the current difficulties in achieving optimal performance, power, and area (PPA) with traditional methods.
  • Discover AI Solutions: Get introduced to the latest standards for AI cluster links and network optimization, including PCIe, Ethernet, UCIe, UALink, and Ultra Ethernet, and learn how silicon-proven technologies can accelerate time to market for advanced SoC designs.
  • Gain Insights on Future Trends: Explore how generative AI is shaping new industry protocols and interfaces, and the critical role of optical interconnects in enhancing connectivity and performance.

Featured Speaker

Madhumita Sanyal

Director of Technical Product Management, Synopsys

Madhumita Sanyal is the director of technical product management for the high-speed Ethernet, PCIe, and D2D IP portfolio at Synopsys. She has over 20 years of experience in ASIC design and the application of logic libraries, embedded memories, mixed-signal IPs, and design methodology for SoCs in high-performance computing, automotive, and mobile markets. Madhumita holds a Master of Science degree in Electrical Engineering from San Jose State University and has completed the LEAD program at Stanford University.
