Implementing AI in Chip Design and Effective Onboarding Strategies

This panel explores the transformative impact of artificial intelligence (AI) on chip design, focusing on practical approaches for integrating AI technologies into design workflows. Industry leaders and experts share insights into current trends, real-world case studies, and challenges faced during AI adoption in semiconductor development. The discussion also highlights best practices for onboarding AI, including deployment models, data governance, platform and app installation, talent upskilling to accelerate deployment.

Highlights:

  • Gain actionable strategies for driving successful AI implementation
  • Learn how to foster a culture of innovation within their organizations
