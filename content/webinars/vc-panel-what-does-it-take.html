VC Panel: What Does IT Take to Succeed

Featured Speakers:

  • Brandon Wang​, Strategic Management, Vice President, Synopsys
  • Steve Fu, Celesta Cp​
  • Kevin Chen, Lam Research Capital​
  • Alizera Masrour, Plug 'n Play Ventures

Key Takeaways:

  • Gain the Investor Perspective: Learn what venture capitalists prioritize when evaluating and funding successful silicon startups.
  • Understand What Drives Startup Success: Discover how architecture, execution speed, and capital efficiency influence long‑term competitiveness and growth.
  • Explore Strategies to Reduce Technical Risk: See how effective support and cloud‑based solutions help startups minimize engineering challenges.
  • Accelerate Time to First Silicon: Understand how leading approaches can compress development timelines and reach critical milestones faster.
  • Build Scalable, Fundable Businesses: Gain insights into how startups can position themselves for sustainable growth and continued investment.
