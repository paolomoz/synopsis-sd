Physical AI and the future of Systems Design

Featured Speakers:

  • Drew Henry, Executive Vice President, Physical AI Business Unit, ARM
  • Rev Lebaredian, Vice President, Omniverse and Simulation Technology, NVIDIA
  • Sam Abuelsamid, Vice President of Market Research, Telemetry
  • David Kehr, President, Humanoid Robotics, Schaeffler
  • Ravi Subarmanian, Chief Product Management Officer, Synopsys

​

Why You Should Watch This Session:

Physical AI is redefining how intelligent systems interact with the real world. From autonomous vehicles and humanoid robots to smart manufacturing and digital twins, the convergence of AI, simulation, and advanced engineering is creating a new generation of systems that can perceive, reason, and act autonomously.

Join experts from Synopsys, NVIDIA, Arm, and Schaeffler as they explore how physical AI, simulation-driven design, and co-optimization across hardware, software, and physics-based models are transforming industries and accelerating the future of intelligent systems.
