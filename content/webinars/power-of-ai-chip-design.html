Unleashing the Power of AI in EDA for Chip Design

Synopsys Webinar | Available On Demand

Growing chip complexity, shorter time-to-market windows, and engineering talent shortages are making it difficult for companies and chip designers to keep pace with demand for increasing innovation. These challenges are driving the semiconductor industry to pursue rapid improvements in PPA, semiconductor yield, and productivity across all facets of chip development with AI technologies. We’ll explore how Synopsys.ai is unleashing the power of AI with comprehensive generative AI, design optimization, and data analytics technologies across the entire stack.

Speaker

Speaker

Arvind Narayanan

Executive Director, EDA Group
Synopsys

Arvind Narayanan ​is Executive Director of Product Line Management at Synopsys driving the digital implementation product portfolio and the Synopsys.ai initiative. He has more than 25 years of industry experience in various capacities including digital design, product engineering and product marketing. Prior to Synopsys he has worked at various EDA and Semiconductor companies including Mentor Graphics, Magma Design and Hal Computer Systems. Arvind holds a Bachelors in EEE from University of Madras, Master’s in ECE from Mississippi State University and a Master’s in Business Administration from Duke University.
