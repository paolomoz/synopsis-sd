The System Is the Bottleneck: Rethinking SoC Design from Architecture to Deployment

As chiplet-based SoCs become the foundation for next-generation AI, data center, and high-performance computing systems, architecture decisions around interconnects, memory hierarchies, and system topology have a greater impact on performance than ever before. Baya Systems presents how an integrated architecture exploration and virtual prototyping flow enables teams to analyze, optimize, and validate NoC and chiplet architectures early, reducing risk while accelerating system innovation.


What You Will Learn

  • How to make better chiplet and NoC architecture decisions earlier by evaluating clustering strategies, memory hierarchies, bandwidth, latency, power, and scalability trade-offs before RTL development begins.

  • How Baya Systems and Synopsys Platform Architect provide deep system-level visibility through realistic workload execution, rich NoC telemetry, and analysis of contention hotspots, latency, buffer utilization, cache behavior, and end-to-end system performance.

  • How a continuous virtual prototyping workflow improves development efficiency by reusing models across architecture exploration, microarchitecture analysis, emulation, and software development while preserving performance insights throughout the design lifecycle.
