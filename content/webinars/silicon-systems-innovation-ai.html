Silicon to Systems Innovations for AI

Date: Mar 20, 2025 | 10:00 AM PDT

Featured Speakers:

  • Jitendra Mohan, CEO, Astera Labs
  • Frank Ostojic, SVP & GM, ASIC Products Division, Broadcom
  • Olivia Wu, AI/ML Silicon Design Lead, Meta
  • Marc Hamilton, VP, Solutions Architecture and Engineering, NVIDIA
  • YK Cheng, Senior Director, Design Solution Exploration & Tech Benchmarking Division, TSMC
  • Kevork Kechichian, EVP Solutions Engineering, Arm
  • Shankar Krishnamoorthy, Chief Product Development Officer, Synopsys

Why You Should Watch On-Demand:

Gain executive-level insights into silicon and system engineering for AI hardware from experts at the forefront of these groundbreaking AI innovations. The complexity and challenges associated with advanced node implementation such as power, performance, and area (PPA) optimization, signoff challenges for multi-billion transistor chips and new complexities introduced by advanced packaging options are stretching the limits of existing solutions. The expert panelists will also explore the growing need for accelerated IP roadmaps to meet growing demands for domain-specific architectures and discuss the role of hardware-assisted verification (HAV) in validating large language model (LLM) workloads to improve the reliability and efficiency of AI applications.

Jitendra Mohan

CEO, Astera Labs
Jitendra co-founded Astera Labs in 2017 to address performance bottlenecks in data-centric systems, bringing over two decades of engineering and management experience in the datacenter and server markets. Previously, he served as General Manager at Texas Instruments and held various technical leadership roles at National Semiconductor Corp. Jitendra holds a BSEE from IIT-Bombay, an MSEE from Stanford University, and has over 35 granted patents.

Frank Ostojic

SVP & GM, ASIC Products Division, Broadcom
Frank Ostojic is the Senior Vice President and General Manager of the ASIC Custom Products Division (APD), a position he has held since 2007. He oversees product strategy to drive growth in Machine Learning, Storage, Wireless Infrastructure, Networking, and Computing ASIC markets, and leads the development of IP for complex custom ASICs. With a background as a design engineer since 1996, he has held various senior roles following his education in Electrical Engineering from Brigham Young University and Colorado State University.

Olivia Wu

AI/ML Silicon Design Lead, Meta

Olivia Wu is the ML Design Lead at Meta, overseeing the design of AI inference and training accelerators for the company's data center workloads. Previously, she held senior technical leadership roles at Intel, Nervana Systems, Cisco, Procket Networks, and Sun Microsystems, contributing to high-performance server processors and AI training ASICs. Olivia holds an MSEE from Purdue University and has 16 patents.

Marc Hamilton

VP, Solutions Architecture and Engineering, NVIDIA

Marc Hamilton leads the worldwide solutions architecture and engineering team at NVIDIA, focusing on delivering end-to-end solutions for artificial intelligence, deep learning, professional visualization, and high-performance computing. Before joining NVIDIA in 2013, he worked at HP and Sun Microsystems, and developed HPC applications for the U.S. aerospace and defense industry at TRW. Hamilton holds a B.S. in math and computer science from UCLA and an M.S. in electrical engineering from the University of Southern California.

YK Cheng

Senior Director, Design Solution Exploration & Tech Benchmarking Division, TSMC

Dr. Yi-Kan (YK) Cheng is an R&D Senior Director at TSMC, managing the Design Solution Exploration & Technology Benchmarking Division since 2005. Kevork Kechichian

EVP Solutions Engineering, Arm

Kevork leads a team at Arm that collaborates with its partner ecosystem to develop and accelerate technologies in response to market needs. Kevork holds a BEng from the American University of Beirut and an MS in Electrical Engineering from Concordia University.
