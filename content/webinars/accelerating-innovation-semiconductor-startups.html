Key Takeaways:

  • Harness the Synopsys‑Ansys Advantage: Learn how the combined portfolio equips startups with a comprehensive solution for tackling advanced chip design challenges.
  • Leverage AI‑Driven Innovation: Discover how AI‑powered EDA tools and generative AI capabilities streamline design processes and improve efficiency.
  • Master Multiphysics Simulation: Understand how integrated multiphysics simulation enables more accurate modeling of real‑world conditions.
  • Conquer Chip Complexity: See how these technologies help simplify increasingly complex designs while maintaining high quality.
  • Accelerate Time‑to‑Market: Explore how startups can shorten development cycles and bring products to market faster.
  • Optimize PPA with Lower Risk: Gain insights into achieving optimal power, performance, and area (PPA) while reducing engineering risk and enabling first‑pass silicon success.
