Agentic AI - Synopsys and NVIDIA Transforming Silicon Design and Manufacturing

Featured Speakers:

  • Avinash Kaur, Product Lead, Generative AI Software Co-Design , NVIDIA
  • Ganapathy Parthasarathy, Chief Architect , Synopsys

Synopsys and NVIDIA are collaborating on a next-generation agentic AI platform for semiconductor design and manufacturing, combining GPU acceleration, generative AI, and autonomous agents to enhance engineering workflows. The platform positions AI as an integrated collaborator that optimizes, assists, and coordinates complex tasks across design, verification, and manufacturing environments. The session describes the joint vision and core architectural concepts behind this platform and explains how agentic AI integrates with existing Synopsys tools and NVIDIA technologies focusing on improved decision quality through agent-based workflows, accelerate exploration, and broaden access to advanced methodologies across the semiconductor lifecycle.

Highlights:

  • Synopsys + NVIDIA vision: A next-generation agentic AI platform for semiconductor design and manufacturing that combines GPU acceleration, generative AI, and autonomous agents to enhance end-to-end engineering workflows.
  • AI as an integrated collaborator: Agents optimize, assist, and orchestrate complex tasks across design, verification, and manufacturing—improving decision quality through agent-based workflows.
  • Practical integration and impact: Built to work with existing Synopsys tools and NVIDIA technologies to accelerate design-space exploration and broaden access to advanced methodologies across the semiconductor lifecycle.
