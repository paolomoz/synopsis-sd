Mitigating AI Data Bottlenecks with PCIe 7.0

Learn how PCIe 7.0 is enabling the next-generation of data center architectures, including the throughput, low-latency, and secure data transfers required to support the world’s most data-intensive workloads.

During this discussion Dr. Ian Cutress, Chief Analyst at More than Moore and Host at TechTechPotato, and Priyank Shukla, Principal Product Manager at Synopsys, will share their thoughts regarding the industry drivers, design considerations, and critical advancements in compute interconnects that will unlock the next phase of data center scale needed for tomorrow’s AI demand.

Register now to hear the insights shared during this exciting and timely conversation!
