Featured Speakers

Mike Borza

Principal Security Technologist, Synopsys

Mike Borza specializes in security for integrated circuits and systems with over 25 years of experience in safety-critical systems engineering. A co-founder and former CTO of Elliptic Technologies, Mike has contributed to security standards and holds over 20 U.S. patents, focusing on safeguarding AI systems and electronic design integration.

Dana Neustadter

Senior Director of Product Management, Synopsys

Dana brings over 25 years of experience in security, IP, and semiconductor industries. She focuses on developing strategies and solutions to address security challenges, including safeguarding AI systems against emerging threats.

Reed Hinkel

Director of Strategic Programs, Synopsys

Reed is a 20+ year veteran in semiconductors spanning leading marketing, strategy and market development for silicon and software for Ethernet networking, wireless networking, personal computer, server infrastructure, 5G, consumer audio, video & IoT. He has vast experience in cybersecurity, embedded system security, IoT security, computer security, and digital content protection. Prior to joining Synopsys, Reed was VP Business Development at Intrinsic ID, before which he led the ecosystem development for Arm’s Platform Security Architecture (PSA) and the affiliated PSA Certified silicon, software, and device certification program.
