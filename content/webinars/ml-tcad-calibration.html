ML-Enhanced TCAD Calibration With 10x Reduction in Time to Results

Date: Oct 15, 2025 | 5:00 PM PST

Featured Speakers:

  • Saurabh Suryavanshi, Product Manager, Synopsys
  • Youngkwon Cho, Senior Staff Engineer, Synopsys
  • Dipanjan Basu, Principal Engineer, Synopsys

Calibration is an essential part of enabling TCAD products usages inside Semiconductor fab. Synopsys has been leading the development of ML-enhanced calibration that reduce the time to results by 10x while improve the quality of results. In this Synopsys Webinar, our team will provide overview of TCAD calibration and recent advances in ML-based calibration.

Why You Should Attend:

  • Cut TCAD calibration time and accelerate project delivery by 10x.
  • Overcome accuracy challenges in semiconductor manufacturing.
  • Streamline calibration workflows for better efficiency.
