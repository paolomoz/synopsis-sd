From Power Integrity Signoff to Design Optimization: The Growing Role of Sigma Technologies

Featured Speakers:

  • Jerome Toublanc, Product Management, Principal, Synopsys

As advanced-node and multi-die designs push power delivery networks to their limits, accurately understanding voltage behavior has become essential for both signoff confidence and design optimization. Traditional approaches often struggle to provide sufficient coverage of dynamic voltage drop scenarios while simultaneously delivering actionable insights for debugging and design improvement.

Join this Synopsys webinar to learn how Synopsys Sigma technologies are redefining power integrity analysis by transforming voltage drop from a signoff metric into actionable engineering intelligence. We will explore how comprehensive coverage and voltage decomposition techniques help engineers distinguish local and background voltage effects, identify root causes, and focus optimization efforts where they deliver the greatest impact.

​

Why You Should Attend:

  • Advanced voltage drop analysis to improve confidence in power integrity signoff for advanced-node and multi-die designs
  • Voltage decomposition techniques to separate local and background voltage effects
  • Actionable voltage intelligence to enable more targeted design optimization and faster problem resolution
  • Sigma technologies for emerging workflows across timing signoff, implementation, engineering change orders (ECOs), and multiphysics analysis
