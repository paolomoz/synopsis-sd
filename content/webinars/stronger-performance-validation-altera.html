A Stronger Performance Validation Continuum for Adaptive SoCs with Virtual Prototyping

In today's fast-paced market, delivering complex System-on-Chips (SoCs) like the Altera® Agilex™ family demands more than just robust design; it requires a revolutionary approach to validation. This presentation will reveal how Altera's strategic integration of virtual prototyping, born from a powerful co-engineering partnership between Altera (an Intel Company) and Synopsys, is fundamentally transforming the journey from architectural concept to confident product launch.

By leveraging these detailed models and ensuring tight correlation with emulation, Altera gains unparalleled accuracy and confidence. This collaborative 'shift-left' approach is not only enabling superior quality products, such as the industry-leading Altera Agilex 3 and Agilex 5, to reach the market faster, but also empowering customers with the insights they need to innovate with unprecedented speed.
