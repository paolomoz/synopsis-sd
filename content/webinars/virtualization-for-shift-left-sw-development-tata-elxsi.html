Virtualization for Shift Left Software Development using Synopsys Virtualizer & Silver

As software-defined vehicles (SDVs) drive a shift toward zonal and high-performance computing architectures, automotive teams must develop, integrate, and validate increasingly complex software long before hardware becomes available. Tata Elxsi presents how Synopsys Virtualizer Development Kit (VDK) and Synopsys Silver enable multi-ECU virtualization, early software validation, and a scalable path toward full vehicle digital twins.


What You Will Learn

  • How Tata Elxsi enables earlier SDV software development by creating multi-ECU virtual environments that support software integration, testing, and validation months before physical hardware is available.

  • How VDK and Silver accelerate system-level verification through parallel hardware and software development, ECU interaction testing, cloud-scale validation, and seamless transition to physical targets.

  • How virtualization supports the future of software-defined vehicles by enabling digital twin initiatives, vehicle-level simulation, continuous software delivery, and cloud-native software engineering workflows.
