Navigating the Complexities of SDV with High-Performance Simulation

Date: Mar 27, 2025

Why You Should Attend:

A robust virtual prototyping strategy is crucial for the development and testing of software-defined vehicles (SDVs). We will address the demands of vehicle-level simulation for accelerating software development from both scalability and performance perspectives, featuring a case study on ADAS requirements-based feature testing within an SDV architecture.

Key Topics:

  • How to resolve connectivity issues across multi-abstraction models, from abstract level 1 to refined level 4 virtual hardware models
  • Scaling up vehicle simulations with synchronized, multi-host, multi-OS, and multi-architecture capabilities
  • The increasing prevalence of accelerated next-generation simulations on ARM host machines and their implications for vehicle simulation integration
  • How the exponential growth in test cases that comes with complex software can challenge traditional workflows
  • Significance of container and cloud readiness as essential components for integrating vehicle-level simulation into modern CI/CD soft software development workflows
