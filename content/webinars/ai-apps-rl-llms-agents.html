The Productivity Promise: AI apps from RL to LLMs to Agents

Date: Mar 19, 2025 | 10:00 AM PDT

Featured Speakers:

  • Sameer Halepete, VP of VLSI Engineering, NVIDIA
  • Brian Amick, SVP, AMD
  • Shankar Krishnamoorthy, Chief Product Development Officer, Synopsys
  • Reynold D'Sa, CVP Silicon, Cloud HW, Infra Eng., Microsoft
  • Manoj Selva, VP & GM Product Enablement, Intel
  • Moderator: Junko Yoshida, Business & Technology Journalist

​

Why You Should Attend:

Join industry leaders as they explore the transformative impact of AI applications on modern Very Large-Scale Integration (VLSI) design today and tomorrow. The team of experts will discuss the growing complexities of the VLSI workflow, which have led to significant challenges across all phases of the design cycle, from specification and architecture definition to layout and verification and provide their perspectives on talent availability and need for increased automation and AI. Panelists will explore the benefits of applying AI solutions including Reinforcement Learning (RL), Large Language Models (LLMs) and AI Agents and examine how they can be used to re-engineer current VLSI workflows. The discussion will also touch upon the challenges and opportunities in scaling these technologies, their impact on workforce dynamics, and the future landscape of AI-driven innovation.

Sameer Halepete

VP of VLSI Engineering, NVIDIA

Sameer Halepete is the Vice President of VLSI Engineering at NVIDIA Corporation. He holds a B.Tech. degree from IIT Bombay and an MS from Stanford University. Since joining NVIDIA in 2002, he has led several successful GPU projects and contributed to advancements in graphics processors.

Brian Amick

SVP, AMD

Brian Amick is the Senior Vice President of Technology and Engineering at AMD, overseeing centralized engineering and driving technology delivery to product teams. With extensive experience in CPU core strategy and architecture, he has previously held leadership roles at Marvell and Synopsys, and holds advanced degrees in Electrical Engineering from Stanford University and the University of Texas at Austin.

Shankar Krishnamoorthy

Chief Product Development Officer, Synopsys

Shankar Krishnamoorthy leads the Technology & Product Development Group (TPG) at Synopsys, focusing on innovative, scalable products across various domains, including design, verification, and silicon lifecycle management. Reynold D'Sa

CVP Silicon, Cloud HW, Infra Eng., Microsoft

Reynold D’Sa is the Corporate Vice President of Microsoft’s Silicon, Cloud Hardware, and Infrastructure Engineering team, leading the development of Azure's cloud hardware stack and custom silicon products.
