Infineon’s Success Story: Standardization in a Complex Semiconductor Environment with Synopsys Silicon.da

As semiconductor companies manage increasingly diverse product portfolios, engineering teams face growing challenges in accessing, analyzing, and standardizing manufacturing and test data across the enterprise. In this session, Infineon shares the company's journey to replace a fragmented landscape of custom data analysis tools with a unified analytics platform powered by Synopsys Silicon.da—supporting thousands of products and users worldwide.

What You'll Learn

  • Why Infineon standardized its semiconductor data analysis environment and moved away from multiple custom tools and workflows.
  • How Infineon evaluated and selected a new analytics platform through a rigorous, user-driven assessment process.
  • Best practices for integrating diverse semiconductor data sources while ensuring data quality and user trust.
  • Key results from deploying Silicon.da across 5,000+ products and a global user community.
  • How improved access to manufacturing and test data is enabling broader collaboration and faster decision-making across engineering teams.
