Navigating Security in Automotive SoCs: How to Build Resilient Solutions

Date: Feb 11, 2025

In today's rapidly evolving automotive industry, ensuring vehicle security has become more critical than ever due to increased connectivity, autonomy, and software complexity driven by AI/ML, leading to greater risks and vulnerabilities for OEMs and Tier 1s. Join our webinar on Automotive Security to hear industry experts discuss advancements, challenges, safety and security standards, and best practices to protect modern vehicles from cyber threats. Enhance your expertise and ensure safer driving.

Featured Speakers:

  • Dana Neustadter – Senior Director – Product Management Security IP, Synopsys
  • Ron DiGiuseppe – Automotive Segment Marketing Manager, Synopsys

Learn About:

  • The security trends, drivers, and threats facing modern vehicles
  • The latest advancements and best practices in automotive security
  • Synopsys Security IP offering for Automotive SoC designs to enhance your security measures
  • Protecting the next generation of vehicles and ensure a safer driving experience for all
