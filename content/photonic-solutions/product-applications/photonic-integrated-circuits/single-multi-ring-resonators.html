Single- and Multi-Stage Ring Resonators

Tool Used: OptSim Circuit

A ring resonator is an important component in silicon photonics. It is made up of an optical waveguide in a loop fashion with coupling mechanism as shown in the schematic of Figure 1. The mode-locked laser acts as a wideband source and has an extremely narrow pulse-width.
