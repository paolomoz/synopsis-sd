Overview

Synopsys Europe and Middle East established its presence in Munich and Reading in 1990. Today, the company has more than 30 offices in 17 countries in Europe and the Middle East focused on selling and supporting Synopsys extensive range of solutions to all the major European Electronic companies.

With the recent acquisition of Codenomicon and Coverity Synopsys has increased its focus on Software Integrity and Security bringing to market the industry’s most advanced automated tools.

Our Europe and Middle East employees are an integral part of Synopsys’ global market, technology and community leadership. Several hundred engineers are working on the design and development of key technologies and products that accelerate innovation in the global electronics market. Discover regional highlights, what it means to be a Synopsys employee, current job and internship opportunities, and more!

Our people and values – Integrity, Customer Success through Execution Excellence, Leadership and Passion – make Synopsys Europe and Middle East a great place to accelerate your career! Learn about our Corporate Social Responsibility Programs and current office locations.

Corporate Social Responsibility

Synopsys Europe and Middle East places a high value on volunteering in the communities where our employees work and live. Through our Corporate Social responsibilities program, employees from each of our sites actively take part in various community activities year-round. From painting at local schools, to donating time and resources for underprivileged homes and families, our employees embody the passion and drive to actively participate in the community. Here are a few of the activities our employees have volunteered for in 2017:

In addition to volunteering, Synopsys has also partnered with several organizations that give back to the local communities. Some of these include:

  • A team of volunteers in Poland performed several gardening and landscaping projects to beautify the Hospice for Children and Adults in Gdansk in partnership with Regionalne Centrum.
  • Throughout the year volunteers in Lisbon partnered with Causaus XXI to help mentor students at a local school in coding and robotics. The mentoring lessons culminated in a robotics championship. The goal is to eventually introduce every child and ideally every teacher in their partner school to programming technology.
  • A dedicated group of volunteers in Porto worked throughout the year in partnership with CDI and Apps for Good to help support students in coding through virtual mentoring, and helped serve as judges during three regional events for student programmers.
  • In support of The Disabilities Trust, volunteers in Reading and Frimley performed a beautification and improvement project at The Maples, a center for support and development of people with autism, epilepsy, and other complex needs.
  • Employees in Armenia performed landscaping and preparation work in Ashtarak City to help the Armenia Tree Project create a new park.
