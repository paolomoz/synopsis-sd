Synopsys delivers the most trusted and comprehensive silicon to system design solutions, accelerating technology innovation. We partner closely with customers to maximize their R&D capability and productivity — enabling them to bring products to market faster and without compromise. Companies trust Synopsys to pioneer new technologies and help shape what’s next in our industry.

India is rapidly emerging as a global hub for semiconductor design, advanced engineering, and technology-driven growth. Synopsys plays a key role in driving this momentum - advancing progress through strong engineering expertise and deep ecosystem collaboration across seven locations in India.

We collaborate with government programs, academic institutions, startups, and industry partners to advance design capabilities, grow skilled talent, and accelerate readiness from design to manufacturing. Our work aligns with India’s national priorities — including digital transformation, electronics self-reliance, and building global competitiveness in the semiconductor value chain.

As India sharpens its focus on product innovation, Synopsys supports this vision by enabling critical technology sectors. Our solutions address the needs of a wide range of industries, with special emphasis on emerging markets such as AI, automotive, aerospace, and smart manufacturing. Through our integrated offerings in silicon design, silicon IP, and simulation and analysis, we empower engineering teams to overcome complex design challenges and accelerate product development.

Our commitment is to help India evolve into a worldwide center of product innovation—enabling breakthrough technologies that are conceived locally and succeed globally.

This is a defining moment for India in the semiconductor journey. Our collaboration with Synopsys brings together complementary strengths to co-develop advanced EDA and TCAD solutions—shaping differentiated outcomes for customers.

Rajesh Nair | Senior Vice President & CTO, TATA Semiconductor Manufacturing Pvt Ltd

Key India Initiatives

Enabling Start-ups

Enabling Start-ups

Synopsys partners with India’s vibrant semiconductor startup ecosystem by providing trusted access to industry-leading design tools, engineering collaboration, and technical mentorship—helping accelerate design cycles and speed product readiness. Through the Synopsys Cloud Startup Program, startups also gain flexible, on-demand access to EDA solutions on the cloud—removing infrastructure barriers and enabling faster innovation. We are proud to support national programs like the DLI scheme, launched under the leadership of Hon’ble Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi, to strengthen India’s design ecosystem and nurture the next generation of chip innovators.

Get Started

Manufacturing Enablement

Manufacturing Enablement

Synopsys is empowering India’s path to semiconductor self-reliance by delivering trusted, industry-leading design-to-manufacturing solutions. With 20% of the world’s chip design experts based in India, we are driving innovation through AI-enabled smart manufacturing (Fab.da) for emerging fabs and OSATs. Our advanced technologies—TCAD, Mask Solutions, and Manufacturing Analytics, lay the foundation to enhance manufacturability, accelerate yield ramp, and strengthen India’s semiconductor ecosystem.

Workforce Development

Workforce Development

Building India’s semiconductor talent pipeline through partnerships with leading engineering institutes, internship programs, and national skilling efforts.
We also support government-led initiatives like Chips to Startup (C2S), which aims to train 85,000 semiconductor professionals and cultivate the next generation of chip designers, verification engineers, and system architects.
