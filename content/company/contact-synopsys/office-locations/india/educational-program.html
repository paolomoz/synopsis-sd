Synopsys India University Program

The Synopsys Worldwide University Program provides electronic design automation (EDA) tools and educational resources to universities around the globe.

style
main

Synopsys products are used throughout many top educational institutions in India, reaching thousands of professors and students each year. Using Synopsys products in a learning environment enables students to master today’s technologies through instruction and hands-on experience giving graduates a head start on delivering future advancements in the field. Benefits of membership in the program include access to technical support, training, curriculum and more.
