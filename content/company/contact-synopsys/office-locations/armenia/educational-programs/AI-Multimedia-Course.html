High School AI Multimedia Program “STEP.ai”

Fostering the Next Generation of Innovators

The Science and Technology Education Program in Artificial Intelligence STEP.ai is designed to introduce high school students to the core principles and key components of Artificial Intelligence (AI). The program aims to equip them with foundational knowledge and practical skills in using AI tools and technologies.

Launched in 2021 with the support of Synopsys for Good Foundation, the AI 3-year program consists of video classes with rich multimedia content and equipped with modern distance learning solutions using the educational e-platform of AVC AGBU (Armenian Virtual College of Armenian Global Benevolent Union). The program is developed by Synopsys Armenia Educational Department in cooperation with the Union of Employers of Information and Communication Technologies (UEICT).

The “STEP.ai” program is included as an elective discipline and is available for all high schools.
