Training

SAED offers on-demand training for specialists holding a university degree in:

• Microelectronics

• Electronic Engineering

• Computer Science

• Communication Systems

• Radiophysics

Internships

For universities cooperating with Synopsys, SAED offers special internship programs in:

• Synopsys’ Digital and Custom IC Design Flows

• Digital Standard Cell Library Design

• Analog IC Design

• Digital IC Design

Student Working Groups

Student Working Groups provide SAED students with an opportunity to work on projects, close to industrial projects, in an educational environment. These groups serve as prototypes of actual design teams. Students can strengthen their skills and acquire hands-on experience to gain competitive advantage in hiring.

For more information regarding training and internship opportunities, please contact us.
