Cooperation with Universities

Synopsys Armenia Educational Department (SAED) offers advanced education for Bachelor and Master programs in “IC design” and “EDA” specializations, and PhD programs in “Automated Systems” and “Microelectronics and Nanoelectronics” specializations.

Synopsys collaborates with the following universities to support and offer our educational programs.

Partner Universities

University Cooperation model Programs Eligible students
National Polytechnic University of Armenia (NPUA) “Microelectronic Circuits and Systems” Chair established onsite at SAED Bachelor, Master, and PhD in “IC design” and “EDA” specializations 4th semester graduates in the Bachelor program from the Institute of Information and Communication Technologies and Electronics in “IC design” and “EDA” specializations and from the Faculty of Applied Mathematics and Physics (only from Mathematics major) in “EDA” specialization.
Yerevan State University (YSU) Two classrooms and an engineering laboratory established at YSU Bachelor, Master, and PhD in “IC design” specialization 4th semester graduates in the Bachelor program from the Radiophysics department
National Polytechnic University of Armenia (NPUA) Gyumri Branch Two classrooms and an engineering laboratory are established as part of SAED Gyumri Branch Bachelor and Master in “IC design” specialization 3rd semester in the Bachelor program from Electronics, Informatics and Applied Mathematics and Information Technologies specializations
Russian-Armenian University (RAU) Classroom is established at RAU Bachelor, Master, and PhD in “IC design” specialization 1st semester students in the Bachelor program from the Institute of Mathematics and High Technologies
European University of Armenia (EUA) “Microelectronic Circuits and Systems of Communication Means” Chair established at EUA Bachelor, Master, and PhD in “IC design” specialization 4th semester graduates in the Bachelor program from the Information Technologies department
French University in Armenia (UFAR)A set of courses is established in UFAR Bachelor in “Computer Science” specialty 3rd semester graduates in the Bachelor program from the Computer Science department

Eligible students apply for Synopsys’ educational program through their respective universities. SAED offers preparatory courses for eligible students.
