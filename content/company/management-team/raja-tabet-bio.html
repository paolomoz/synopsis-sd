Raja Tabet

SVP, Strategic Missions & Technologies

style
rail

Raja Tabet is Senior Vice President of the Strategic Missions & Technologies at Synopsys, where he and his team are responsible for:

  • Leading a world-class information security program assuring confidentiality, integrity and availability of Synopsys and partner assets.
  • Leading corporate infrastructure transformation initiatives and executing on leadership IT operations, ensuring foundational platforms are secure, scalable and ready for the AI era.
  • Defining and driving Synopsys’ future enterprise architecture in the era of AI across corporate functions and engineering.
  • Driving enterprise-wide AI governance, to ensure secure, responsible and compliant adoption of AI technologies across the organization.
  • Shaping Synopsys’ technology vision and innovation agenda across AI, Quantum, among other advanced technologies while strengthening external partnerships with customers, partners, and academic institutions to inform strategy and drive ecosystem influence.

Raja has more than 25 years of experience as a business and technical leader in the semiconductor industry, particularly in the networking, data center, and automotive market segments. Prior to Synopsys, he was at Cadence, serving as corporate vice president of Emerging Technologies, working on broad-based solutions for various market segments.

He also served for 10 years as Freescale’s vice president of Software Solutions, creating solution platforms for Freescale’s product lines and launching a successful and rapidly growing and thriving software and services business. Prior to Freescale, Raja was at IBM, and served in a multitude of technical and leadership roles in the company’s Microelectronics Division. He has a master’s degree in Computer Engineering from Syracuse University and a B.S. degree in Computer Science from SUNY Plattsburg.
