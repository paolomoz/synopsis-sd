overview

Synopsys is a gold sponsor of NVIDIA GTC, where the world’s leading engineers, developers, and innovators gather to explore the future of AI and accelerated computing. Experience how Synopsys and NVIDIA bring engineers and scientists the power of accelerated computing and AI to simulate the future before it comes to life.

​

GPU-Accelerated Simulation

Integrating multi-physics solvers with NVIDIA GPUs boosts simulation performance and scalability. Synopsys provides EDA and silicon-to-systems expertise, enabling engineers to perform complex analyses with greater accuracy, throughput, and speed-to-market.

​

Omniverse and Digital Twins
Our tools create highly accurate digital twins, powered by NVIDIA Omniverse. Synopsys EDA and system design solutions connect chip, system, and software development—fostering real-time collaboration, optimization, and earlier design validation.

​

AI and Sustainability
By leveraging AI, machine learning, and advanced EDA, Synopsys and NVIDIA enhance predictive analytics and simulation. Together, we help companies minimize waste, reduce energy consumption, and advance sustainability in aerospace, automotive, semiconductor, and energy sectors.

​

Agentic AI for Chip Design & Verification

Our partnership enables dramatic gains in performance, scalability, and productivity. Engineers can tackle unprecedented complexity—moving faster from concept to silicon and system realization—while leveraging AI-driven automation and high-fidelity simulation.
