Synopsys Virtual Prototyping Day 2025

On-Demand

Join us for our 5th Annual Virtual Prototyping Day to learn how you can Shift-Left your development cycle with virtual prototypes. This popular series invites our customers and partners to speak about how they solve the challenges of highly complex SoC and multi-die designs using virtual prototypes.

This year's event featured 3 specialized tracks designed to cater to the diverse interests and needs of our community:

  • Track 1 - System Architecture Design & Exploration
    • Presenters: Altera, Arteris, Bosch, Meta
  • Track 2 - Software Development & Test
    • Presenters: Microsoft, MIPS, NVIDIA, Synopsys
  • Track 3 - Electronics System Design & Optimization
    • Presenters: Buehler Motor, Grundfos, Keysight Technologies, Raytheon, Schaeffler
