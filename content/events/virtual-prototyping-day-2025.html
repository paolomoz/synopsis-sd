Synopsys Virtual Prototyping Day 2025


An Online Event | Wednesday June 25, 2025

View the presentations from our 5th Annual Virtual Prototyping Day and learn how you can "shift left" your development cycle with virtual prototypes. This popular series invites our customers and partners to speak about how they solve the challenges of highly complex SoC and multi-die designs using virtual prototypes.

This year's event featured 3 specialized tracks designed to cater to the diverse interests and needs of our community:

Track 1 - System Architecture Design & Exploration

Optimize the efficiency and performance of complex systems by enabling early architecture design exploration and optimization. This approach helps in identifying potential issues and opportunities for improvement, ultimately leading to reduced development costs and faster time-to-market.

Altera A Stronger Performance Validation Continuum for Adaptive SoCs with Virtual Prototyping
Arteris Performance Analysis for Network-on-Chip Development Using Synopsys Platform Architect
BoschUsing Verilator for Processor Benchmarking of Automotive SoC Platforms
MetaEnabling Informed Processor Core Selection through Early Architecture Analysis

Track 2 - Software Development & Test

Enhance software development and testing with a virtual environment, where developers can simulate and validate system behavior without the need for physical hardware. This accelerates the development process, improves early detection of issues, and reduces costs associated with hardware dependencies and testing infrastructure.

MicrosoftAccelerating Windows Driver Development Using Synopsys Virtualizer
MIPSDesigning for RISC-V Success: Addressing the Triple Threat in ASSP Development
NvidiaBreaking the Speed Barriers of Virtual Prototypes for Advanced Use Cases
SynopsysExtending Android SoC Virtual Prototypes Beyond VIRTIO with Virtualizer Native Execution

Track 3 - Electronics System Design & Optimization

Improve electronics system design and optimization by enabling comprehensive simulation and analysis of mixed-signal electronics and multi-domain physical systems. This helps engineers to accurately predict system behavior, optimize performance, and ensure reliability, ultimately reducing development time and costs.

Buehler MotorVirtual Validation of an Electric Coolant Pump for ICE and BEV Vehicles
Keysight TechnologiesDeveloping & Verifying a Discrete-DC Converter Controller with Saber
GrundfosFrom Concept to Reality: The Impact of Simulation on Premium Pump Development
RaytheonAC Analysis of DC Power Converters
SchaefflerWide Band Gap (SiC) MOSFET Modeling for Automotive Applications Using Saber and MAST
