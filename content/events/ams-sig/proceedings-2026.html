Synopsys AMS SIG India 2026 brought together industry experts, Synopsys users, and ecosystem partners to explore the evolving role of analog and mixed‑signal technologies in next‑generation electronics. The event highlighted innovations in AMS simulation, mixed‑signal verification, custom design automation, and multi‑physics integration—showcasing how advanced Synopsys and Ansys solutions are addressing growing design complexity across semiconductor applications.
