overview

Synopsys next-generation Verdi® platform extends prior pioneering AI-based debug with an integrated development environment and advanced verification management capabilities. The integrated platform enhances overall productivity with an integrated design environment, verification planning, test execution and aggregation of coverage with connections to most simulation solutions including Synopsys VCS functional simulator, ZeBu emulation system, and HAPS prototyping solution. Furthermore, Verdi streamlines debug across a range of domains including power, mixed-signal, protocols, and hardware-software.
