Get Started

Synopsys ZeBu® Empower delivers breakthrough performance for fast hardware-software power verification. Its performance enables multiple iterations per day with actionable power profiling in the context of the full design and its software workload. The power profiles can be used by software and hardware designers to identify substantial power improvement opportunities for dynamic and leakage power much earlier. Synopsys ZeBu Empower also feeds forward power critical blocks and time windows into Synopsys PrimePower to accelerate RTL power analysis and gate-level power signoff.

Next-Generation SoC Architectures

Silicon technology innovation is propelling the evolution of next-generation SoC architectures towards massively parallel structures executing increasingly complex software workloads. While emulation has long been essential for hardware verification and early software initiation, it alone is no longer sufficient for successful market introduction.

Power Verification Challenge in SoC Projects

High-profile SoC projects have often stumbled due to deficiencies revealed in post-silicon power measurements with real software workloads, which were not detected during the design phase. The challenge of verifying the power profile across entire software workloads and SoC designs was previously insurmountable without emulation technology.

Revolutionizing Power Profiling

Synopsys ZeBu Empower offers a unique solution for power profiling, leveraging breakthrough emulation technology suitable for large vector sizes and complex billion gate designs. It provides actionable information for hardware design teams to detect power bugs and enhance hardware architecture, enabling software teams to optimize systems for minimal power consumption. Its output allows signoff teams to focus on critical blocks and time windows for genuine power reduction benefits.
