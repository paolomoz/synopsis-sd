About VC Portable Stimulus

style
centered

As products become more complex and market windows shrink, we need to understand what improves efficiency in verification and how can these improvements be achieved? Synopsys VC Portable Stimulus (VCPS) enables verification stimulus to be captured, automatically generated, and for the same specification to be reused. This solution enables users to accelerate coverage convergence by achieving coverage 10-100X faster than constrained random stimulus, and provide solutions that enable productive verification at the Block, Subsystem, and SoC levels.
