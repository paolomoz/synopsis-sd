GIF: Large Coverage Testing for Dual Clutch Transmission

German transmission development specialist GIF has integrated TestWeaver into its development and test process. The new process has been successfully used to develop control software for a DCT. Key features:

  • test of TCU control software with TestWeaver
  • integrated into the MATLAB/Simulink development cycle
  • database with 20.000 test cases generated and used for regression tests.

Download Paper

N. Papakonstantinou, S. Klinger, M. Tatar: Test-driven Development of DCT Control Software, 8th International CTI Symposium Innovative Automotive Transmissions, 1 – 2.12.2009, Berlin, Germany.
