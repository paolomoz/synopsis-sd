overview

Product development teams using virtual prototyping require a comprehensive set of transaction-level models (TLMs) that serve as the building blocks of virtual prototypes. Synopsys offers the broadest set of models, covering performance optimized processor models and peripheral. The Virtual Prototyping Models can also be used together with the Architecture Design Models for hardware-software co-design.
