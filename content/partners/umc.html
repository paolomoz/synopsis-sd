overview

United Microelectronics Corporation (UMC) and Synopsys collaborate to provide advanced design methodology and process technology. Founded in 1980, UMC is Taiwan’s first semiconductor company and a leading global semiconductor foundry that provides advanced technology and manufacturing for applications spanning every major sector of the IC industry. Synopsys and UMC's collaboration is committed to timely delivery of leading-edge solutions that serve specific and unique requirements of our mutual customers in today’s advanced applications and SoC designs.

Synopsys' Optimized Design Environment for UMC Process

Synopsys and UMC collaborate to deliver an optimized design environment for UMC process technology. By teaming together, we are delivering a production-proven design solution based on Synopsys design platforms that allow designers to use the latest leading-edge technologies for their most challenging SoCs.

Synopsys IP Solutions for UMC Process

Synopsys provides a comprehensive portfolio of silicon-proven Synopsys IP solutions for UMC process technologies ranging from 65-nm to 14-nm FinFET. The IP solutions consist of logic libraries, embedded memories, embedded test, analog IP and wired and wireless interface IP.
