O-Foundation (2018.06, 2018.09, 2018.12) CentOS support changes


Dear Synopsys Customer:

NOTE: The information here affects only those customers using the CentOS and RHEL Operating Systems (OS). If this does not apply to you, please disregard.

Synopsys is providing updated guidance on support for CentOS and RHEL for 2018 and beyond for its EDA Products. The guidance is as follows:

  • For the QSC-O release (2018.06, 2018.09, and 2018.12), CentOS will be supported as a "binary compatible" operating system.
  • Starting with the QSC-P release (2019.03), CentOS will become the "build" platform, and Red Hat Enterprise Linux (RHEL) will be supported as a "binary compatible" operating system.

Please also refer to the Solvnet article available at https://solvnet.synopsys.com/retrieve/2821174.html

More about Synopsys Compute Platforms:
Information on supported hardware platforms, operating systems, OS versions, and patch bundles for Synopsys products is available at the Synopsys web site at:
https://www.synopsys.com/support/licensing-installation-computeplatforms/compute-platforms/compute-platforms-roadmap.html

  • If you have any questions, please contact the Synopsys Support Center at https://www.synopsys.com/support.html or your local sales support team.
