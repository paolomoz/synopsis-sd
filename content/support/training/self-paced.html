Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Synopsys had Self-Paced Training. What's new?

New and revamped courses with guided Learning Journeys. Content made robust with addition of demos, hands-on labs and quizzes. A new badging and certification program and cloud labs enhance learning experience.

Why are you charging for foundation courses?

Synopsys courses traditionally had a cost associated with it for both Instructor-Led Training and Self-paced Learning. In order to address the need and accessibility of training during the pandemic, limited number of self-paced courses were made available for free for a limited time

What do I get in the subscription?

Both Elite and Apex subscriptions come with:

  • 12-month access to all content in the Product Catalog
  • Video Lessons and Demos
  • Quizzes to measure your progress
  • Hands-on downloadable labs

Apex subscription gives added flexibility and value with:

  • Cloud labs - practice labs without the hassle of setting up the environment
  • Take unlimited certification exams and earn badges with Apex subscription
  • Access to subject matter experts with live Q&A in office hours

My company has Synopsys Education Credits. Can I use them for a subscription?

Absolutely. You can exchange 2 credits for Elite or 3 credits for Apex subscription.
