Course Options

Public Courses

Synopsys offers an extensive curricula of classroom training at our worldwide Learning Centers. Learn from expert instructors with a deep knowledge of Synopsys tools and design methodologies. All trainings include both lectures and hands-on labs that give you an opportunity to practice and develop proficiency with the concepts in the course.

Private Courses

Synopsys offers private training at the time and place most convenient for your staff and project schedule. Our experts can come to your site or use one of our global training facilities. This combines the interactivity of instructor led training with the convenience of online learning. For private training pricing or scheduling assistance, contact us.

Self-Paced Learning

Synopsys offers self-paced learning for those who need a convenient option available anytime and anywhere. The online courses are organized in a way that allows the learner to consume the content at their own pace.

Virtual Classroom

Synopsys offers live public and private courses delivered to you virtually. Public courses are scheduled regularly in various locations. Please check the Virtual Classroom schedule for courses in your timezone.
