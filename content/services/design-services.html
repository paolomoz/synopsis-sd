SoC Design

SoC Design

New system architectures introduce new challenges in hardware/software partitioning and microarchitecture optimization. HPC, AI, and automotive segments are particularly dynamic markets where complex SoC development is getting more complex. Tight correlation between front-end design, implementation and package design are critical and require accurate performance and power modeling to meet product specifications. We offer flexible engagement models including turnkey SoC, subsystem block level and anything and everything between Spec-to-GDSII. We work closely with you to scale quality resourcing specific to your design needs.

Arm-Based Solutions

Arm-Based Solutions

Synopsys offers a range of complete solutions to enhance performance and energy efficiency for Arm-based SoCs, focusing on improved methodologies for optimal power, performance, and area (PPA). Our offerings include fast system verification and ensure IP interoperability, along with robust verification and validation solutions. Specifically designed to work seamlessly with Arm Compute Subsystems (CSS), our silicon-proven IP portfolio, comprehensive SoC prototyping tools, and AI-driven verification solutions collectively support the development of high-performance systems. Synopsys leverages Arm Total Design by integrating its comprehensive suite of design tools and solutions with Arm's architecture and intellectual property. This collaboration enhances the design and development process for Arm-based SoCs.

Methodology and Flow Deployment
