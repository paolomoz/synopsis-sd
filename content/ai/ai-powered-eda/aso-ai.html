Analog Design and Migration

Analog Design and Migration

The Custom Compiler design environment, infused with AI- and ML- based automation technologies, is built to deliver an unparalleled level of productivity to analog engineers and layout designers. Its learning-based implementation solution captures decades of engineering experience and analog design knowhow to ensure design quality and a predictable path to signoff. The AI-driven analog design environment includes extensive features to accelerate analog design migration workflows and a proven set of technologies that save weeks of manual and iterative effort. The combined results for designers are reduced turn-around-time (TAT), reduced cost-of-results (COR) and increased design quality.

Learn More

Multi-Objective Optimization

Multi-Objective Optimization

One of the key technologies of Synopsys ASO.ai is the breakthrough optimization technology with reinforcement learning that can intelligently learn in order to narrow down the design optimization space and converge on multiple objectives faster. Synopsys’ ASO.ai AI-driven design optimizer can take hierarchical design schematics and post-layout parasitics and optimize the design simultaneously across hundreds of PVT corners, spanning multiple tests, in order to meet dozens of analog measurements and objectives. The result is better Time-to-Results (TTR) and improved performance and robustness.

Learn More

Intelligent Analysis

Intelligent Analysis

Synopsys PrimeWave Design Environment executes and manages large simulation jobs efficiently and reliably. It provides integration of PrimeSim simulators to enable a variety of AC, DC, transient, RF, noise and other analyses and executes sophisticated corners, statistical analysis, mixed-signal, electro-optical and multi-die verification jobs. It also supports any third-party simulator. The PrimeWave Design Environment offers a unique AI-driven waveform analysis technology that helps engineers intelligently group and classify waveforms across thousands of simulations and identifies outlier waveform behavior quickly for further analysis. This technology can help engineers not only reduce engineering time, but improve analog design robustness in a way a simple spec-driven design environment cannot provide.
