faq

What is AI-powered EDA Technology?

AI-powered technology uses artificial intelligence to manage complex design tasks, optimize power, performance, and area, and automate repetitive tasks in chip design.

How does this technology benefit engineers?

It frees engineers to focus on chip quality and differentiation by handling complex design tasks and optimizing various aspects of the chip design process.

Can this technology help with chip design migration?

Yes, it enables quick migration of chip designs from one foundry to another or across different process nodes, ensuring flexibility and efficiency.

What role does data analytics play in this technology?

AI-powered data analytics unlocks, connects, and analyzes vast amounts of data collected from design, manufacturing, testing, and in-field operations to enhance productivity throughout the chip development lifecycle.
