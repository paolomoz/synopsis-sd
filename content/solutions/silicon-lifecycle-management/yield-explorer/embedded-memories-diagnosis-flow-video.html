Embedded Memories Diagnosis Flow for Advanced Technology Nodes

STMicroelectronics is designing complex SoCs in advanced technology nodes, containing tens of thousands of embedded memories. Many divisions at STMicroelectronics chose to use the Synopsys Star Memory System (SMS) IP for test, repair and bitmap of their most advanced products. This presentation describes the flow setup by STMicroelectronics R&D and Networking divisions to test and bitmap embedded memories using Synopsys tools SMS Yield Accelerator and Yield Explorer. A comprehensive demo is also shown.
