Designing RF ICs for 5G

5G cellular networks will operate in FR-1 and FR-2 with 5G devices using multi-band multi-mode radio transceivers for a reliable and fast wireless connection anywhere in the world. Today’s RF ICs are complex chips by themselves and hyperconvergence is combining RF, analog, and digital logic into single devices making design challenges even harder. Synopsys offers solutions to help you design RF ICs with a complete front-to-back flow from concept to verified layout ready for manufacturing.
