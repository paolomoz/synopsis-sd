Monitor and Predict Silicon Lifecycle In-Field

Monitor and Predict Silicon Lifecycle In-Field

The Synopsys SLM product family allows OEMs to continuously monitor critical metrics such as silicon voltage, temperature, margins, and health, enhancing the reliability and performance of ECUs, CPUs, GPUs, and architectures. Synopsys SLM products utilize real-time edge and cloud analytics to assess silicon aging and reliability, offering valuable insights into both systemic and random defects within your vehicles and across fleet. It ensures silicon reliability and provides predictive maintenance capabilities, extending silicon lifecycle in field and lowering cost.
