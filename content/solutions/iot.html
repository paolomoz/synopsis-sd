The Internet of Things (IoT) connects over 25 billion devices today, and this number continues to grow rapidly. At Synopsys, we help you meet the evolving demands for processing power and energy efficiency—key factors for IoT growth.

Whether you're developing smart home devices, wearables, or industrial sensors, we assist you in designing the essential chips that power these devices.

Design Smart IoT Devices with Synopsys' Leading Silicon-Proven IP

Synopsys Silicon IP enables you to quickly add more features to low-power SoCs for intelligent IoT applications like smart homes, industrial robotics, connected vehicles, smart cities, and wearables.

Customers worldwide rely on our comprehensive portfolio, including wired and wireless interface IP, data converters, low-power embedded memories, analog IP, energy-efficient processors, and integrated IP subsystems. Whether you need interoperable connectivity, secure data handling, or advanced human interface technologies, Synopsys Silicon IP offers both off-the-shelf and custom solutions to accelerate your time-to-market.
