overview

Design teams need to “shift left” in the overall memory development effort in order to accelerate development and satisfy the market requirements with bespoke chips. Synopsys Custom Design Platform solutions are key enablers of the “shift left” approach providing faster simulation TAT, fast ML-driven design optimization, early parasitic and reliability awareness, and higher productivity and design TAT with layout reuse. In addition, there is an increased emphasis on “digitization” of memory design by applying digital flows and methodologies to solve custom memory design problems.
